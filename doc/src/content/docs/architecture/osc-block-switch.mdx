## Functional contract

Manual maintained A/B selector with IN-A, IN-B, OUT.

## Work before schematic lock

Not a crossfader, sequential switch or clocked switch. Prevent output-output shorts during possible contact overlap; buffer/protect. Clicks are possible.

All corresponding port/control cells are in the canonical grid. Read the generated per-instance entries in `project/osc-hole-field/block-contracts.json`; do not infer pinout or voltages from those visual labels.

## Deliverable

A reviewed one-channel schematic, explicit signal/CV ranges, chosen exact parts and pin maps, corner calculations, simulation/bench plan, power budget and factory test procedure. Replication count follows the current spec, not historical panel versions. Never patch independent blocks internally just to simplify testing; use external test cables or clearly temporary fixtures.
