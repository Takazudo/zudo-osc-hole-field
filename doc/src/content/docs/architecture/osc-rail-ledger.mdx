> **Unvalidated draft.** Neither the instrument's guaranteed maximum current nor either source's measured capacity is established. The allocation is a review proposal, not a second-inlet schematic, cable design or supply approval.

`design/power/rail-ledger.json` is generated by `scripts/schgen/build_rail_ledger.py` from the captured master worksheet, the instrument specification and `design/power/rail-allocation-input.json`. It has 640 fitted IC package records with exact supply pin/net pairs, 347 worksheet load rows and an explicit feed/rail assignment for every row. Multi-unit IC sections use one physical reference and are charged once as whole packages. The generator compares declared IC package counts with the captured circuit and rejects missing, duplicate or wrong-rail load allocations. The master netlist remains the KiCad 10.0.6 export checked by `scripts/schgen/regen-master-reports.sh`; this ledger is an electrical planning audit, not a behavioral test.

## Actual supply pins and missing demand

The package trace uses the exact fitted circuit and source pin maps in `design/standard/pin-maps/`. The S&H `REF5050AIDR` pin 2 connects to **+12 V**, pin 4 to AGND. Its missing supply/reference demand belongs on +12 V. The `SN74HC14DR` supply pins 14/7 and `CD74HC221M96` pins 16/8 connect to **+5 V/AGND**. The previously reported S&H +5 V zero is only a known subtotal. The `NOISE2` pin 1 is on `NOISE_VDD`, a 22 Ω filtered branch of +5 V, and pin 8 is AGND. Its 10 mA planning value is an unsupported design allowance, twice its illustrative 5 mA worksheet value; at 10 mA the 22 Ω feed drops 0.22 V. Neither value is a manufacturer maximum.

The generated package records retain literal pin nets. Their `rails` summary maps local `NOISE_VDD` to its upstream +5 V domain and oscillator `VEE5` to the upstream −12 V domain; those aliases do not claim that the package pin itself sees the upstream voltage.

The 92 magnitude, ten clip and twelve envelope-stage LED terms are already in their module worksheet rows. The 48 B104 DC pots are counted only through their recorded per-module resistor/pot terms; their preliminary 0.125 mA per rail allowance is not added a second time. External output terms use the documented 5 V/10 kΩ planning load, typically 0.5 mA per direction, and do not include shorts or the unmeasured transient supply current of the revised 100 Ω/1 nF precision feedback network. The +12/−12/+5 V inlet bleeders are counted at worst specified voltage/resistance once per inlet; separate TVS/capacitor leakage remains an assumed allowance.

## Provisional complete-module allocation

`rail-allocation-input.json` assigns all 33 modules and the shared `OCTAVE_REF` to feed A or B as indivisible loads. It also enumerates every worksheet row's feed and active rail, and requires one complete inlet per feed. Feed B and its cable/return route are **planning domains only**; no physical connection or source capacity is established. The added inlet contributes another 5.730028/5.679522/2.387511 mA bleeder at the three rail limits. Each inlet gets a separate 0.1 mA per-rail leakage assumption. Each S&H gets a +12 V 2 mA REF5050 assumption and a +5 V 4 mA logic/pull-up assumption. These are finite draft reserves with 2× sensitivity (10× for leakage), not guaranteed bounds.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Feed A | 703.024097 mA | 659.923591 mA | 80.395489 mA | −12 V **19.923591 mA** |
| Feed B | 711.725097 mA | 659.524591 mA | 107.478467 mA | −12 V **19.524591 mA** |
| Both, including two inlets | 1,414.749194 mA | 1,319.448182 mA | 187.873956 mA | −12 V **39.448182 mA** against two 640 mA ceilings |

These ceilings are 80% of unmeasured zudo-pd design targets. The negative-rail excess is therefore a failed *planning* criterion even before qualification. A two-feed split cannot close the current deficit at the inherited ceiling. The source decision must be re-planned with real capacity and load evidence; the allocation does not silently reduce module scope.

**Recommendation for #48 review:** start its source comparison with at least three complete-source domains if the inherited 640 mA −12 V planning ceiling remains unchanged, then compare that with a source revision or a circuit-level load reduction supported by new evidence. This is a starting candidate, not a capacity claim or a three-inlet layout. The alternatives carry different connector, return-path, startup, thermal and mechanical risks; no board or cable allocation should be committed until #48 closes those interfaces.

## Distinct operating cases

The continuous case above simultaneously sums worksheet on-state LED, 10 kΩ output and package allowances at nominal 12/−12/5 V rails and 15–30 °C ambient. That is a declared draft envelope, not a source-guaranteed temperature range. The sensitivity case doubles both S&H reserves, doubles NOISE2's 10 mA allowance to 20 mA and multiplies each inlet leakage reserve by ten. It yields feed A **705.924/660.824/95.295 mA** and feed B **714.625/660.425/112.378 mA** (+12/−12/+5 V). These increases are not stacked into the continuous values.

The conditional startup case assumes each inlet's 1.32 µF tolerance-adjusted local filter charges monotonically in **at least 1 ms**. `C × ΔV/Δt` gives an added 15.84 mA on either 12 V rail and 6.6 mA on +5 V per inlet. Added to the continuous case, feed A is **718.864/675.764/86.995 mA** and feed B **727.565/675.365/114.078 mA**. Downstream 100 nF IC capacitors, any later bulk, nonlinear device startup and rail sequencing are excluded, so this is not a physical inrush maximum.

The bounded cable case is the 5 V into 10 kΩ output state already charged in the worksheets; its increment is **0 mA**. It excludes cable capacitance, a short, external drive and output contention. For a narrower fault illustration, one shorted H1/H2 output has two fitted 499 Ω isolation resistors in series. If its internal driver stays within ±12 V, the resistor-path current is at most `12 V / 998 Ω = 12.024048 mA`. The report adds that amount to either analog rail for one S&H output on each feed, without credit for the existing 0.5 mA load term. Op-amp transient supply demand and thermal/current-limit behavior remain unknown; **complete short-circuit rail current is null**.
