## Panel first, not panel after PCB

One flat front panel defines the user-facing datum. A jack's shoulder and threaded bushing must reach the normal panel hole and be secured by the exact dress nut. No recessed socket requiring a plug-sized hole is accepted. Pot shafts, OCT bodies, toggle bushings and buttons have independent mounting-height contracts.

Split compatible families into interface boards and connect them to a rear core using factory-populated mating parts or factory-terminated harnesses. Connector friction and solder joints are not structural support for patch insertion, knob torque or trigger pressing.

The 3D illustration contains jack, OCT, pot, toggle and trigger domains. R21 adds the two-pot SLEW board so its high-impedance lag circuit stays local. Merge or split boards only after demonstrating the full mechanical and electrical interface. A large number of boards is not a requirement in itself.

## What is not locked

The 24 mm core plane and deeper power pocket are inherited design targets. No assembled shell thickness, standoff length or plate aperture is released. The preview's jack-nut envelope, caps/plungers, lever projection and generic IC blocks are not approved manufacturing models.

The records in `project/osc-hole-field/board-contracts.json` list the missing contracts. Resolve exact shoulder/bushing/shaft datums, tolerances, component solder tails, 3D collision checks, insertion/removal sequence, support screws, tools and harness bending before routing.

## Sensitive nodes

Never run the LF398 hold capacitor node, oscillator timing capacitor, envelope integrator node or SLEW storage node through an arbitrary connector. Place their active circuits nearby; cross board boundaries with buffered audio/CV, logic and suitably decoupled supply/returns.
