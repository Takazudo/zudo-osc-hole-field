The preferred candidate has separate front jack and control PCBs, a middle interconnect/power/reference zone, and removable rear functional cards. A limited-change core-only-card alternative retains existing front boards. Neither is adopted. All five oscillators, 33 functions and 438 locked panel centres remain fixed. Canonical copper and KEEP_K1402_INCUMBENT remain unchanged; every schematic and PCB is an unvalidated draft.

The reviewable packet lives in `design/architecture-278/` and its reproducible extraction/checking tools in `scripts/architecture278/`. The packet includes whole-instrument source/reuse and requirement ledgers, every O1/shared-reference Ref-pin and package unit, both ends of the 11 O1 harnesses, raw control wipers, existing rear supply terminals, bounded mechanical/electrical studies and adjacent O1/O2 assembly comparison. Source extraction and arithmetic do not qualify hardware.

Revision 2 recommends a mixed connection candidate: short front links, a supported direct rear signal mate, and separate bolted power/return straps. Populated 103 × 115 mm O1/O2 tiles carry all 154 rear-core packages and six rear-facing trims each. Proposed middle/core/cover depths are 50/69.2/105 mm. Regrouping front links by control gives 54 conductors and 108 crimps rather than the literal two-cable comparison's 204 crimps, but adds connector soldering and support hardware; no cost or time savings are established. Courtyard capacity, scalar calculations and source tests are not PCB routing or physical qualification.

The current `revision-mechanics.md` and `revision-connectors.md` distinguish sourced dimensions, proposed bounds, complete scoped connection counts and remaining work. Independent reviewers check the changed models and rejection tests. The gate recommendation and specific acceptance limits are in `design/architecture-278/GATE.md`; `WORK.md` records inputs, actual checks and reviews. `NEXT-PROMPT.md` is a separately approvable continuation, not an execution instruction. Start from the packet's explicit source revision, never from whichever parked worktree happens to exist.

Existing #57 source, #59 protection, #55/#65 mechanical and #247 electrical completeness obligations remain open. Missing whole-load, fault, installed support and service evidence prevents physical acceptance. Five-cent tracking at 25 °C and 2 mV adjacent octave steps are targets; 0.015–20,000 Hz is characterization only. A physical unit and independently assembled second unit are required before replication, followed by all-five and full-instrument qualification.

The old #242/#253 execution stack and E16 campaigns remain parked. PR #260/#277 stay draft and unmerged. No production CAD, decision.json, fabrication/order files, native campaign, deployment or hardware action is authorized by this design packet.
