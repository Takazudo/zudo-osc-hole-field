`zudo-osc-hole-field` is a standalone, cable-patched analogue synthesizer built around visible, independent signal blocks. The R21 handoff fixes its user-facing grid and scope; it is pre-schematic and does not claim a fabrication-ready or bench-verified instrument.

## Purpose and experience

A user patches signals between the instrument's labelled 3.5 mm jacks and adjusts the controls on the corresponding modules. Every connection is made with a cable: there are no hidden inter-module normals, including a default noise feed to either sample-and-hold input. The intended experience is to make the signal path legible through separate blocks rather than concealed routing.

The handoff does not define a target user, use environment, prototype quantity, budget, or acceptance criteria for a successful first prototype. Those remain owner questions. `Scope10` is a separate project and is outside this instrument's scope.

The intended session is to supply power, patch a source into an independent block, adjust that block's controls, and observe its output before removing the patch leads and powering down. Manual patching between visible blocks is essential; performance goals such as precision tracking and through-zero FM remain later experiments rather than first-prototype claims.

## Instrument scope

| Area | R21 scope |
|---|---|
| Panel | One flat panel, 318 × 298 mm default; all jacks are above all controls |
| Patch points | 180 jacks on an 18 × 10 grid |
| Controls | 144 occupied cells on an 18 × 8 grid: 101 continuous pots, 5 six-position octave selectors, 19 two-position toggles, 11 three-position toggles, and 8 buttons |
| Indicators | 114 LEDs: 92 magnitude, 12 envelope-stage, and 10 clip indicators |
| Independent modules | 33 instances across 11 families, listed below |
| Power intent | Owner-delegated #48 proposal: one external regulated-source requirement contract; physical source/inlet NOT SELECTED. Original pinned P+B comparison and physical qualification remain open |
| Assembly | Electrical soldering and required programming are completed externally before delivery, and connectors arrive populated; the handoff does not establish an accepted factory scope |

| Module family | Instances |
|---|---:|
| Oscillator | 5 |
| Filter with parallel VCA output | 3 |
| Five-input mixer | 2 |
| Four-input mixer with VCA | 2 |
| Attack-release envelope | 6 |
| Wavefolder | 2 |
| Attenuator/offset | 6 |
| Buffered one-to-three multiple | 2 |
| Sample-and-hold with output slew | 2 |
| Manual A/B selector | 2 |
| Four-output noise source | 1 |

The two SLEW controls occupy R21 cells `[7,6]` and `[7,7]` (zero-based). The proposed path is a symmetric post-hold exponential lag; it adds no CV, bypass, or raw-output jack.

## Fixed constraints and open parameters

- The R21 hardware positions are fixed. The generated `design/grid/placements.lock.json` is the coordinate authority used by the design tooling; the workbench grid is its source. A fit problem must be reported with dimensions rather than solved by moving a control or jack.
- The panel intent is black solder mask, exposed gold accents, and white silk, with black IN dress nuts and red OUT dress nuts. Styling remains owner-controlled.
- Use panel-fastened jack bushings, and design the panel, exact component footprints, and board stack together before routing.
- Preserve the R21 layout relationships: FOLD 2 stays left of FOLD 1, six OFFSET sections stay below the ENV columns, and both multiples remain one-input/three-output blocks.
- Module inputs and outputs remain independent unless an explicit cable connects them. The S&H inputs have no default noise connection.
- No qualified panel thickness, mounting datum, connector seating, or component height has been established.
- The environment, operating temperature, quantity, budget, and prototype acceptance criteria are not specified in the R21 source.

## Explicit claim boundary

The current specification does not claim a fabrication-ready schematic, PCB, BOM/CPL, native ERC/DRC result, qualified panel and board stack, jack thread or connector fit, or button-plunger reach. It also does not claim ADDAC215-like precision, a 25-second slew, 20-minute sample hold, through-zero FM, clickless A/B switching, calibrated volts-per-octave tracking, a validated power or thermal budget, or surge protection. Those statements require circuit evidence, exact mechanical data, and bench results at the relevant revision.
