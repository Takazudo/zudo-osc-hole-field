## Engineering sequence

1. Settle the jack/nut/panel and control-board Z contracts on a factory-built mechanical coupon. Include OCT, one Bourns pot, toggles, trigger pressing surface, LEDs and connector mates.
2. Capture/test one S&H+slew/trigger channel and one precision MULT/selector section on a factory-assembled utility coupon. Characterize hold drift, capture window, slew tail and hot error.
3. Finish one complete oscillator with its reference, octave switch, sine shaper, FM/sync and calibration. Validate before replicating five times.
4. Capture the filter/VCA, mixers, envelope, folder, AO, noise and indicator circuits. Quantify all rail loads and define protections.
5. Finish symbols, pin maps, footprints and measured stack. Then place/route the actual PCBs and run native ERC/DRC plus mechanical checks.
6. Obtain an accepted factory scope and reviewed release package. No Gerber, BOM or CPL is authorized by this handoff.

Record every result with its actual scope. Unavailable tools are NOT RUN, not PASS. The browser engine is not firmware and is not an analogue simulation.
