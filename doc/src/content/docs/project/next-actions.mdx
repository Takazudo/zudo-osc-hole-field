This project is in the R21 design handoff phase. The epic turns the imported layout and module contracts into an evidence-backed project record, generated schematic drafts, board drafts, and honest status pages. Those artifacts organize engineering work; they do not establish circuit performance or manufacturing readiness.

## Work this epic performs

1. Resolve the instrument-wide electrical standard, exact panel-hardware evidence, and the real supply interface before downstream circuits depend on them.
2. Capture module circuits from named topologies, record consequential choices, and generate the master schematic from the circuit specification.
3. Integrate the module sheets, compare the generated design against its contracts, and reconcile the rail budget and board partition from actual circuit and mechanical evidence.
4. Generate per-board schematic and PCB drafts, route and check them with the pinned KiCad 10 oracle, and publish status pages that distinguish completed checks from open work.
5. Keep generated outputs reproducible from their source specifications. A clean ERC or DRC result describes rule checking only; it does not establish that a circuit works or a board fits.

## Gates that remain open after the design epic

| Gate | Evidence still needed | Current boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanical fit | Factory-built coupon or equivalent measured stack for the jack shoulder and nuts, pots and shafts, octave selector, toggle levers, button/plunger reach, LED optical path, connectors, panel, and board planes | Preview geometry and catalogue dimensions are not an installed fit result |
| Sample-and-hold and slew | Coupon measurements for acquisition, hold drift, capture window, lag response, and tail under documented conditions | The SLEW proposal and ideal model do not establish a 25-second response or long-term hold performance |
| Oscillator | Build and characterize one complete oscillator, including reference, octave selection, shaping, FM/sync, and calibration, before any replication claim | The five oscillator instances are functional scope, not a validated circuit |
| Electrical integration | Measured or source-supported loads, rail budget, protection, startup behavior, and cross-board return paths | Reusing zudo-pd is design intent; the exact installed supply state and instrument load are not qualified |
| Manufacturing | Accepted external assembly scope, exact released design revision, and owner review of the eventual release package | No fabrication order or manufacturing release is authorized by this handoff |

The [release gates](../verification/osc-release-gates.mdx) and [S&H test plan](../verification/osc-slew-test-plan.mdx) hold the specific criteria. Record each result against its board revision, population, setup, and observed conditions. If a tool or physical test cannot run, report it as `NOT RUN` with the reason.

The [precision-output model sweep](../verification/osc-precision-output-model.mdx) now passes its twelve modeled cases for one fixed compensation network. Before layout or rail-budget sign-off, select exact 100 Ω and 1 nF passive identities, measure a populated output coupon over the same cable/load matrix, and capture transient supply current. Physical cable, tolerance, temperature and thermal checks remain `NOT RUN`.

## Immediate continuation

Continue with the open electrical-standard and panel-hardware evidence work, then capture and integrate circuits against the R21 contracts. Keep component candidates out of the inventory until exact identity and selection are resolved. Use [the local R21 sequence](./osc-next-actions.mdx) for the detailed mechanical-coupon, circuit-capture, and verification order.
