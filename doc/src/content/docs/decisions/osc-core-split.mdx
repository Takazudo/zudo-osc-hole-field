## Power, feeds and adjustment access

All 13 POWER parts, the 18 original K load lands and 76 × 70 mm protection
reservation remain provisionally on A. Each satellite needs six additional
factory-soldered wires, twelve lands, two 91 × 6 mm terminal rows and two
91 × 28 × 28 mm F-side service corridors. Existing GH connectors retain their
identities and complete pin order; they are not qualified full-core feeds.
New bulk and monitor identities, positions and assembly origins remain unresolved.

A 26 mm plane pitch gives 24.4 mm bare board gap. A 28 mm normal service corridor
therefore has a **conditional 3.6 mm deficit** wherever it projects through the
neighboring board. Origins and rotations are not yet assigned, so this is not
proof that every split is impossible. A dimensioned edge escape or reviewed
opening could resolve it. Moving the entire stack deeper does not increase that
local gap. Actual wire paths must include bends, slack, strain relief and service
travel within the 110 mm branch cut ceiling; straight-line lower bounds are
insufficient.

The 1 mΩ rail, 0.5 mΩ common-AGND and 20 mV allowances belong to the entire joined
network, including hub, branches, necks, vias and contacts. They cannot restart
at each core. Unequal demand, absent return conductors and partial-power behavior
remain design obligations. #57/#59/#65 remain open.

All 100 trimmers, including 56 on B.Cu, need supported adjustment without owner
desoldering or loading solder joints. #248's COMPONENT_REVIEW_READY covers 23
MPN-gap reviews; it does not adopt replacements or prove calibration access.
Bourns TC33X-2-102E and TC33X-2-103E source identities remain unchanged.
The reviewed TC33X family envelope is a candidate bound, not verified tool-axis,
seating, mirrored installation, hand clearance or calibration range. Label
reservations may require leaders displaced up to 111.832 mm in two or 82.354 mm
in three; zero modeled label overlap does not establish installed readability.
#249 native annotation checks and physical readability remain open.

## Smallest next work

The new source-to-settled target join finds a concrete pilot blocker: the current
sensitive-signal samples for **two-A, two-B, three-A and three-B have no recorded
open demand**, in both their candidate and accepted K dumps. They cannot count as
routing exercises. Three-C's signal pads occupy two open groups in both. Two-B's

group; closing that candidate pair would restore a partition-induced obligation,
not demonstrate an improvement over K. Supply samples are single pads on open
nets and still need reviewed remote landing groups. These findings are recorded
per exact Ref/pad/UUID in the replay; no source manifest or copper was altered.

   joined return budget, and check access to all 100 trim axes with opposing

3. **Prepare matched native cases:** replace the four unexercisable signal samples
   with reviewed real open demands in their owned source modules, and join
   #252's nine authenticated cases. Select real open
   demands with legal landings and common obstacle/search windows. The #252
   12 × 12 mm windows and separate AGND fanout window can exclude targets; such
   failures would not prove one-core infeasibility. No native admission is active.
4. **Run only after exact admission:** require at least one accepted difficult
   supply closure and one coupled signal closure per proposed board, with its
   return proved or preserved; retain both supply polarities in the comparison.
   Already-connected cases are NOT EXERCISED, never successes. Record identical
   predeclared search budgets, setup/refill/search/audit times, copper delta and
   memory. Require full warnings, original-group/copper preservation, zero
   error/parity and fresh-copy agreement before counting useful progress.

A split advantage requires a source-identical difficult demand that it resolves
within the common budget while K does not, and a geometric explanation. Three
additionally needs a gain unavailable to two that justifies its extra board and
service burden. No broad production router harness is needed to answer that
question. Full physical qualification follows a selected source design; no order
or energization is authorized here.

The stale panel receipt is a separate failed qualification: panel bytes match,
but `scripts/kicad/run.sh` changed. Candidate settles do not refresh it. Aggregate
canonical regeneration is NOT RUN because it enters native work without a new
parent admission. The accepted design is preserved in the meantime.
