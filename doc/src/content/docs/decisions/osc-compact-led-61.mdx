# Compact indicator LED draft

Issue [#61](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-osc-hole-field/issues/61) replaces the active KENTO KT-0603W (LCSC C2290) and KT-0603R (C2286) indicator candidates. Their distributor-only identities and family CAD receipts remain in Git history and the retained receipts, but their 0603 courtyards conflicted with fixed jack and paired LED centres. No manufacturer-primary exact-package drawing was retained for those candidates. The original white 0402 candidate, Kingbright APG1005VWF-A1-5MAV (DSAQ0073 V.2B, 27 April 2022), had a 5 mA DC absolute maximum and was rejected for this draft because the stage driver had no guaranteed tolerance/fault bound below that limit. Its inspected primary PDF remains under `circuit/source-archive/kingbright/` (SHA-256 `f9c8a064df7c312382aafcc4e0b639d72b14a52985b7cfb7e1b5a5a4a8bd78a5`).

The conditional active parts are **Kingbright APHHS1005QWF/D white**, exact PDF DSAK0125 / 1203009228 V.10B (13 October 2025), and **Kingbright APG1005SEC/E-T hyper red**, exact PDF DSAM9600 / 1203013767 V.10A (12 March 2025). The white has a 1.0 × 0.5 × 0.5 mm body and 30 mA DC absolute maximum at 25 °C. The red has a 1.0 × 0.5 × 0.2 mm body and 25 mA DC absolute maximum at 25 °C. Both page 1 drawings show top emission, pin 1 cathode / pin 2 anode, and a recommended pair of 0.7 × 0.5 mm copper pads with a 0.2 mm gap. The source-backed package cuboid WRLs show dimensions only; lens, seating, brightness and optical coupling are unqualified. No supplier order code, stock status, or assembly process has been established for either exact MPN.

The generated footprint has a 2.10 × 1.25 mm courtyard, including project clearance around source pads and a small silkscreen polarity mark. The machine-readable `design/reports/compact-led-fit.json` checks every LED's two pads and courtyard against actual jack and pot footprint geometry at all 114 fixed centres. All 102 jack indicators clear neighboring jack/pot courtyards and through-hole copper in this 2D same-face abstraction. The minimum jack-to-LED courtyard gap is **0.35 mm**; all ten magnitude/clip pairs have at least **0.80 mm courtyard** and **1.55 mm copper** gap. The 12 stage LEDs overlap their parent pot copper if put on the same face. They require the separate optical plane from #35, which this issue does not design or qualify. Neither solder mask tolerance nor assembled jack/pot overhang is proved by this 2D test.

A **1.2 kΩ nominal series resistor** was added to each white stage path. Under the explicit planning assumptions +5 V ≤ 5.5 V and resistor ≥ 99% of nominal, a zero-voltage LED/transistor lower bound yields **4.630 mA** and **25.46 mW** in that resistor. This is below the white 30 mA absolute maximum at 25 °C and the roughly 5 mA limit read conservatively from its page 3 derating graph at 85 °C. The 92 magnitude paths use a raised 5.6 kΩ nominal sense resistor and yield at most 4.762 mA from an assumed 26.4 V rail span and ≥99% resistor value, below that same approximate 85 °C graph value. Nominal ±5 V magnitude current falls from roughly 1.00 to 0.893 mA, so visible brightness needs a fresh bench check. The red 22.1 kΩ clip path gives 1.207 mA under the same rail span and 1% resistor assumption, below its 25 °C absolute maximum. These are **conditional calculations**, not demonstrated electrical bounds: regulated rail extremes, cold/hot fault behavior, and installed LED ambient remain unresolved. At nominal 0.5 mA stage current, the new resistor drops 0.6 V, reducing high-forward-voltage headroom. The manufacturer's minimum white luminous intensity applies at 20 mA, so it gives no minimum at the draft sub-milliampere level. The existing rail ledger retains its planning LED allowances because the resistor is in series and does not add an independent load; actual current and visibility need bench measurement.

Yageo's retained [RC0603FR-071K2L manufacturer spec sheet](https://www.yageogroup.com/component-documentation/download/specsheet/RC0603FR-071K2L), generated 28 September 2026, SHA-256 `20324e9b3c842d6386823c689ea5aad1376ac3413e6fe26172e0772f8e4e2716`, confirms an exact 1.2 kΩ, ±1%, 0603, 0.1 W at 70 °C candidate. It does **not** provide a power-derating curve through the proposed 85 °C limit. The stage resistor remains unassigned to an exact schematic MPN until its hot dissipation is bounded from primary evidence and assembly sourcing is reviewed. The 25.46 mW worst-case assumption is below its 70 °C rating, but no 85 °C power claim follows from that alone.

**NOT RUN:** light output at actual current, panel-window coupling, light bleed, assembled jack/pot/LED fit, installed temperature, rail tolerance and startup/fault capture. Every schematic and PCB remains an **unvalidated draft**. All 33 module instances, all 438 UID/x/y centres, and the existing package/island electrical boundaries are retained.

## White land clearance revision for issue 38

The exact white-part drawing labels its recommended dimensions with a ±0.1 mm tolerance. The project now selects 0.65 × 0.5 mm pads at x = ±0.475 mm, producing a 0.30 mm gap while retaining the outer copper edges at ±0.8 mm, all hardware centres, polarity, layers and owner styling. This preserves the 0.25 mm project clearance target. The original 0.7 × 0.5 / 0.2 mm drawing transcription and CAD receipt remain retained as historical source; the nominal manufacturer fact is unchanged. The former unsupported 80% mask-opening clause has been removed from the white-part evidence.

This is a project land choice within the published drawing ranges, not a manufacturing guarantee. Actual etch/registration, solder wetting, alignment and reflow remain unqualified under issues 64/65. It affects 104 fitted white indicators: 41 on JL, 51 on JR and 12 on EL. The red footprint is unchanged. Historical native/model receipts remain historical; affected current boards require regenerated source copies and fresh native checks.
