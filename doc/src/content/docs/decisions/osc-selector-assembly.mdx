## Decision and evidence

Use five independent local PCB adapters, grouped **3 front + 2 rear**. O1/O3/O5 have body rotation 0° and PCB component faces at z = −16 mm. O2/O4 have rotation 180° and faces at z = −29 mm. Z is positive toward the user from the front panel surface. The 13 mm step separates even the deliberately conservative whole body/support/tail envelopes. O2/O4 receive custom 13 mm keyed shaft extensions. All five receive custom Ø8 mm knobs with adjustable pointer collars. These are **project-designed draft parts**, not sourced commercial parts or qualified replacements. The previous Ø8 mm cap was an unselected study envelope.

The source specification is `design/mechanical/selector-assembly.json`. The placement generator consumes its orientation and depth per UID; the original R21 grid and owner artwork are unchanged. Footprint and keepout model generation lives in `scripts/libgen/gen_selector_assembly.py`, and the CAD receipt records derivation and hashes. The component WRL uses [KiCad’s 0.1 inch coordinate units](https://dev-docs.kicad.org/en/file-formats/legacy-pcb/index.html) with drawing Y inverted; the independent checker converts back to mm and verifies its bounds. The independent numerical/oracle report is `design/mechanical/selector-assembly-check.json`.

## Corrections and rejected alternatives

Drawing No.7 specifies **Ø2 PCB mounting holes**, not Ø2 solid legs. The old assertion that two solid legs necessarily collide because their holes overlap was unsupported and has been removed from active evidence. Same-orientation hole centres are 1 mm apart at 17 mm shaft pitch, so the **bores** overlap 1 mm.

Alternating 0°/180° at one height changes the closest mounting-hole separation to `sqrt(1² + 2.2²) = 2.416609 mm`. This gives a nominal **0.416609 mm bore web**, but unchanged Ø2.6 copper pads overlap **0.183391 mm**. A hole-only pass does not establish assembly clearance.

The 16.2 × 18.5 mm central body is asymmetric: local y = −10.5 to +8 mm about the shaft in direction A. Another view gives an **18.2 mm support span**. Its complete contour and tolerances are not dimensioned. The footprint now depicts both the asymmetric central body and the larger conservative support envelope; the translucent WRL represents keepout bounds, not an exact solid. Treat the 18.2 × 18.5 × 11.5 mm body/support/mounting-tail bound, including 4 mm rear projections and 7.5 mm body depth, as occupied for separation analysis. Also include the narrower electrical-terminal tail bound x=±5.45, y=±9.6, z=−4…0: the lower terminal row extends beyond the body’s +8 mm edge. These pin bounds reserve the source bore diameter as clearance, without claiming that diameter for solid pins. Do not infer narrow reliefs by scaling a raster drawing.

## Panel, load path and indexing

Use distinct apertures for the two actual interfaces: **carrier Ø9.5 ±0.1 mm for the M9 bushing**, and **front panel Ø7.0 ±0.1 mm for only the nominal Ø6 shaft/extension**. The old Ø6.3 front hole cannot be called M9 clearance. In this stepped design the M9 bushing never passes through the front panel. No manufacturer-recommended panel hole is retained; these are explicit project choices based on the sourced M9 and Ø6 interfaces.

The carrier's 9.4 mm minimum bore gives 0.4 mm nominal diametral M9 clearance. For the front interface, allocate a 6.9 mm minimum bore, 6.1 mm maximum actual shaft/extension, 0.1 mm relative axis error and 0.2 mm radial wobble: `(6.9−6.1)/2−0.1−0.2 = 0.1 mm` residual radial clearance. The shaft source specifies **nominal Ø6 only**, so the 6.1 mm upper limit and wobble are acceptance allocations requiring measurement in #55, not manufacturer tolerances or proven performance. Front panel thickness 2 mm is also a coupon assumption.

The first **Ø9.5 front** attempt created **40 hole-to-copper errors and 5 OCT legend clipping warnings** against the untouched owner art. **Ø9.2 front** removed the copper errors but still clipped all five OCT legends. Both are rejected; the original panel had zero native DRC violations. The proposed Ø12 knobs also covered the fixed labels centred 5.12 mm below each shaft, so select custom **Ø8** knobs instead. The final Ø7 front / Ø9.5 carrier split and Ø8 knobs preserve owner artwork. No deterministic conflict was relabelled as a passing check.

Each insulating carrier plate is 15 × 15 × 2 mm, against the switch body front at PCB z+7.5, with a Ø9.5 hole. An open 4 mm relief toward the projecting feature clears its nominal 3 mm width without assigning an undocumented feature centre or tight key fit. Rotate the relief with the body. The washer sits at PCB z+9.5…10 and nut at +10…12; the nominal threaded bushing ends at +14.5. The nominal remaining thread beyond the nut is 2.5 mm. Torque and actual engagement remain unqualified.

The carrier and captured rear adapter resist torque and axial load through enclosure supports. The two rails reserve panel y=170…172; local adapter tongues extend to y=171. Local boards are 19.8 × 21 × 1.6 mm, with a 6 mm capture tongue. All original mounting legs and plated pads remain. Carrier material, rail anchorage, tongue capture, clamp fasteners and harness strain relief need detailed structural qualification; solder joints cannot be the sole panel-load path.

O2/O4 extensions reserve a Ø10 socket at PCB z+16…22.5 and a Ø6 stem up to +35.5. The draft socket Ø6.1/4.6 flat gives 0.1 mm nominal fitting allowance. With L=15±0.5 and C1, the allocated minimum usable engagement is **5 mm**. Retention, material, concentricity and backlash have not been tested. All five Ø8 knobs occupy panel z=1…10, with their pointer collars separately adjustable; qualify retention and shaft engagement on the coupon.

Do not renumber pins after rotation. Terminals **1/10 are common**, **2–7 are the six positions**, **8/9 are dummy**; MP1/MP2 are mounting features outside the electrical circuit. Clockwise terminal order viewed from A is 2→3→4→5→6→7, mapped to octaves −2, −1, 0, +1, +2, +3. Body rotation preserves chirality; O2/O4 pointer collars receive a 180° index correction. The code checks this transformation, while actual continuity and angle/end-stop indexing remain **NOT RUN**.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Whole support/body/tail depth | 11.5 mm; plane separation 13 mm | Drawing dimensions, full conservative envelope; no general tolerance given |
| Inter-selector Z gap | 1.5 mm nominal; 0.75 mm allocated minimum after 0.5 mm envelope growth and 0.25 mm relative placement error | Allocation is a project acceptance target; actual component spread unknown |

| Adjacent knobs / washers / reserved Ø15 tools | 9 / 3 / 2 mm | Tool access assumes knobs installed after nuts |
| Rotated body to adjacent toggle body in Y | 0.96 mm | Existing 5.08 mm toggle body drawing; actual travel/installed envelope unqualified |
| Closest selector feature to existing neighboring courtyard in XY | 0.25 mm | Conservative check including adapter board; not a worst-case manufacturing gap or strength result |
| Selector to F1 pot courtyard | Positive; checked from actual generated courtyard | Pot seating, shaft/cap and board stack remain open |
| Mounting pads | Ø2.6 copper, Ø2 hole, 0.3 mm annular ring | Retained project pad choice; plating unspecified by manufacturer |
| Terminal pads | Ø1.55 copper, Ø0.9 hole, 0.325 mm annular ring | Retained project pad choice |

No overlap of nominal conservative inter-selector volumes is known for the selected candidate. This statement does not prove a fit at unknown tolerance extremes, neighboring lever motion, carrier strength, enclosure/core clearance or harness/tool access.

## Reproducible checks and downstream work

`bash scripts/libgen/fixtures/check_selector_assembly.sh` uses the pinned KiCad **10.0.6** oracle. It builds five-up fixtures as front (3) and rear (2) adapter islands at the actual fixed x/y and two rejected coplanar reference fixtures. Real pad centres, drills, diameters, classification and rotations are measured from pcbnew. The selected front and rear fixtures each have **0 DRC violations, 0 unconnected items**; they contain no functional routing. The regenerated panel also passes native DRC with **0 violations**, matching the original panel baseline after the rejected enlarged-aperture attempts were corrected. No warnings are suppressed. The checker independently verifies the 438 UID/x/y hash, 33 modules, pin geometry, six-position indexing arithmetic, inter-selector keepout intersections and nearby existing courtyards. The PCB and native DRC files stay in the ignored `.circuit-cache/selector-fixture/`; the deterministic report is retained in the repository.

Issue **#35** must consume the five local adapters/two planes as the O board group, preserve all five oscillator instances, and add the inter-adapter harness and carrier volume to partition/resource checks. Do not collapse them back into one coplanar O strip. The rear envelopes occupy z=−33…−21.5 and cross the provisional core plane z=−24; carve their fixed-XY keepouts from that board allocation. Issue **#37** must carry these same fixed-centre/rotation/depth constraints into board drafts, retain all pads/legs, and check each physical board plus the complete assembly. Neither task may treat this fixture as a routed instrument board or close the physical gate from DRC.

The review target is the source interpretation, source-to-generated geometry, retained annular rings, stepped separation, indexing transforms, neighboring clearances and honest open conditions.
