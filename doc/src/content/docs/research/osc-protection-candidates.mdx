## TMUX comparison without assumed channel reuse

The retained TI table specifies 0.6/0.5 mA positive/negative maxima at ±16.5 V through 125 °C. WQFN continuous channel rating is 60 mA at 125 °C. The ±15 V table has a 16.5 Ω maximum and 14 pF typical on-capacitance. Floating-rail drain leakage is **typical only**. Ground must remain connected; overvoltage protection faces S, while D has rail clamps. The ±12 V dual-supply and finite-discharge trajectories have no equivalent guaranteed table. These are source-confirmed conditions, **not project-condition PASS**. [TI datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmux7412f.pdf).

The audit derives 110 existing ADG packages from the current circuit specification. There are 98 additional drive/sense connections for 82 outputs, plus 30 octave-reference receivers. The following is a **conditioned planning sensitivity**, using the table values above and existing ADG planning allowances of 1.3/0.7 mA per package:

| Package allocation | Quads | Positive/negative current |
|---|---:|---:|
| Output/sense, unrestricted global packing | 25 | 15 / 12.5 mA |
| Output/sense, module-local packing | 35 | 21 / 17.5 mA |
| Module-local output/sense plus receiver-local references | 45 | 27 / 22.5 mA |
| Replace 110 existing packages and add all 45 | 155 | 93 / 77.5 mA |

The last case changes the existing 143/77 mA switch allowance by **−50/+0.5 mA**. It could preserve the source envelopes without booking spare-channel reuse, but it is not a proven current saving at ±12 V. It also requires a coordinated input-cell substitution, local supply/control reconciliation and new package geometry. No actual substitution or ledger saving is booked. Newly fitted bypass capacitors, fault currents, pull-ups and supervision must be charged separately.

## Power-path candidates

LM74800 provides separate ideal-diode and disconnect gates. Under its specified conditions, forward servo maximum is 13.4 mV; reverse threshold is −6.5…−1.3 mV; reverse-off delay maximum is 0.875 µs for the documented voltage step and 10 nF gate load. The common-drain two-NFET arrangement is a positive-rail starting point. Its +5 V conditions, actual gate load, coarse UV/OV thresholds, hot leakage and fault energy still require analysis. [TI datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm7480.pdf).

The LTC4371 official web table suggests a 25 mV forward-servo ceiling and describes negative 4.5–16 V operation, but those claims remain **UNSOURCED in retained evidence** until byte acquisition succeeds. The next negative circuit must include a separately controlled, opposing cutoff FET; an ideal diode alone does not disconnect normal load current. Candidate connectivity is Qideal source at load −12 V, Qcut source at raw −12 V, drains joined. Driver, default-off bias, gate clamp and slew/control paths are not selected. [ADI web PDF](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/4371f.pdf).

Ideal-diode loss includes the controller's servo drop. Do not substitute a two-FET fully-enhanced resistance calculation for that drop. The [architecture decision](../decisions/osc-protection-architecture.mdx) retains the PSMN1R0-40YLD hot-resistance feasibility screen and the LM74502 reverse-current exclusion. No complete MOSFET/driver/SOA solution is selected. The source/inlet still owns maximum delivered current; this batch does not add a mandatory synth-side limiter shunt.

## Fault requirements that remain open

- **All rails and control power:** preserve +12/−12/+5 continuous requirements of 1.7/1.6/0.3 A and maximum-delivered 2.1/2.0/0.5 A. Protection drop remains at most 40 mV. Prove hot resistance, every body diode, enabled reverse current, startup linear SOA and fault let-through. Autonomous load monitors must use guaranteed threshold, tolerance and hysteresis bounds inside 11.53–12.48 V, magnitude 11.74–12.37 V, and 4.81–5.20 V. Capture surviving control power, default inhibit, startup/shutdown latch, all six arrival and failure orders, brownout/hiccup, isolation timing and controlled discharge. An abruptly collapsing raw rail still needs injection bounds before the controller reacts.
- **Return observability:** rail measurements cannot distinguish a failed inlet return replaced by a patch sleeve. A conventional pilot can share that replacement path; mechanical withdrawal detection does not cover broken conductors. Balanced bipolar loading can produce zero normal return current. Independently excite and observe the actual inlet conductors before their common bond, and prove every/all return-open cases with external returns attached. A proposed magnetic branch test would need a bounded diagnostic return for the single-surviving-wire/no-patch case, saturation/parasitic/noise analysis and an explicit revised pin contract. It is not yet a circuit. Pin 4 remains NC in the current R1 contract.
- **Local device ground:** remote AGND sensing cannot establish an individual switch's GND pin or internal bond. Redundant traces do not prove a lifted pin safe. Ground-dependent CMOS protection has no supported behavior in that state; either capture independent protection that covers it or leave the gate open. No ground connection may be silently assumed away.
- **Every signal path:** cover all 82 outputs, 16 sense branches and 30 receiver connections under powered-off/partial-power external ±12 V. Preserve source-facing orientation and local feedback; bound pre-opening clamp current, floating/discharging leakage, delay, charge/energy and enabled contention. A central rail-valid flag does not cover a disconnected receiver's local supplies. Keep references within EXT and storage/summing/timing nodes local.
- **Current and heat:** the 24.48 V, −1% resistor screen produces 24.78 mA in the 998 Ω drive path, 9.02 mA in a proposed 2.74 kΩ sense path, and about **0.303 W per 499 Ω drive resistor**. These are prospective circuit bounds, not functional guarantees at amplifier absolute maxima. In-range contention need not trip an overvoltage switch. Exact resistor derating, amplifier operating-point dissipation, simultaneous package loading and switch thermal limits remain open.
- **Precision and implementation:** preserve ≤1 mV load error, ≤2 mV calibrated path error and existing cable/load/settling/stability targets. The twelve issue-58 R/C diagnostic cases do not establish switch fault behavior. Obtain conditioned models/limits and analyze enable/fault transients, leakage, calibration, noise and current. Reconcile every actual package, island, control route and fitted capacitor before regenerating board reservations.

## Isolated precision-output candidate

A source-driven analog candidate now exists in
`design/power/precision-output-cell.json`, with generated connectivity, source
receipts, calculations and a work note beside it. It uses exact Panasonic
AQY232G3HSX isolated contacts, a 4.99 kΩ sense resistor and permanent
10 MΩ / 10 nF local feedback. The contacts have no analog ground pin, but the
independent permit source and ground-observation circuit are still missing.
No installed component or board population changes.

The pinned amplifier model meets the original 1 ms settling target in all 24
powered-on diagnostic cases (maximum error 0.272 mV, overshoot 5.486%). Only
12 cases meet the earlier stricter 400 µs diagnostic; those misses remain
recorded. Static contact R/C does not model relay switching or fault isolation.

The candidate quantifies the remaining design constraints. Its simple +5 V
LED-drive arrangement exceeds the original current ceiling. Alternative series
strings can reduce bias demand, but no driver, grouping or project-temperature
guarantee is established. Some no-substitution cases fit the hard current
ceilings while losing the required positive limiter window after rounding.
During a cold rail collapse, contacts remain conductive through detection and
release; leakage bleeders cannot absorb that current. The reservoir and 0.3 V
excursion used in the counterexample are explicit diagnostic assumptions, not
fitted parts or new acceptance limits.
