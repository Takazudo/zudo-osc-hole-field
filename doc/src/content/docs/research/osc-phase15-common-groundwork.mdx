## 1. OPA4197IPWR → OPA4197IDR

### Owner evidence: PASS in the scratch worktree

The candidate bundle `component-ti-opa4197idr` (record `rec-op-precision-d`) reuses the retained TI SBOS737C bytes (SHA-256 `27653a7d…`).

- The package option addendum (physical index 34, Addendum-Page 2) lists OPA4197IDR as Active, Production, SOIC (D) 14, 2500 LARGE T&R, MSL Level-3-260C-168 HR, −40 to 125 °C.
- The pin configuration (index 3, printed 4) is headed "D and PW Packages: OPA4197, 14-Pin SOIC and TSSOP", with one pin-function column for both packages. The pin map is therefore unchanged.
- The full-temperature input-bias maximum for D is ±5 nA; the ±15 nA subrow is PW only (§6.7, index 6, printed 7).
- The JLC row C2058532 is distributor identity only: stock 6533 on 2026-10-31.

The patch adds:

- the owner bundle;
- the inventory line `line-op-precision-d` and its routing case;
- symbol fragment `symbols/src/OPA4197IDR.kicad_sym`, derived from the IPWR fragment with the SOIC-14 footprint;
- CAD receipt `ic15-op_precision_d`;
- an intentional catalog exclusion, so no component page is published.

### Floorplan fit: 85 of 89 placements fit; the 4 control-board ones collide

The trial overlay swaps the precision packages to `SOIC-14_3.9x8.7mm_P1.27mm`, with the OPA4196IDR courtyard of 7.4 × 9.63 mm instead of 7.7 × 5.99 mm. It then runs the partition chain.

| Placements on SOIC-14 | First failure | Result |

| All 89 | `control_locality.py`: "slew courtyard collides: U102" | FAIL |
| 87 (U102 and U202 kept on TSSOP) | `control_bypass_locality.py`: "Bypass courtyard outside P: U3303" | FAIL |
| 85 (core K 68, jack JL/JR 17); U102, U202, U3303 and U3403 kept on TSSOP | none | **PASS**: floorplan, loom, mechanical, partition35 and drawing all pass |

The four control-board parts sit in fixed source-locality groups: sample_hold H1/H2 slew islands U102 and U202, and the manual_ab X1 buffers U3303 and U3403. The panel hardware stays fixed (project rule 1).

There are two ways forward:

- **Option A:** redesign those groups in `control-locality.json` and `control-bypass-locality.json`.
- **Option B:** keep two precision lines: 85 OPA4197IDR plus 4 OPA4197IPWR.
  - Option B costs $259.73 per unit and $1155.26 per lot, against $219.14 for an all-IDR build and $974.20 at HEAD. That is −$714.47 per unit against HEAD.

This trial is a courtyard capacity proposal only. Routing, DRC and physical fit are NOT RUN.

## 2. R-exact: all 14 lines meet the `r_precision` class

All 14 lines are Yageo RT0603BRD07 thin film, and each one has a retained manufacturer spec sheet that names the exact MPN. Each sheet shows 0.1 %, ±25 ppm/°C, 0.1 W at 70 °C and 75 V maximum continuous. That meets the `r_precision` policy of 0.1 %, 25 ppm, 0.1 W and 50 V.

The cost is $7.15 per unit for the added or changed elements, with 80 added placements per unit. Every stock figure covers a 5-unit lot. The smallest margin is 620R: stock 120 against 25 needed.

The tolerance model takes every element at ±0.1 % independently, across all sign corners. TCR, ageing and solder drift are excluded. Each band is relative to the HEAD nominal.

## 3. S2b groundwork

### Jack-facing pin: 98 of 98 inputs have the jack on the protected source

`s2b_protection_map.py` reads the specification. For every `input_fault_switch` channel-1 part in the 31 placement groups (98 per unit):

- ADG5412F pin 3 (S1, protected source) is on the jack-tip net.
- Pin 2 (D1) is on the local `_PROTECTED` net.
- There are 0 errors.

The TMUX7412F WQFN mapping keeps the protected side toward the jack: S1 to pad 1 (jack), D1 to pad 16 (local), SEL1 to pad 15 (enable). SCDS404B Table 6-1 states that the drain pins are not overvoltage protected, so this orientation is mandatory.

The TSSOP-16 PW TMUX7412F has the ADG pin order, but the datasheet marks it as Preview. A JLC search for TMUX7412FPWR returned no row.

### Fault rows: TMUX7412F changes three rows

Sources:

- ADG5412F Rev. C (`circuit/sources/ic-library/ADG5412F.pdf`, `ea3d12f9…`);
- TMUX7412F SCDS404B (`7396d8a3…`, the same bytes as `protection59-sources.json`).

All limits come from ±15 V or ±16.5 V tables and are not ±12 V guarantees.

| Fault row | ADG5412F | TMUX7412F | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 powered, ±12 V on the jack | PASS | PASS | — |
| F2 powered, jack beyond a rail | PASS (±55 V, 80 V channel stress) | PASS (±60 V, 85 V source-to-supply and source-to-drain) | — |
| F3 unpowered, rails 0 V, GND present | PASS. Source leakage ±40 µA typical only; drain ±50 nA max at 85 °C | PASS. IS(FA) ±125 µA max; ID(FA) ±50 nA max at 85 °C | — (TMUX has guaranteed maxima) |
| F4 unpowered, rails floating, GND present | PASS. Leakage ±10 µA typical only | PASS. Leakage typical only | — |
| F5 GND absent | **FAIL** (a GND reference must be present) | **FAIL** (same) | — |
| F6 one analog rail missing | PASS, conditional on VDD − VSS ≥ 8 V for the on state | PASS, same condition | — |
| F7 +5 V enable before the analog rails | PASS (digital input protection, switch off) | PASS (fail-safe logic to 44 V) | — |
| F8 an input must not energize an unpowered rail | PASS (source ESD has no rail diode) | PASS (powered-off protection; source ESD) | — |
| F9 several inputs patched in one quad (packing) | **PASS**: Rev. C index 24 says "the channels undisturbed by the overvoltage input continue to operate normally without additional crosstalk". The project worst case is 24 V against the 80 V limit | **UNKNOWN**: no published statement; only the 85 V stress rule, −100 dB AC crosstalk and the shared FF | **yes** |
| F10 RON and leakage on pitch inputs | PASS (14 Ω, ±2.0 nA at 85 °C) | PASS (14 Ω, ±2 nA at 85 °C) | — |
| F11 land and pin map | PASS (current land) | **FAIL**: WQFN-16 only; new land needed | **yes** |

Totals: ADG5412F has 10 PASS and 1 FAIL. TMUX7412F has 8 PASS, 2 FAIL and 1 UNKNOWN.

The protection-standard change S2b needs is therefore explicit:

1. Change the input-switch identity and land (F11).
2. Accept TMUX7412F F9 as UNKNOWN, or keep one channel per package for jack inputs. That is ADG-2 instead of ADG-3.
3. Keep the F5 limit, which is unchanged from today.

For **ADG-1** (ADG5412F packing), F9 is now covered by a manufacturer statement. The earlier cross-channel evidence gap is closed for ADG5412F. The statement is qualitative and has no table limit.
