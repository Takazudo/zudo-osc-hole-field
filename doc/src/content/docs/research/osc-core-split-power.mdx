## Keep three different quantities separate

1. **Physical ownership:** 3,385 existing K parts include 425 fitted IC packages,
   eight DNP capacitors and thirteen POWER parts. Every fitted IC's supply pins,
   each bypass owner, and 1,903 fitted AGND contacts have a candidate core owner.
   Other physical packages retain their current board owner.
2. **Module responsibility:** the 347 existing worksheet rows include core,
   jack, control and LED circuitry. Their totals below are bookkeeping for whole
   modules, **not the current in a core feed**. The source does not resolve every
   dynamic/resistor/output term into physical board currents. No part-count
   allocation or speculative current saving is used.
3. **Conductor envelope:** use the unchanged whole-source concentration screen
   of 2.1 / 2.0 / 0.5 A on +12 / −12 / +5 V and 4.6 A in any one intended AGND
   return. These are conditional source/interface requirements, not measured
   capacity, guaranteed IC current or stored-energy/fault bounds. Per-core and
   per-contact physical maxima stay **null**.

## Returns, copper and deterministic rejection screens

Retain the six-layer roles: In1.Cu continuous AGND, In2.Cu inner signals,
In3.Cu signals with −12 V fill, In4.Cu +12 V, and surface signal/local AGND.
Keep each bypass and local timing, hold, summing, integrator and feedback group
with its source owner. O1–O5 and OCTAVE_REF remain together on KA. Existing
adjacent Kelvin AGND and reference harness ≤300 mm / ≤1 nF conditions remain;
local reference feedback does not cancel remote contact or ground drop.

The preserved 191 K harnesses contain **505 signal + 376 AGND + 6 of each rail
+ 4 NC = 903 positions per end**. `harness-contract.json` retains both endpoint
Refs, every numbered pin, exact header/housing/contact identities and original
poses. Each harness has one core destination in both options, including the
shared Noise/mixer JR harnesses. Four unchanged JL/P utility harnesses are outside
this subset and remain source-hash protected. Existing GH connections are not
repacked to create supply feeds. NC positions remain unwired.

One conditional new feed reproduces the existing arithmetic:

- Wire plus two terminations: 0.013 Ω/m × 0.110 m + 0.0002 Ω = **0.00163 Ω**.
- With rail copper ≤1 mΩ and total common AGND copper ≤0.5 mΩ,
  rail-plus-one-return losses are **15.321 / 15.058 / 11.113 mV**.
- Residual to 20 mV is **4.679 / 4.942 / 8.887 mV**. One return wire and its
  ends dissipate **34.4908 mW** at the assumed 4.6 A concentration.
- Copying the complete allowance into two serial sections gives
  **30.642 / 30.116 / 22.226 mV**, a **deterministic violation** of 20 mV.
  The KA star, branches, satellite, all accesses, necks and vias share one
  global path budget; it cannot restart at a board boundary.

The retained NBS copper reference gives nominal 70 °C resistivity
2.064351135×10⁻⁵ Ω·mm. At proposed 70 µm foil, a uniform strip has
0.294907305 mΩ/square; 1 mΩ allows 3.390896 total squares and 0.5 mΩ allows
1.695448. This is only `R = ρL/(wt)`: no real plane spreading, barrel, neck,
contact class, layer-sharing or hot material acceptance follows. The completed
old K nominal field model is specific to its geometry; this lane does not restart
that parked work or present it as split-board evidence.

New core-to-front and core-to-core parallel AGND paths invalidate direct reuse
of the old GH return extrema. All mates present, unbalanced demand, a missing
return and a partly powered core require fresh joined current/return analysis.
Do not subtract opposite-rail currents, assume equal sharing, or count patch
sleeves as intentional supply returns. Local capacitor discharge and circulating
currents are outside the simple source-concentration screen.

Keep C106/C146–148 and C206/C246–248 **DNP**. In particular, six optional
4.7 µF footprints are not fitted bulk. Each existing bypass follows its IC.
Retained nominal fitted rail capacitances are 49.9 / 42.7 / 24.5 µF.

| Three cores | 7 | 82.8 / 75.6 / 57.4 | 67.2 / 74.4 / 42.6 | 131.1552 / 119.7504 / 37.8840 |

Order is +12 / −12 / +5 V. This conservatively reserves 4.7 µF per rail on
all powered boards, including a currently unused rail; passive octave boards
receive no invented reservoir. Each core bulk candidate has an explicit
5×3×5 mm B-face slot near its feed. Exact MPN, voltage/temperature behaviour,
effective ceramic capacitance, ESR, leakage and fit remain unknown. Protection
and later capacitors must consume the remaining 150/150/100 µF nominal ceilings.
The startup calculation uses the existing partition screen's 13.2/13.2/5.5 V,
+20% capacitance and ≥10 ms ramp; it fits the 2000/1900/400 mA transient
requirement after adding 1700/1600/300 mA continuous delivery. It is not an
inrush measurement or permission for a faster ramp.

## Open gates and reproducible checks

The contract's focused tests reject missing/duplicate modules, split reference
or bypass ownership, endpoint pin changes, new-feed repacking and duplicated
auxiliary reserves. Run from the repository root:

```sh
python3 design/partition/core-split/power/build.py --check
python3 -m unittest discover -s design/partition/core-split/power -p 'test_*.py' -v
python3 design/partition/core-split/power/build.py --compare-ref 1da1909846dc3255888ecd36c88cee9737c85834
```

Missing/late rails and unplugged cores can receive injection through signals,
outputs and references. #59 still owns load-side monitoring, reverse blocking,
inhibited enables, bounded injection, coordinated shutdown/discharge and
independently observable return integrity: 82 outputs, 16 feedback paths and
30 octave receivers remain obligations. Ground is never switched or fused to
hide a loop. Power down the source and external patched equipment before service.
