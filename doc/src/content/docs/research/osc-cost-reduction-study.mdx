**UNADOPTED research.** This page selects no part. The standard, inventory, schematic, PCB and BOM are unchanged. No order, quote or fabrication file exists. The study is measured against HEAD 34063a00 with P59-A rev2 not applied.

The machine-readable record is `design/readiness/cost-reduction-study.json`. `scripts/checks/cost_reduction_study.py` builds it from the read-only JLC search responses and TI datasheets retained in `.circuit-cache/sources/readiness-20261031/`, all retrieved 2026-10-31.

Cost rules:

- Unit cost uses the JLC tier that contains the per-unit quantity.
- Lot cost uses the tier that contains five times that quantity.
- Distributor rows give price and stock only. Electrical rows come from the cited manufacturer datasheet sections.

## 1. ADG5412FBRUZ: what the 110 placements do

| Function | Placements per unit | Spec row |
|---|---|---|
| `input_fault_switch`: one per input jack, channel 1 only. The source faces the jack and the drain faces the local circuit; IN is held on from +5 V through 1 kΩ with a 100 kΩ pulldown | 98 | "Every input jack first meets an ADG5412FBRUZ **source** terminal"; a bare clamp to a supply rail is rejected |
| Envelope `SLOPE_SELECT` (4 channels: REF9 or curve voltage to the rise/fall drives) | 6 | Internal selector. Not a jack-facing protection role |
| Envelope `STORAGE_CLAMP` (2 channels used: hold clamp to REF8, reset of the storage capacitor) | 6 | Internal switch on the storage node; leakage matters |

Per instance the input switches are: envelope 3, filter 4, mix5 5, mix4_vca 5, oscillator 4, offset 2, wavefolder 3, sample_hold 2, manual_ab 2 and mult 1. Each one is a quad carrying one channel.

## 2. Op-amp quads per role

Package counts per unit are: precision OPA4197IPWR 89, audio/cv OPA4196IDR 141 and indicator OPA4196IDR 33. The indicator packages are LED_PACK, IND_PACK and magnitude_indicator.

The Iq change applies to every section in a package, whether used or not: `packages × 4 × ΔIq(max over temperature)`. It falls equally on +12 V and −12 V.
