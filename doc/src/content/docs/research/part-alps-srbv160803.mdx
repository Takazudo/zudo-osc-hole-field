## Current status

Existing selected direction; manufacturer specification rechecked

Six positions; the central body is 16.2×18.5×7.5 mm, asymmetric about the shaft, and the drawing also gives an 18.2 mm support span. The central-body 0.8 mm gap alone does not establish assembly fit. See the [conditional stepped assembly decision](../decisions/osc-selector-assembly.mdx).

## Qualification boundary

The selected draft uses 3+2 adapter planes, two custom keyed extensions and five custom Ø8 mm indexed knobs at unchanged shaft centres. The earlier Ø8 mm cap was unselected study geometry. The 15 mm L dimension is not an installed PCB-to-panel spacing. Physical qualification remains NOT RUN in [issue #55](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-osc-hole-field/issues/55).
