Research pages record candidate identities, evidence reviewed, and what remains open. A candidate page does not establish selection, complete qualification, or inventory inclusion; those steps follow the exact-component workflow.

[Protection candidate research](./osc-protection-candidates.mdx) records the issue 59 source receipts, conditioned switch/package comparison and the next complete protection cell. No protection candidate is selected or installed.

The [passive identity candidates](./osc-passive-identity-candidates.mdx) and the [cost-reduction study](./osc-cost-reduction-study.mdx) price unadopted alternatives from dated JLC catalog reads. Neither page selects a part.

The [S1/S2b common groundwork](./osc-phase15-common-groundwork.mdx) records scratch-worktree trials for the OPA4197IDR footprint migration, the R-exact precision values and the S2b input-switch fault rows. Nothing is adopted.

The [component intake](./osc-components.mdx) explains that boundary. Topic research covers [sample-and-hold and slew evidence](./osc-slew-evidence.mdx), [documentation-runtime integration](./osc-documentation-integration.mdx), and [corrections to stale handoff material](./osc-handoff-corrections.mdx). The part pages retain candidate context for panel hardware, analog circuits, indicators, interconnects, and the separate zudo-pd supply.
