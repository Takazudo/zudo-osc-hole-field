## Placement: module-spread locality

`scripts/checks/partition35_floorplan.py` places the 3,385 free core packages with `scripts/checks/jack_locality.py`. The core has no panel hardware, so the core GH header pins anchor each signal net.

The first header-anchored placement crowded about 1,300 envelope/offset packages into one corner and scattered modules: 751 nets spanned 80 mm or more. Negotiated routing then stalled at about 2,500 of 2,727 nets in conflict. The current placement (v3) adds three steps:

- **Module home discs.** Each module gets an area-proportional disc around its header home. `scripts/checks/core_locality.py:spread_module_homes` pushes the discs apart inside the outline and the notch. Packages are then legalized one module at a time, largest first.
- **Core-only spacing.** A 0.6 mm courtyard gap and a 0.5 mm keep-clear ring around each IC.
- **Swap pass.** A same-footprint swap pass penalizes nets longer than 60 mm, with candidates within 25 mm of each package's net median.

Through-hole packages block both faces. IC + bypass clusters stay rigid. The #59 protection rectangle, the bulk-capacitor reserves and the supports stay obstacles. The 18 POWER-* reserves are the TP load-wire lands themselves.

| Placement | Signal HPWL | Peak RUDY / p99 | 5 mm tiles > 0.7 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Header-anchored locality | 162 m | 1.39 / — | 682 |
| v3 module-spread + swaps | 105 m | 1.24 / 0.78 | 68 |
| JL with swaps (for comparison) | 35 m | 0.93 / 0.81 | 42 |

RUDY is the bounding-box wire-demand estimate divided by ideal four-signal-layer supply. It is a congestion screen, not a routing result. The core header positions are tied to their partner boards' headers by the straight harnesses, so they stay fixed.

## Stack: six layers

`scripts/pcbgen/cut_capacity.py` bounds the first native locality placement. Its worst line is y=177 (board frame), which 853 signal nets must cross.

| Signal layers | Ideal track capacity | Ratio to demand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F.Cu + B.Cu (four layers with intact planes) | 942 | 1.10× |
| F.Cu, In2.Cu, In3.Cu, B.Cu (six layers) | 2,364 | 2.77× |

Four layers fall below the 1.5× margin used for the jack halves, so the core takes the same six-layer stack:

- F.Cu, In2.Cu, In3.Cu and B.Cu carry signals.
- In1.Cu is a continuous AGND plane.
- In3.Cu also carries the −12 V fill. A signal batch that splits the fill is rejected.
- In4.Cu is a continuous +12 V plane.
- +5 V is routed as 0.4 mm tracks.
