The shared control board now has a reproducible **partially routed, unvalidated candidate** at `boards/osc-control/osc-control-routed-draft.kicad_pcb`. Its 418 source packages and 36 supports preserve all 139 fixed control positions. The two complete slew circuits remain beside their unchanged potentiometers; all four amplifier bypass capacitors are 2.2975 mm from their supply pads.

The candidate uses the reviewed local edge tab beside RV601 and separate reservations around the six power terminals. It remains separate from the canonical partition outline. The tab and neighboring octave adapter pass their conditional source-envelope screen; installed fit remains unqualified.

The source now permutes 123 complete K/P header pairs among existing identical sites. Both ends move together; all 390 connector references and pin maps, 195 modeled cable-route geometries, 18 main power routes, and all non-header component positions remain unchanged. Native comparison confirms the same occupied pad geometry and ground-pad positions. Only the 123 corresponding reference labels move. The source proposal remains an unvalidated assembly allocation.

The summed nearest-signal-pad distance estimate falls from 29.90 m to 6.66 m on P and from 29.70 m to 25.75 m on K. These are straight-line placement estimates, not routed lengths or electrical qualification. The explicit assignment is in `design/partition/control-connector-locality.json`.

Moving header terminals invalidates their old routes. The previous routing checkpoints remain in git history; this branch rebuilds from compatible local control/bypass links and the six main-terminal arrays. Seventy-two short inner-layer segments connect the three rail arrays on a second layer. The 453-edge rerouting seed was reduced to 270 edges by selectively retaining new routes that preserve every checked ground connection, then to 189 edges by adding native-checked local links. A further protected-copper continuation and front/back detour routing reduce the count to 93, preserving every copper object from the 189-edge checkpoint. Candidate routes that disconnected ground contacts were removed before acceptance. The previous header layout reached 257 edges on main. The bounded autorouter run timed out; its partial copper was accepted only after separate native replay, DRC, connectivity and ground checks. Independent refill retains all 344 source grounds and sixteen local bypass pairs. Current and resistance limits have not been checked for this copper.
