The shared control board now has a reproducible **partially routed, unvalidated candidate** at `boards/osc-control/osc-control-routed-draft.kicad_pcb`. Its 418 source packages and 36 supports preserve all 139 fixed control positions. The two complete slew circuits remain beside their unchanged potentiometers; all four amplifier bypass capacitors are 2.2975 mm from their supply pads.

The candidate uses the reviewed local edge tab beside RV601 and separate reservations around the six power terminals. It remains separate from the canonical partition outline. The tab and neighboring octave adapter pass their conditional source-envelope screen; installed fit remains unqualified.

The earlier assignment of 123 complete K/P header pairs to existing identical sites remains. The current correction moves ninety envelope debounce passives into thirty complete resistor/resistor/capacitor groups beside their existing Texas Instruments SN74HC14DR inputs. All ICs, bypass capacitors, headers, panel controls, component identities and electrical net assignments are unchanged. Native comparison checks all 1,740 pads and confirms that exactly the 180 passive pads move.

The largest debounce-capacitor to input-pad distance falls from 144.626 mm to 7.665 mm; the median falls from 93.580 mm to 4.039 mm. The 8 mm input and 4 mm neighboring-resistor limits are project layout targets, not manufacturer requirements. These straight-line pad distances do not establish routed length or functional performance. The proposal is explicit in `design/partition/control-debounce-locality.json`.

Moving these terminals invalidates affected routes. The previous 72-edge checkpoint remains in git history. Rebuilding compatible copper and removing collisions produces a 199-edge seed, then checked local back/front routing reduces it to 23 open edges. Candidate links that disconnect ground are rejected. All 344 ground contacts, all 77 source AGND copper objects and sixteen local bypass pairs remain connected after independent refill. The six main-terminal arrays and local C222 ground stitch remain; their current and resistance capability is unqualified. Later inner-layer searches also consider distant rail islands; search distances do not establish electrical resistance or voltage drop.

All 418 visible references retain 1 mm text and checked clearance. Some moved passive references require offsets up to 16.553 mm. Native source replay, DRC, complete connectivity and independent refill form the acceptance gate for this partial copper. Current and resistance limits have not been checked for it.

The merged 43-edge checkpoint is preserved. A further bounded autorouter run timed out; its raw 30-edge candidate disconnected two grounds. Selectively retaining seven improved signal nets restored all grounds at 36 edges. Native-checked inner-layer rail and signal links then reached 23. All 3,320 prior copper objects remain unchanged. Proposed routes that violated unplated-hole clearance or disconnected ground were rejected.
