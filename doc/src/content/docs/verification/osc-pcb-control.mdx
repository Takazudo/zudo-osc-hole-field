The shared control board now has a reproducible **partially routed, unvalidated candidate** at `boards/osc-control/osc-control-routed-draft.kicad_pcb`. Its 418 source packages and 36 supports preserve all 139 fixed control positions. The two complete slew circuits remain beside their unchanged potentiometers; all four amplifier bypass capacitors are 2.2975 mm from their supply pads.

The candidate uses the reviewed local edge tab beside RV601 and separate reservations around the six power terminals. It remains separate from the canonical partition outline. The tab and neighboring octave adapter pass their conditional source-envelope screen; installed fit remains unqualified.

The unrouted placement reference remains at `boards/osc-control/osc-control-layout.kicad_pcb`, with 872 open edges. Adding the original ground prerequisite reduced that to 529. The earlier partial checkpoint reached 366. Correcting twelve remote bypass capacitors moved 22 unfixed IC/capacitor packages and required removal of their obsolete routing. The repaired copper had 432 open edges; sixteen bypass connections and ten local signal links reduce it to 406. Seventeen additional local signal links use short layer changes and reduce it to 389. A checked partial continuation preserves all prior copper and reduces it to 323; the full per-net inventory is retained in `design/partition/control-copper/ratsnest.json`.

In the earlier recovery, fresh refill exposed four ground islands hidden by the first autorouter fill. Four short front-layer signal bridges now open those back-plane boundaries, and a separate via joins an unfinished signal-layer transition. The replay checks every source ground contact after a fresh load and a second refill. It preserves the current source footprint, outline and reservation bytes. Native checks also reject a displaced bypass capacitor and require all sixteen local rail pairs to remain connected after independent refill. A passing DRC alone is not treated as connected or electrically compliant copper.
