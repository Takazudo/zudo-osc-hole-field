## Historical connectivity checkpoint

The following paragraphs retain the [f663143 integration checkpoint](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-osc-hole-field/commit/f663143edf92476dd6a23730931b02b92413c88d), before later source I/O and component changes. Their counts and native results are historical, not a new check of the current tree. Current retained identities and counts live in `design/reports/master-audit.json`, `design/reports/netlist-stats.json` and `design/reports/master-erc-warning-baseline.json`; use the pinned smoke command below for a fresh native check.

At that checkpoint the generated master had **35 hierarchy instances**: 33 modules, `OCTAVE_REF` and `POWER`. The source audit found all **438 locked panel UIDs** exactly once (180 jacks, 144 controls, 114 indicators), **5,747 unique designators** including ten power flags, **5,737 exported physical components**, **4,912 KiCad nets**, **209 Sensitive nets**, **97 named islands**, and **622 multi-unit packages**. At that checkpoint every populated library unit was present exactly once, with explicit no-connect pins. The checkpoint versions of `design/reports/master-audit.json` and `design/reports/netlist-stats.json` retain its Sensitive-net/island membership and component, IC, instance and pins-per-net distributions.

No Sensitive net touches panel hardware outside its own island, and every Sensitive member has an island. Nine Sensitive nets cross two *internal* named islands: N1 `NOISE_VDD`, `WHITE_RAW`, `PINK_RAW`; and A01–A06 `OUT_INTERNAL`. These are visible in the audit and must co-locate on one board despite their different logical island names. No Sensitive net may cross any board connector. They touch no panel part across an island boundary. That checkpoint did not close the physical board partition.

At that checkpoint, pinned KiCad 10.0.6 ERC on the full hierarchy had **zero errors and 240 documented `pin_to_pin` warnings**; `schematic/reports/erc-notes.md` enumerates exact per-sheet counts and causes. The KiCad exported netlist matches every specified symbol pin, including deliberate no-connects. Numeric clip LED designators remove the earlier generic annotation warning without changing panel UIDs or coordinates. Integration also corrected 143 formerly all-NC active units across 63 packages: unused OPA channels are grounded followers, unused HC14 inputs are grounded, and unused LM393B inputs have defined AGND/+5 V states with outputs NC. The common cell compiler and one manually instantiated oscillator comparator are the only circuit sources changed; selected signal-unit pins, 438 panel bindings and 5,747 designators remain stable. This reduces net count from 5,153 to 4,912 by replacing isolated NC nets with defined local connections. `schematic/exports/master-hierarchy.pdf` and one SVG for each of the 13 distinct generated child-sheet sources are local draft exports under `schematic/exports/`.

## Reproduction and limits

`bash scripts/schgen/regen-master-reports.sh` regenerates the schematic and produces the netlist statistics, full sensitive/island audit and rail budget. Its `--check` mode rejects drift. Canonical regeneration also refreshes these marked current tables; `python3 scripts/schgen/build_schematic_status.py --check` checks their correspondence to the master budget. `bash scripts/schgen/smoke.sh` checks the pinned KiCad ERC warning map, netlist parity and a deliberately corrupted hold-node fixture. `bash scripts/schgen/export-master.sh` regenerates the PDF/SVG exports. `pnpm circuit:check`, `pnpm check`, and the guarded site build are documentation gates. Their success does not establish analog behavior, installed power capacity, physical fit, safe faults or thermal margin.
