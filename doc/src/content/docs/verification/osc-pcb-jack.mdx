## Electrical acceptance remains unresolved

Closed local bypass loops **do not mean their rails are fed**. Only 4/214 JL and 2/200 JR bypass rail pads join their load-side land. JL still has +12 V 135, −12 V 143 and +5 V 59 native open edges; JR has 139, 132 and 59 respectively. Only 52/599 JL and 25/553 JR fitted master-package rail pads reach their land (547/528 remain unfed). All fitted rail pins/passives, their exact native identities and land-feed status are enumerated in `reports/power-locality.json`. These are unresolved power obligations, separate from the remaining 1,063 JL / 1,109 JR signal edges.

The combined branch plus K common AGND plane/neck target remains ≤0.5 mΩ, including actual shared copper and main terminal transfer. The GH contact bound remains ≤0.5 A, with every alternative's finite access resistance charged and the worst candidate's own PCB/contact access taken as zero. Rail transfer ≤1 mΩ and hot distribution drop ≤20 mV also remain unaccepted until actual fitted feeds and current-dependent paths are extracted. No target was relaxed.

Ground screens are **historical or experimental, NOT ACCEPTED**. The final ownership-corrected 0.25 mm attempt did not solve: J900001 pad 2 at its actual courtyard cut (119.875, 84.905 mm) had 89 native-contained candidate couplings, all rejected for overlapping represented plane/access conductor ownership. Native ground continuity passes; the unresolved gate is the numerical finite interface/collar representation. Earlier seam-excluding meshes, arbitrary nearest anchors, whole-courtyard cuts and overlapping annular/sheet models cannot be acceptance evidence. The next implementation needs local fine-region meshes or shared finite faces at arrays and GH cuts, preserving fixed physical patches and one physical barrel network. `design/partition/ground-extraction-handoff.json` records exact cache/source hashes, commands, failures and successor scope.

The minimum 25 µm finished barrel copper, 70 µm foil and nominal 70°C calculation are conditional draft requirements. [NBS Copper Wire Tables](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/hb/nbshandbook100.pdf) supplies the retained copper reference constants; it does not qualify this PCB. The arrays require factory-qualified filled/capped via-in-pad, or a documented solder process proving full coverage, no wicking and retention. This is **NOT RUN #65**, with no supplier or service selected. K's actual extraction and finite residual common/access allocations remain [#43](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-osc-hole-field/issues/43); no zero-access K assumption is accepted.

Bounded full-half 180 s, selective 120 s and extended 1,800 s autorouter strategies produced no SES and imported no copper. The current copper comes from the preserved deterministic native-gated stages. Continue [#38](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-osc-hole-field/issues/38) for authoritative ground/current extraction, all fitted power feeds and the exact remaining signal topology. Warning identities/dispositions remain in `reports/warning-dispositions.json`; silkscreen/text cleanup and native regression coverage remain [#44](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-osc-hole-field/issues/44). Physical fit, looms, hot Kelvin/current, solder retention, stability and enclosure qualification remain [NOT RUN #65](https://github.com/Takazudo/zudo-osc-hole-field/issues/65), with protection #59, source/inlet #57, selector #55 and LED #64 gates still open.
