Three separate 5 × 3 mm bulk slots consume another 45 mm² of that area, leaving at most 1,345.8 mm² for output/sense protection. The bulk slots were moved within the proposed rectangle to clear current source courtyards. Their exact capacitor identities and protection circuit dimensions remain **OPEN #59**; no fit is inferred from the remaining area.

`boards/osc-jack/reports/replication-feasibility.json` compares 22 repeated J module pairs against the exact source face and footprint centres. None has a complete single translation, so whole-module route replay would require a source placement revision. `routing-attempt-1.json` records the first timeout; `routing.json` records the second. Both left 5,611 native ratsnest edges and no copper. A revised J partition or placement/routing strategy is required before #38 can close. The current `boards/osc-jack/reports/drc.json` and top/bottom PNGs are the native KiCad 10.0.6 evidence.
