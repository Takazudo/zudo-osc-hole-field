Component-led spacing

The selected SRBV160803 body is 16.2 × 18.5 mm in plan. 17 mm column pitch leaves 0.8 mm nominal body gap. OCT/RANGE/SYNC occupy consecutive rows so a full-size pot body does not immediately follow the long OCT body.

The small Bourns actuator is 6 mm nominal. Its 12 × 12 mm board reservation is a conservative planning box, not a selected courtyard. Jack nuts and trigger caps remain unqualified.

Construction retained

Flat panel → height-compatible interface boards → rear core. Jack bushings clamp through the panel; board connectors and supports are factory-fitted. No home soldering.

Core front at 24 mm behind the panel is an adjustable design target, not a measured requirement. The existing power stack needs its own deeper pocket and full thermal/fit check.
