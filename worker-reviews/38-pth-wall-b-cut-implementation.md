# Conditional PTH wall mean to internal B.Cu cut

Issue 38 local work note, 2026-09-30. Implemented `scripts/pcbgen/pth_wall_to_b_cut.py` and its focused fixture. This is a standalone constructive field for the **mean** current on one full circular PTH inner wall. The complete finished wall, full same-net B annulus, B foil thickness, resistivity maximum and their positive dimensions are conditional PROJECT inputs. The actual manufactured 25 µm wall and current support have not been qualified. No model entry or electrical acceptance is claimed.

Let `ri<ro<R`, board depth `L`, B foil thickness `t_B`, and `0<d<t_B`. Source current `I` enters uniformly across the complete inner wall. The shell occupies `ri<r<ro, 0<z<L`; the sleeve occupies `ro<r<R, L-d<z<L`. Write `D=ro²-ri²`, `s=(r²-ri²)/D`, `g=1/d` on `(L-d,L)` and zero elsewhere, and `G(z)=integral_0^z g`. With `Q=I/(2*pi*L)`, the shell stream function is `Psi=Q[(1-s)z+s*L*G]`; `Jr=Psi_z/r`, `Jz=-Psi_r/r`, `Jtheta=0`. Thus `div J=(1/r)partial_r(r*Jr)+partial_z Jz=0` exactly. At `r=ri`, `Jr=I/(2*pi*ri*L)`; at `r=ro`, `Jr=I/(2*pi*ro*d)` only in the B sleeve interval. At `z=0,L`, `Jz=0`. The jump in `Jr` at `z=L-d` has no distributional divergence because the normal `Jz` is continuous there.

For `D2=R²-ro²`, the sleeve field is `Jr=I*(R²-r²)/(2*pi*d*D2*r)` and `Jz=I*(z-L)/(pi*d*D2)`. Its radial divergence `-I/(pi*d*D2)` cancels its axial divergence `+I/(pi*d*D2)`. The sleeve inner radial trace matches the shell pointwise; outer radial trace and exterior B-face axial trace vanish. On the internal B-foil cut `z=L-d, ro<r<R`, the outward profile is `I/(pi*D2)` and integrates to `I`. The shell continues below this annular cut. It is one physical metal volume with the sleeve, joined at `r=ro`; their common interface is not counted as an external terminal.

`resistance_upper` charges shell and sleeve energies once, using `pi>3`, exact fractions of the represented binary64 dimensions and an outward binary64 result. Its bound is conditional on a pointwise `rho<=rho_max`; further fields on overlapping metal need full cross-energy accounting. The separate existing `barrel_source_flux` zero-mean correction can cover wall redistribution, and F/B exterior annular face injections need their own lifts. A downstream native conductor field must match the uniform internal B-cut profile pointwise; a scalar equal current is insufficient. Its paired primal must extend continuously over the full possible wetted wall and exterior annular faces and their attached terminal metal.
