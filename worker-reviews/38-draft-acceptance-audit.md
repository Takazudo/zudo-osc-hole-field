# Issue 38 draft acceptance audit (read-only, 2026-09-30)

## Decision

**Issue 38 is not ready to close.** Its latest body permits an **unvalidated draft** and a specifically bounded, named *signal-routing* remainder after a source/partition replan. It does not permit unresolved rule, parity, source isolation, geometry, AGND, rail-feed or electrical-model contradictions to be called completed routing. The old single-J block in the body is superseded by the explicit #66 two-half handoff. The coupled-power-recovery block calls its broad-plane candidates **NOT SELECTED** and says to retain the canonical checkpoint until native all-load power/AGND continuity and the electrical model are accepted.

The issue body names these deterministic electrical limits: combined branch plus K common AGND spreading/neck <=0.5 mΩ including main transfer and vias; every GH <=0.5 A with finite positive K allocation; each rail transfer <=1 mΩ; hot board distribution <=20 mV; complete source/rail/return <=0.20 V. The 4.6 A return, 2.1/2.0/0.5 A maximum-delivered rail envelopes and conductor/wire/termination allowances are conditional requirements, not measured or guaranteed source performance. The chosen current/redistribution class is a separate project requirement, not inferred from the rail caps. A numerical result for a selected pad profile cannot be silently promoted to a result for all permitted physical source profiles.
