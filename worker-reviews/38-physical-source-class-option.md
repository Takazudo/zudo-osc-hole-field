# Issue 38 physical source class: independent read-only assessment

**Decision:** The repository can state a *hypothetical conditional project class* and prove a theorem parameterized by its assumptions. The retained evidence cannot yet select a physically applicable finite class for all 4,143 own-source contacts, compute a defensible numeric whole-network bound, or accept issue 38. Issue 65 properly remains **NOT RUN** for manufactured and hot measurements. Its existence does not fill missing computational inputs.

## What is already fixed

The current source manifest identifies JL 1,070, JR 896, K 1,903, P 214, and EL 60 fitted own AGND contacts. The family split is 3,620 drill-free convex SMD, 218 via-intersected SMD (213 single drill and five multiple drill), and 305 PTH. Native identities, nominal pad/cut/foil geometry and local geometric covers are useful inputs. Each manifest row nevertheless says physical source support is unqualified. The external signed-current coefficient budget and a separate zero-net redistribution L2 budget are each 4.6 A globally, under `contact-flux-class.md`; these are selected **PROJECT requirements**, not demonstrated circuit operating limits or guarantees about real terminal flux. A zero-net source contact cannot be dropped.

The retained NBS copper resistivity relation supports a *nominal* 70 °C extraction (`ground-material-evidence.json`), but does not bound actual hot PCB, lead, solder, plated barrel or contact resistivity. Four-layer nominal 70 µm foil and 1.6 mm board depth, and the 25 µm finished PTH wall requirement, are proposal inputs rather than manufactured lower/upper intervals. The 0.18 × 0.25 mm GH solder support, 0.025–0.05 mm thickness, and hot solder rho ≤2e-4 ohm mm are a conditional GH candidate only; they have not been admitted for each GH and cannot be copied to 4,143 own-source contacts.

## Exact missing admission by family

| Family | Existing nominal result | Required physical and mathematical inputs still absent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3,620 convex SMD | Native ref/pad/UUID/face and convex pad; no drill intersects pad. | For **each** contact, a source-applicable maximum possible wetting/contact-metal domain and a positive contained foil slab under that entire domain; a nonzero contained reference patch; finite positive slab-height interval; material/etch/registration and hot rho upper bound; face-correct normal; exact lead/solder and active-circuit boundary. A KiCad full pad is not proof of full wetting or a process-minimum slab. `convex_source_flux.py` can evaluate a coefficient only after these are supplied. |
| 218 drilled SMD | Actual plated-via cuts, finite 2D local covers, and 213/5 single/multiple drill split. | Physical cut, wall, annulus, overlap and material intervals under drill/etch/registration perturbations; support partition across exterior pad and inner-wall boundaries; constructive 3D wall/annulus/foil and reference transfer for each cut piece; cross energy and continuous primal extension. Replacing the cut with its convex hull or ignoring a drill is invalid. |
| 305 PTH | Nominal full-depth inner wall and exterior annuli for 100 JL, 80 JR, five K, 120 P contacts; conditional ≥25 µm shell and positive nominal overlap squares. | A physically supported full 360° wall and finished diameter/plating/foil/registration ranges; actual lead and solder attachment and all possible wall/F/B annular current supports; current partition among them; exterior-face and annulus-to-foil lifts, material ownership at overlaps, full cross energy and a compatible primal field. The wall zero-net and mean-to-one-annulus constructions in `barrel_source_flux.py` cover only pieces of this. |

All families additionally need complete passive GH/main lead, crimp, mating, solder and wire volumes with pointwise matching traces; all ten board operators, paired primal/dual witnesses and outward numerical allowances. A selected current/redistribution class does not make an internal passive cut an equipotential terminal.

## Minimal legitimate option

Retain the current 4,143-row native manifest and publish a **symbolic conditional source-class specification**, with explicit free positive variables for each required minimum area/thickness, maximum support, hot rho, dimensional perturbation, and external L2 flux class. State the premise separately for each exact native contact and process family. For the 3,620 convex rows, apply `convex_source_flux.py` only to rows for which an actual contained slab and patch are supplied; otherwise keep the coefficient unresolved. Use the already retained `contact-flux-class.md` current budgets without narrowing them to make a voltage result pass. For drilled SMD/PTH rows, record the missing 3D transfers rather than assign zero cost. This can be a reviewable mathematical *if-then* model, with #65 tasked to determine whether physical assemblies satisfy it. It is not a numeric acceptance result until all premise values and all fields are supplied and checked against the current board epoch.

A numeric conditional class could be chosen as a **new explicit project process requirement** without selecting a supplier, but the present record contains no reviewed value set that works for all rows. Choosing arbitrary convenient minimum wetting, slab height, wall dimensions, or hot resistivity after seeing numerical margins would be a fabricated guarantee. Exact orderable MPN/package support is incomplete for some component/footprint families (generic KiCad 0805 receipt has manufacturer/MPN null), and even exact MPN identity alone generally supplies no solder-wetting minimum, flux-shape class, or factory finished-copper interval. The viable next design step is to specify the per-family process envelope and per-row geometry with traceable premises, then recompute; the later qualification in #65 must test those premises and may reject them.

The impossibility of a present uniform numeric bound is direct: a fixed terminal current has unbounded slab energy as supported area tends to zero, and redistribution energy can diverge as slab height tends to zero or hot rho has no finite upper bound. Finite nominal KiCad copper and a 25 µm *proposed* plating shell do not prevent these limits for real source/lead/solder support. Even a valid convex coefficient cannot close drilled/PTH transfers or whole-network common and voltage bounds by itself.
