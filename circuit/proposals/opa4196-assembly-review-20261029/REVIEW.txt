Remaining analog acceptance dependencies, reusing existing bridge findings:
Source gives +/-5V nominal magnitude indication, 5.6kohm sense resistance and qualitative brightness
saturation allowance. It does not establish quantitative acceptance for zero-cross dead band,
recovery after clipping, or visible modulation/flicker. No new analog bound claimed here.
A meaningful qualification requires (1) minimum/maximum actual rails and operating temperature,
(2) command waveform/amplitude/slew/frequency envelope including overload, (3) acceptable dark
zone and settling/recovery/brightness error, and (4) bounded bridge parasitics and diode/LED
forward-drop/recovery behavior at the actual current and temperature. Existing typical curves
and op-amp-only models cannot close those requirements. Preserve prior conservative bridge bounds.
A targeted future test should measure/model command-vs-sense error across zero, step recovery
from both clipped polarities and ringing at those declared corners. Physical testing is not
currently authorized; a faithful full-loop simulation remains dependent on suitable component
models/parameters. No new source defect is proven and no repeated broad tests are useful.
