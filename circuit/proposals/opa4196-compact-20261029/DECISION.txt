Recommendation: advance this named alternative for development review. Concrete choice
A. Existing alternatives: U110 and U210 are TL074HIDR (two quad packages).
B. Proposed overlay: those same two packages become OPA4196IDR. All other components, electrical pins/nets, footprint assignments and placement are preserved. H1/H2 remain 13 IC packages each; the 12 removed spare-only repository packages are not restored. No PCB is generated or edited.
Both use the existing SOIC-14_3.9x8.7mm_P1.27mm footprint. The cached symbols have equal electrical pin geometry. OPA4196 identity and historical LCSC C2057385 are taken from the received candidate's U106; this is not a current stock, price or procurement approval. Cost difference and live availability remain unknown. A low-cost preference alone does not establish TL074H suitability.

Why this advances the engineering decision
At the source's +/-12 V rails and nominal +/-5 V monitor signal, the stricter TL074H recommended input range is -8 to +12.1 V. Nominal operation fits, but a -10 V excursion does not. OPA4196 has rail-to-rail input capability; restoring it removes that particular TL074H limitation. This does not prove clipping/recovery behavior of either nonlinear bridge loop.
TI typical per-channel quiescent currents are 0.9375 mA for TL074H and 0.14 mA for OPA4196. The two-quad arithmetic difference is 8*(0.9375-0.14)=6.38 mA. This is a datasheet-typical comparison, not a measured current or guaranteed whole-board power saving.
The actual magnitude loop uses R128/R228=5.6 kohm, four BAS16GW-QX diodes and APHHS1005QWF/D LED. At a 5 V command, nominal sense current is 0.893 mA. Returning the original amplifier preserves circuit function/connectivity, but does not establish analog equivalence, zero-crossing dead band, saturation recovery, cold forward-drop margin or nonlinear-loop stability. The existing bridge analysis and missing qualified transient models remain applicable. No invented simulation model is used.
