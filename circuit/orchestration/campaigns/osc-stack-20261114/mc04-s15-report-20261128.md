## Readings made where the brief does not spell out a rule

- **R1 (C5 scope).** C5 compares the recomputed figure only where the S8 record carries a pad figure. Runs that
  have only the origin-based estimate are listed and not compared. Control only.
- **R2 (stage 3 caps shared).** The 400-replay and 100 000-call caps are shared across all eight sets, the stricter
  reading. Neither cap was approached, so this cannot change a verdict.
- **R3 (C16 placement).** C16 runs at the start of stage 2, on its first 12 tasks, because it needs stage 1 output.
  Its time counts toward stage 2.
- **R4 (C10 reused).** Stage 1 reads the NG census that C10 writes (`checkpoints/census_ng.json`). The driver then
  confirms that the stage 1 counts after rule 4 equal C10's (STOP 5 otherwise).
- **R5 (pin-distance pairs under min).** On a net where a protected part moved, a (point, pin) pair that is missing
  from paper or base fails NG3, the stricter reading. Every failing NG vector also fails NG1, so this cannot change a
  verdict.
- **R6 (stage 2 pass).** A stage 2 pass means A1, A3, A4 and A6 on the rows-only replay, plus NG on the effective
  scene. Overlay items (A5, A7 to A9, A11) and A12 belong to stage 4.
- **R7 (overlay knock-on).** A decoupler row that breaks the block rule while its IC row is unchanged fails A8, with
  no knock-on exemption. This is the stricter reading. Stage 4 never ran an overlay, so the reading was not exercised.
