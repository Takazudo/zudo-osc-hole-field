## Method

The code is `scripts/stack/studies/mc04_s9_slot_moves/slot_moves_a2.py`. It writes `result-a2.json`, SHA-256
`9d524988de302e6d0052ab26b503bf24c71ce5f2c693f30b111e66955cc348d2`. Its tests are in `test_slot_moves_a2.py`.

- **Inputs.** The model, inputs and fixed items are as in S9-A. The S9-A files match `254d1cbf`. The input pins include
  `design/stack/trimmers/relocation-plan.json`.
- **Old rules reproduced.** `slot_moves.py --check` rebuilds the S9-A `result.json` byte for byte under the old rules.

Only the four rule changes apply:

1. **Input-buffer parts by kind.**
   - These may not move: the buffer IC, and any part on a `*_FB`, `*_SUM` or `*_SENSE` net.
   - A decoupler may move on its IC's face, no farther from its IC than in the current record.
   - An R_ISO (touching only `*_DRIVE` and `*_REMOTE`) may move no farther from its op-amp than now.
   - Any other input-buffer cell part may not move. This is my reading; it decided no candidate.
   - In the S8 displacement step, only R_ISO parts of input-buffer cells are displaceable, and the condition above is
     checked after the move.
2. **Covered block members.** A target's block members that a fixed link covers are re-placed. Its decouplers stay on
   the IC's face, no farther than at their paper position.
3. **Trimmers keep their face.** This applies to both single re-placement and S8 displacement.
4. **The five ICs stay clear.** No moved link may meet the recorded courtyard of U1415, U5110, U5209, U5210 or U8409. The
   courtyard is grown 0.35 mm, the obstacle-model growth; this is stricter than the brief's raw overlap.

**Runs.**
- Every accepted candidate is evaluated alone against the record scene: up to 200 per link and grid, under a 4 h ceiling.
  Neither was reached (the run takes 19 min).
- U5209 variant B pairs: each of the first 4 clean L13.01.01.02 candidates (the U5209 block kept clear, U5209 not yet
  restored) is tried with a full L13.01.02.01 search.
- The joint scene takes, in the ruled order, the cheapest clean candidate of each IC that stays clean with the moves
  already taken.

## Interpretations made

- **The R_ISO REMOTE condition.** The site and its link do not move, so "the REMOTE site keeps its class distance" is
  read as follows: the R_ISO stays within the class distance of its REMOTE site, or, where it is already beyond it, no
  farther than now.
- **Decouplers outside the targets.** Every re-placed decoupler is held to its IC's face and its current-record distance,
  not only decouplers of input buffers. This is conservative.
- **Rule 2 for U5209.** Rule 2 uses the S8 block definition (U5209, C5234, C5235, C5207, R5246, R5250, R5251). C5213,
  R5228 and R5253, which the brief lists, are not members of that block. C5213 is handled as a gate part on the recorded
  area.
