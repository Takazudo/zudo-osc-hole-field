## Item 1: the fix

`scripts/stack/studies/mc04_s7_r12/sleeve-eviction.patch` changes `scripts/checks/partition35_floorplan.py`:
- `sleeve_keepouts(board)` reads every active sleeve fixing (`kind` "sleeve …") and its keep-out radius for each
  board it joins. The source is `design/stack/fixings/fixing-sites.json`, which `fixings_check` also uses.
- `occupy_keepout(grid, centre, r)` marks every 0.25 mm cell of the open keep-out disk as occupied. A courtyard
  placed on free cells is therefore at least r from the centre, which is `fixings_check`'s rule.
- The near-slot eviction loop (`EVICTED_LOCAL_BOARDS`) occupies these disks on both faces before it places anything.
  Only that loop sees them.

`sleeve_eviction_tests.py` (4 tests, all passing with the patch applied; named so the suite does not collect it) checks:
- an evicted 0603 whose nearest free pose is inside SLV-U2's keep-out takes the next pose clear of it;
- every cell inside the disk is occupied;
- only active sleeves count, on their own boards;
- the committed list holds SLV-U2.

## Options

1. **(a) Defer the keep-out parts, as in run 2.** Make "place keep-out-displaced parts after the others" part of the
   generator rule, and accept D4101 and D4102 at about 34 mm as interim-GH locality acceptances, as R8381 and R8382
   already are. This is the smallest change: four parts move and no others.
2. **(b) Accept run 1.** Seven parts move: D8311 by 24.07 mm, to 36.6 mm from its slot. D4102 goes to 29.6 mm.
3. **(c) Let the near-slot loop evict or nudge small pinned passives** to give D4101 and D4102 a pose within 24 mm.
   This moves more parts, and I have not computed it.

I recommend (a). It is the only option that leaves every other part where it is. Both diodes stay on B.Cu on the JR
top row, 12 to 16 mm from SLV-U2.
