## What to establish

Read the plan page first. The facts that bound the search:

- **Envelope.** The replacement courtyard must stay inside the JST GH courtyard it replaces, on both boards: 6.1 × 7.7 mm (three positions), 6.1 × 12.7 mm (GH7 sites, which take a five-position part) and 6.1 × 13.94 mm (six positions). The planning envelope is 5.8 mm × (2n + 1) mm for n positions at 2.00 mm pitch, single row.
- **Both halves SMT.** The opposite face is occupied at most sites, so through-hole tails are excluded.
- **Positions.** 1×3, 1×5 and 1×6.
- **Heights.** The mated height is the gap between the facing copper faces. EL to P is fixed at 7.2 mm. The three gaps to K move together with K's plane: adapter gap `h`, P gap `h + 4.2 mm`, jack-board gap `h + 6.0 mm`. The planning value is `h` = 6.9 mm (so 6.9, 11.1 and 12.9 mm); any `h` of about 6.2 mm or more is acceptable if one family then gives all three. Report which `h` values each family supports and in which height steps.
- **Electrical.** Contact current rating and contact resistance (maximum, and typical if stated). The return share is about 0.3 to 0.8 A per contact by first estimate.
- **Mating.** The manufacturer's allowed mating misalignment in X, Y and angle, the insertion and withdrawal force per contact, and the rated mating cycles.
- **Process.** Pick-and-place feature (cap or pad) on the three-position parts, reflow stability of a tall single-row stacker (locating pegs, weld tabs), coplanarity, and compatibility with through-hole soldering of jacks and pots on the same face.
- **Availability.** Whether the assembly factory's own parts channel lists the exact part (the inventory's `lcsc` codes show the channel in use). Read public listings only. A listing snapshot is an identity and stock observation, not a datasheet.
- **Spacers for ST-04.** Conductive threaded posts between facing 4 × 4 mm lands at gaps of `h + 4.2` and `h + 6.0` mm: reflowed SMT spacers and mechanically fixed alternatives (screwed standoffs, swaged or press-fit posts). For each class: thread, body and land dimensions, height steps and tolerance, material and plating, any stated resistance or current figure, torque and pull-out figures.

Catalogue-level anchors from the plan page, NOT RETAINED and not a preference: Samtec SMM sockets with TMM or TW-SM stackers; Würth WA-SMSI spacers; a 1.27 mm single-row pair as the fallback class. Survey other manufacturers before recommending.
