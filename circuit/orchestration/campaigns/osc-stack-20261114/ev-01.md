## Why this task exists

The thermal screen (ST-05) compares the board at a part with the limit of the exact part, and it takes a limit only from a fact of a bundle (DS-95). The bundles of the panel parts hold no temperature fact, although each of them retains the maker's sheet. The manager read those sheets on 2026-11-15: the panel pots print an operating temperature of −10 to +50 °C, and the control board reads above that on paper in a closed case. A decision on this goes to the owner (DS-108), and a figure that the owner decides on is a fact of a bundle and not a reading in a chat (DS-105). Attempt a3 of ST-05 waits for this task and pins the facts you add.
