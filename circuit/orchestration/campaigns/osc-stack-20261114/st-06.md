## What to specify

Read the plan page section first, then `design/spec/modules/oscillator.py` (the pulse path from `PULSE_RAW` through `PUL_DIV_TOP`/`PUL_DIV_BOT`, `PULSE_CMP`, `PUL_PULL` and `PUL_SCALE`; the octave path `OCT_SELECTOR`, `OCT_SER`, `OCT_PD`, `OCT_FILTER`, `OCT_RECEIVER`; `SYNC_SELECT`), `design/spec/modules/oscillator-work-note.md`, the evidence bundles `component-ti-sn74hc74dr`, `component-ti-sn74hc00dr`, `component-ti-sn74hc14dr`, `component-ti-lm393bidr` and `component-dailywell-2ms3t1b1m2qes` under `.claude/skills/`, the pages `architecture/osc-rail-ledger.mdx` and `architecture/osc-standard-cells.mdx`, and the placement lock rows `C:O2.OCT` and `C:O4.OCT` in `design/grid/placements.lock.json`.

1. **Clock node.** The existing `PULSE_LOGIC` stops toggling when the pulse width reaches 0% or 100%. The second half of `PULSE_CMP` is tied off (pins 5, 6, 7) and could square the sawtooth instead. Compare both, including what the player hears at the pulse-width extremes and during hard and soft sync, and propose one.
2. **Divider.** Two stages from one SN74HC74 per oscillator: connections, preset and clear tie-offs, power-up state, behaviour on sync, bypass capacitor, and the unused-input rules from the evidence bundle.
3. **Select logic.** The toggle is a single-pole on-off-on part. Derive two static select lines with defined levels in all three positions and during contact travel, and gate PUL normal, the divide-by-two square and the divide-by-four square with parts already in the inventory. No audio signal crosses the board joint.
4. **Level into `PUL_SCALE`.** `PULSE_LOGIC` is an open-collector output with a 4.7 kΩ pull-up to +5 V. State the source impedance and levels of the selected signal and show that the scaled output stays within the present PUL level in all three positions.
5. **Octave input.** How the octave input of OSC2 and OSC4 is held at zero octaves on K once the selector is gone: the node, the parts that stay or go, and the effect on the shared octave reference.
6. **Joint and panel binding.** The pin map of the 1×3 pair (two select lines and AGND, in the pin order of every other P-to-K pair), the toggle's pin map with its evidence status (the bundle marks some labels unverified), the panel binding at the two fixed centres, and the proposed change of identity, board domain and hole class for the grid source revision. Proposal only.
7. **Loads and placement.** The +5 V and other rail deltas per oscillator for the rail ledger, and the board area the added parts need inside the OSC2 and OSC4 cores on K (count and courtyard area only; no placement).
8. **Verification plan.** An ideal-model simulation plan and the checks ST-11 must run (ERC, netlist parity, the model contract tests), with pass criteria.

Write `design/stack/sub-toggle/README.md` (verdict headings), `design/stack/sub-toggle/spec.json` (nets, parts by inventory line, pin maps, the state table) and `scripts/stack/sub_toggle/` with a unit test that enumerates every toggle position and both contact-travel states against the state table.
