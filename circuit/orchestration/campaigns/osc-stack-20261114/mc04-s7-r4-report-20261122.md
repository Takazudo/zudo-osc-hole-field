| `scripts/checks/partition35_floorplan.py` | Applies the move to the floorplan, as below |

**How `k_block_poses.py` places a block:**
- The IC and its bypass capacitors move as the floorplan's rigid bypass cluster (`bypass_cells`), turned to the
  IC's front rotation.
- The IC goes to the nearest free pose to its front-board position.
- The other members go nearest the IC, on the same face.
- A block that does not fit fails clearly.

**What the floorplan does:**
- A moved part drops its front pin and is pinned at its K pose. An unmatched relocation entry fails.
- A K block pin that overlaps any K pin, header, aperture or support fails.
- Pinned parts under a GH header land are released with their IC or bypass group.
- The bypass check now covers the moved K blocks.
- The beyond-24 mm acceptances are repeated (below).
