## Method (Revision 2 item 5)

**Ruled poses first:**
- the eight of Revision 1 (Part A, with their passives);
- U4524, U1318 and U4417 at their A2 poses.

**What a target may displace:**
- up to two other IC blocks;
- small SMD passives under the A2 rule.

**Rules for a displaced block:**
- It is the IC, its decouplers and its cell-local R/C within 12 mm.
- It moves rigidly within 24 mm, at any turn on either face.
- It must land legal without displacing anything further.
- Input buffers are never displaced. Any IC with a `high_impedance_input` or `remote_buffer` unit counts as one.

**Fixed:**
- outline;
- panel hardware and holes;
- jacks and locked parts;
- link fields, tails, slots and latch margins;
- sleeves and fixing keep-outs;
- the ruled poses.

**Order of the search:** candidates (target poses within 24 mm whose only obstacles are allowed blocks and passives)
are tried fewest blocks first, then nearest first, up to 120 trials.

**When nothing fits:** the poses within 24 mm are scanned for the smallest set of obstacles the rule cannot move:
fixed items, non-evictable footprints, and IC blocks that are input buffers, have no own pose, or exceed two.
