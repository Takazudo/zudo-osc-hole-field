## Stop: step 7

I ran `sync.py` on JL, JR and K (all exit 0), then `pose_swapped.py --all-offset` (JL 42, JR 21 and K 17 parts at
their floorplan poses). `place.py` then refused all three boards: "J9xxxxx: board differs from source footprint
origin".

**The cause is the GH header packer.** `connector_packing35.py` sorts each board pair's signal nets and cuts them
into consecutive groups of four, one per header. It also numbers the header references in that order. The stage 7
move changes the crossing nets:
- JL-K goes from 100 to 108 nets and JR-K from 106 to 118;
- 12 nets stop crossing.

Every group after the first changed net shifts:

| Item | Count against 6093081c |
| --- | --- |
| Header sites | 390 unchanged, 10 new (the 5 ruled pairs), 0 removed |
| Harness IDs at unchanged sites | all unchanged |
| **Unchanged sites whose pin nets changed** | **82**: 25 on JL, 16 on JR, 41 on K (50 JL-K and 32 JR-K header ends) |
| Signals that move to another header | 322 |
| Reference designators | shift with the order: for example J900051 now names the header at (242, 170) instead of (140, 198), and J900063 the one at (293, 86) instead of (327, 86) |

**Why this blocks step 7.** The committed boards are routed drafts: JL has 26,678 segments and 2,664 vias, JR
35,597 and 2,779, K 86,502 and 7,310. The routed copper at those 82 header ends now carries the wrong nets.

Posing the headers by reference would move 41 JL, 30 JR and 196 K header footprints, up to 106 mm, with their
copper left behind. Leaving them put would leave 322 pin-net conflicts. Either way, step 7's pass condition (no
new short or clearance error, clean parity) cannot hold without stripping or re-routing every connection to those
82 headers. That exceeds the `--max-share 25` stripping budget for these connections, and it is a routing
decision, not a stripping one.

`place.py` on K also failed separately once the headers were posed: "placement overflow in E5: R4547: source
courtyard/origin violates region or obstacle; needed 4134.9 mm², available 4770.8 mm²". Its region fit also
needs a look after this is resolved.

## Options

- **(a) Stable header assignment (recommended).** Change the packer so that every existing header keeps its site,
  reference and pin nets:
  - nets that still cross keep their header and pin;
  - nets that no longer cross leave their pins NC, or AGND where the return rule needs it;
  - new crossing nets fill the 5 new headers first, then any freed pins on that pair's headers.

  Only the new headers and the freed or filled pins then touch routed copper, so step 7's strip budget applies as
  intended. The changes fall in `connector_packing35.py` (assignment), in reference numbering by site in the
  board-interface schematic generator, and in a test that an unchanged net keeps its header and pin.
- **(b) Accept the re-chunked assignment.** Strip every connection to the 82 reassigned headers and re-route them
  in the routing stage. That is a large routing change.
