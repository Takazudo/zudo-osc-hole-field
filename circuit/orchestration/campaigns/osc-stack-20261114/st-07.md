## What to deliver

Read the plan page first, then `boards/osc-core/osc-core.kicad_pcb` (K; confirm it against `design/partition/partition.json` and `circuit/routing/osc-pcb-current-checkpoint.json` before use), the schematic sources under `design/spec/modules/` and `design/spec/cells/` for the role of each trimmer, the evidence bundles `component-bourns-tc33x-2-203e`, `component-bourns-tc33x-2-503e` and `component-bourns-tc33x-2-504e`, and the pages `architecture/osc-block-osc.mdx`, `architecture/osc-standard-cells.mdx` and `verification/osc-release-gates.mdx`. `/mnt/project-files/stack-plan/trim.py` shows how the page's numbers were measured; it is a reference for the search only, not evidence and not code to retain as is. If `scripts/stack/geometry/` exists in the worktree at launch, its board reader may be imported; it must not be edited.

1. **Inventory.** Every trimmer on K by reference, footprint, face, position, net pair, module instance and role (for an oscillator: scale, offset, high-frequency trim, pulse width, and so on, as the source names them), read from the board and the schematic source. The two test pads likewise. State how the role was derived for each class.
2. **Node sensitivity.** Classify each adjusted node: summing node, exponential-converter or scale node, high-frequency compensation, reference, or insensitive DC. Give the reason from the circuit, the present loop length from the trimmer to the part it adjusts, and the limit you apply to a move (maximum added length, no layer change inside the loop, guard needs). Each limit is a proposal with its reasoning; mark it ASSUMED where no source sets it.
3. **Position search.** For each part, the nearest rear-face position that is free at courtyard level with the retained clearance rules, inside a stated window, after the planned removals (the retired headers and the 191 service apertures) and without using an area that another planned change claims (the 18 post holes and their neighbour moves, the SUB parts, interior fastener candidates as far as the sources record them). Report distance, added vias and whether the adjusted loop stays on one side. Sensitive nodes stay local: where the nearest free position breaks the limit of item 2, say so rather than widening the window.
4. **Mutual consistency.** The 44 moves and 2 pad moves must not collide with each other; the plan is one assignment, not 46 independent answers. State the order in which the positions were assigned and prove no two proposed courtyards overlap.
5. **Service access.** With rear service (Q8), each moved part must be reachable with a screwdriver from K's rear: tool envelope against neighbouring rear-face parts by retained heights, and legibility of a reference on the rear silkscreen. Report conflicts with dimensions.
6. **Fallback.** For each part with no acceptable position: a rear-adjust part with an access hole through K, or keeping the part on the front with an access aperture through the facing board, with what each needs (hole size, the facing board's free area at that XY, and the evidence need for the exact part). No part is selected.
7. **Calibration procedure impact.** Which calibration steps of the release gates change when the adjustment is made from the rear with the instrument closed at the front, and what a test fixture must provide. Listing only.

Write `design/stack/trimmers/README.md` (verdict headings and the reconciliation with the plan page), `design/stack/trimmers/relocation-plan.json` (one record per part), and under `scripts/stack/trimmers/` the generator, the checker and tests: fault injection for each defect class the checker claims to catch (an overlapping courtyard, a position outside the board, a position inside a reserved area, a sensitive-node limit exceeded, a missing part) and a staleness test bound to a canonical digest of the inputs the plan uses, so that a routing-only change of K does not make it stale.
