# MC04-S7 Part A0 report: function blocks on the current scene, and the input-buffer cases (a) and (b)

stopped Part A). Study folder: `scripts/stack/studies/mc04_s7_a0/` (README lists the commands and the result

## Summary

- **Tooling.** `capacity.py --current` and `blocks.py --current` now work on the committed link-gate scene. They
  read the unresolved list from the gate's displacement record, record the sha256 of every input, and refuse a
  stale input. `budgets.py` and `loops.py` take `--source NAME=PATH`. The pinned MC04-R1 history is untouched:
  the no-flag code paths are unchanged, the R1 tests pass, and the no-flag budget and loop outputs are
  byte-identical before and after the change. 40 study tests pass.
- **Function blocks.** All 20 moving blocks (K's 14, plus U1417, U2218, U2219, U4347, U4348 and U8318) pack on K
  with no exact-predicate violation. There is no raw-sensitive or forbidden crossing, and every LED net crosses
  one cable span. The stage 1b loop screen passes for every indicator unit (worst phase margin 68.8 degrees
  against 82.1 at baseline).
- **One draft-limit finding.** U4347's block, as the ruling assigns it (no keep-front), carries three
  `/E3/*_ISO_MID` nets onto the cable. Stage 1a passes them only with ground on both sides; unshielded, the
  crosstalk exceeds the 10 mV ANALOGUE limit. Putting U4347 on the same keep-front boundary as U1417 and U2218
  removes this (variant below), with nothing failing and no shielding needed.
- **Case (a).** None of the three input buffers has a legal local pose within 24 mm, at any 90-degree turn,
  even after the moved blocks have left their boards.
- **Case (b).** Holding JL-L02 at its retained slot resolves U2217 and U2219 in place. In exchange, U2119 (an
  F1 magnitude indicator) and its diode D2243 become unresolved. U1318 and U8314 are unaffected and still have
  no local pose within 24 mm.

### Variant: U4347 on the keep-front boundary

With `--move JR:K:U4347:_PROTECTED,ISO_MID`, all blocks still pack, with the same positions apart from U4347's
smaller block (8 members instead of 11). The three ISO_MID nets stay on JR. Instead, U4347's three op-amp

Stage 1b still passes for all four indicator ICs. JR then gets 17 nets newly crossing and 5 no longer crossing.

it with the indicators. **The manager should rule which boundary U4347 takes.** I recommend the keep-front
variant, because it is the same rule the ruling applies to the other general-output units.
