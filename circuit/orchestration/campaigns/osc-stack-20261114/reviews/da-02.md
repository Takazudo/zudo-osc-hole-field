## E1. Capture: the closing order stands; the pilot pins become locators and the pin tips are registered at soldering

**Decision.** DS-12 is **changed** in one row and confirmed otherwise.

1. The closing order of DS-12 stands: front boards in the panel with loose nuts, K drawn on by jack screws, nuts last. Step 2 changes as E8 states.
2. The pilot pins are locators for capture. Each of JL, JR and P carries two pins, mechanically fixed to the front board, at least 100 mm apart, entering one round and one slotted unplated hole in K. The cylindrical land is fully engaged at least 1 mm of travel before the first stacker tip reaches a socket face, so the pin stands at least gap + 1.6 + 4 mm above the front board's rear face including its taper. Radial play of the land in its hole is at most 0.06 mm with the hole at its tolerance limit. The pins stay in place after closing; they carry no load.
3. The stacker pin tips are registered by a drilled plate when the stackers are soldered (E6).
4. Engagement is not staged. No lead style offers a stagger: all 191 J and P pairs reach their sockets within 0.02 mm of travel.
5. Capture criterion on paper: the worst pair of the whole unit is within 0.39 mm along the row at the 95th percentile. ST-03 reports that row.
6. A pilot pin that will not enter with the nuts loose is a stop signal. The unit is not forced; the board without play is measured.

**Reasons (scratch, P unit of 135 pairs / JL of 29 pairs, median and 95th percentile of the worst pair per axis, mm).**

| Case | Board terms, mm | Pair terms, mm | P | JL | Share of boards within 0.39 mm (P) |

| ST-03 a2 | 0.100, 0.075, 0.075, rest offset | 0.100, 0.100, lean | 0.401 / 0.590 | 0.344 / 0.524 | 46% |
| Registered tips, pilots not locating | as a2 | 0.100, 0.075, 0.060 | 0.321 / 0.510 | 0.268 / 0.441 | 72% |
| Located pilots only | 0.075, 0.075, 0.060 | as a2 | 0.317 / 0.409 | 0.287 / 0.389 | 91% |

| Located pilots, lean 1° | 0.075, 0.075, 0.060 | 0.100, 0.100, 0.268 | 0.434 / 0.529 | n/a | 15% |

## E2. Fixing rule: 40 mm is confirmed, defined per side, with a computed criterion and a budget of 18 added sites

**Decision.** DS-15's rule is **confirmed in value and changed in definition**.

1. **K side.** Every pair to K lies within 40 mm of a fixing; a fixing of any front board counts.
2. **Front side.** A P pair lies within 40 mm of a P-to-K fixing. A JL or JR pair lies within 40 mm of a fixing on JL or JR; the two boards share the panel's plane.
3. **What counts.** The 18 posts, the 12 kept supports and each site that ST-08 declares with its coordinates. A clear candidate site is not a fixing until declared. EL collar posts and the adapters carry no fixing to K.
4. **Computed criterion.** For every pair the Z need (ST-03's chain on root sum of squares, plus `t`, plus the dish of E3) is inside the pair's open and closing margins. The 40 mm figure is the planning screen; the computed need is the criterion. A pair between 40 and 50 mm from its fixing is allowed only when its computed need passes; ST-08 lists each such pair with its numbers. No pair is beyond 50 mm.
5. **Budget.** At most 18 added sites: the 12 clear interior candidates that the cover uses (no part move), the 5 provisional sites (part moves on K and the front board) and one more for the two P pairs near (269.5, 172 to 192). ST-08 minimises part moves first and the count second. If more than 20 are needed, ST-08 returns NEEDS_DESIGN.
6. **When a pair cannot be covered.** A jack or a control never moves. ST-08 reports the pair with its distances and its computed need, and then tries, in this order: part moves on K or on the front board; a fixing carried on a P tab; the 40 to 50 mm exception of item 4. A pair still uncovered is returned as NEEDS_DESIGN with the dimension.

**The design basis for the count.** The basis is the count from declared fixings under this rule. Today that is 107 pairs with the 30 retained fixings and 67 with the five provisional sites: ST-03's figures. The two tasks differ for two reasons, both reproduced in scratch:

| Fixings | Any fixing on K (ST-02's method) | Same front board (ST-03's method) | This rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 30 (posts and kept supports) | 97 | 107 | 107 |
| 35 (with the 5 provisional sites) | 57 | 67 | 67 |
| 96 (30 and the 66 candidates) | 27 | 32 | 29 |
| 101 (all) | 0 | 5 | 2 |

- 107 against 97: ten P pairs lie within 40 mm of a J-board fixing but not of a P fixing. Such a fixing holds K there; it does not hold P.
- 67 against 0: ST-02 counted the 66 candidate sites as fixings; ST-03 did not have their coordinates.
- ST-02's 27 and 0 are a reachability bound: what the clear sites could give if every one were used. They are not the state of the design.

**Reasons.** At 40 mm on both boards the Z need is 0.435 mm with ±0.05 mm posts and 0.474 mm with ±0.15 mm posts, against open margins of 0.74 mm (P) and 0.72 mm (JL, JR at the E10 gap). At 50 mm it is 0.55 to 0.58 mm before the dish, and at 60 mm 0.70 to 0.72 mm: the rule cannot be relaxed to 60 mm once the dish is added. A tighter reach on P is not reachable from clear sites: at 35 mm twelve pairs stay uncovered after 18 sites, at 30 mm nineteen after 20.

**Alternatives considered.** A 15.5 to 23 mm rule: unreachable (DA-01 M5). 50 or 60 mm everywhere: 15 or 9 sites in place of 17, but the need with the dish exceeds the margin. A rule on K only: leaves P's own bow and dish unbounded. The shallow height set of DS-13 (±0.335 mm): would need a reach near 25 mm.

**Evidence status.** Coordinates: DERIVED from the candidate (ST-02 a2). Bow ratio: SOURCED as ST-03 cites; bow model ASSUMED. The greedy cover: DERIVED in scratch, not optimal and not checked for clearance at the five provisional sites or the eighteenth. Post tolerance ±0.15 mm: SOURCED (ST-01c). `t` = 0.2 mm: ASSUMED; coupon C-5.

**How to reverse.** The rule is a parameter of two checkers and of ST-08's brief; no source carries it yet.

## E3. Closing load: the fixing rule carries a load criterion; K is still mated in one step

**Decision.** **New**; it extends DS-15.

1. **Dish term.** The Z need of every pair includes the dish of its bay under the mating load, computed for a plate on point supports (w = 0.00581·q·a⁴/D for a square bay of side a, D = 6,984 N·mm for 1.6 mm FR4 at the ASSUMED 20 GPa), with q from the positions inside the bay at 0.97 N per position. The 1.33 N value is reported beside it. For a P pair the dish of K and of P both count. For a JL or JR pair only K's counts, because the panel backs the jack boards.
2. **Remedy where the need exceeds the margin.** Either one more fixing in that bay, or a pusher pad of the closing fixture on K's rear face within 15 mm of the bay centre, which removes K's share. ST-08 states which, per bay.
3. **Load per fixing.** At most 150 N on one jack screw at 1.33 N per position, counted from the positions nearer to it than to any other fixing.
4. **Order of the draw-down.** From the centre of each front board outward, in a fixed sequence. No two neighbouring screws differ by more than a quarter turn (0.125 mm on M3) when they are 32 mm or more apart, or by more than an eighth turn when closer. The limit comes from the 593 microstrain design limit: the allowed step between fixings is 0.198 mm at 40 mm, 0.111 mm at 30 mm and 0.049 mm at 20 mm (fixed-guided beam, a lower bound).
5. **One step.** K is mated in one step. A staged mate needs pin tips at two heights, which the lead styles do not give at these gaps.
6. **Separation.** K needs 365 to 761 N to separate. ST-08 gives the jack-out element; a post screw cannot push.

**Reasons.** With the densest bay that meets 40 mm, the dish is 0.14 mm per board at 0.97 N and 0.19 mm at 1.33 N. For a P pair at the 40 mm limit the need is then 0.474 + 0.28 = 0.75 mm against 0.74 mm at 0.97 N: P misses by 0.01 mm in its densest bay unless K is pressed there (0.61 mm) or a fixing is added. JL and JR need 0.539 + 0.14 = 0.68 mm against 0.72 mm. The rule alone therefore does not close P; the load criterion does.

**Alternatives considered.** A press platen that closes K in one stroke: K's rear face is populated (39% free at courtyard level), so a full platen needs a relieved tool; kept as an option for ST-12. Mating K to P first on the bench and then to the jack boards: P would hang on its pairs and the P unit could not then enter the panel freely.

**Evidence status.** Force per position: SOURCED for the double-row pair, ASSUMED for the single row (C-1). Plate model, modulus and Poisson ratio: ASSUMED; coupon C-9 measures an 80 × 80 mm bay. Residual dish after the screws seat: UNSOURCED; taken as the full figure.

**How to reverse.** Parameters of ST-03's calculator and ST-08's brief.

**For information.** The draw-down is about 23 quarter turns on about 48 screws, near 1,100 screw operations per closing. ST-08 states the time and whether a geared or multi-spindle aid is worth a fixture.

## E6. Supply form and sides: the sides stay; the K-joint stackers are soldered in a second operation on a registration plate

**Decision.** DS-13 is **confirmed** for sides and lead styles and **changed** in its placement assumption.

1. The socket stays on the front board and the stacker on K. Lead styles −03, −06 and −07 stay; no other set gives the gaps.
2. The working assumption is no longer machine placement of the stackers. All 197 K-joint stackers are consigned parts soldered to K in a second operation, after K's machine assembly, with their pin tips held in a drilled registration plate that sits on gauge spacers at the stack heights. The plate is drilled like the socket pattern of the front boards in one drill programme.
3. The sockets are machine-placed with their pads; all three codes are listed in the channel.
4. The EL stacker (double row, with pad, listed) and the CLT socket stay machine-placed. The stacker's extra manufacturing step at its lowest height is accepted.
5. The coupon carries both variants: unregistered stackers (as DS-16 planned) and plate-registered stackers.

**Reasons.** The documents do not support machine placement for 185 of 197 pairs (C12). Swapping sides does not change that: the same stackers are needed, on a board that is equally machine-assembled. Hand soldering 777 gull-wing tails at 2.00 mm pitch replaces 1,822 crimps of the cable design. The plate also removes the stacker's float and lean from the pair terms, which is what E1 needs.

**Alternatives considered.** Swap the halves: no supply gain; the socket's flux cap is wanted on the hand-soldered front boards. Machine placement by nozzle on the body: unsupported by the documents; kept as a coupon variant and as a question to the assembler (owner item 2). A stacker with an alignment peg: a special part number that needs a manufacturer request (owner's matter).

**Evidence status.** Supply forms: SOURCED (prints; channel capture is distributor identity only). That a registration plate gives the E1 pair terms: ASSUMED; C-10. Who performs the second operation: an owner item.

**How to reverse.** Before footprints are drawn the choice costs nothing. The footprint is the same for both placements.

## E7. SUB divider clock: SAW_LOGIC from the free comparator half

**Decision.** **New.** The divider is clocked from `SAW_LOGIC`, the squared sawtooth from half B of `PULSE_CMP`, as ST-06 proposes.

**What it costs.** Per SUB oscillator on K: 17 parts and 299.0 mm² of courtyard, against 11 parts and 197.6 mm² for the pulse clock; one SN74HC14DR more. On +5 V: 1.625 mA typical and 3.594 mA planning per oscillator, 7.188 mA planning for O2 and O4 together, against 0.9 and 2.0 mA per oscillator for the pulse clock. Nothing on ±12 V. Nothing on the joint: the pair stays 1×3 with two contact lines and AGND, and no audio crosses it.

**Reasons.** The sub-octave must not stop when the pulse width or its modulation reaches 0% or 100%, and under sync its count must not depend on the pulse width. The pulse clock fails both, and its edge polarity is not retained. The added load on `SAW_RAW` is one comparator input beside the one already there; the hysteresis feeds the threshold node, not the sawtooth.

**Working assumption and closing check.** The sawtooth swing straddles 2.38 to 2.62 V with margin at every pitch and under hard sync: ASSUMED. It is closed by the AS3340 evidence need in `spec.json` and by ST-11's comparator deck. If it fails, the threshold divider is re-centred; if that fails, the pulse clock is the fallback.

**How to reverse.** Remove one package and four resistors per oscillator and move one net (ST-06's comparison netlist).
