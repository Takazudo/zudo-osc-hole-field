## Findings to resolve before acceptance

1. **HIGH: the stop condition's scope needs a ruling.** Revision 1 says "a newly displaced part is an input-buffer IC
   or a part on a `*_FB`, `*_SUM` or `*_SENSE` node". Five new gate rows match that text:
   - R5249 on `/W1/FOLDER_SUM` (also B→F);
   - R5256 on `/W1/F2_SUM`, which moves 7.25 mm;
   - R8221 on `/M5B/Ca92eeb_W_SENSE`;
   - R8331 on `/M4A/C38051c_W_SENSE`;
   - R8356 on `/M4A/C24ce01_W_SENSE`.

   `overlay.py` `buffer_stop()` checks nets only when the part or its owner is in `buffer_ics` (the DS-146 reading).
   That check is also inconsistent: R8222 on the same net would trip it, while R8221 (owner None) does not. The report's
   claim that no newly displaced part sits on an FB, SUM or SENSE node is false as written. **The design authority must
   rule whether the stop applies literally, in which case S10 stops, or under the DS-146 scope.** Either way, the report
   is corrected and the owner test in `buffer_stop` is fixed.
2. **MEDIUM: the face-change list is wrong in two places.** It misses C4554 (U4537's decoupler, B→F in the overlay). It
   also lists C5212, which was already on F at `6e5716e7`. R4238's 3.55 mm move on B appears only inside the overlay block.
3. **LOW:** R5257 is 7.934 mm, equal to its unchanged paper distance. It meets the 7.93 mm limit only after rounding;
   the record should say the limit is that paper distance.
4. **LOW:** the `locality-acceptances.json` recompute reads poses from the same file, so it is partly circular. No
   figure got worse.

Also recorded as a Gate A item: U2313's decoupler C2339 moves from 10.64 to 12.14 mm (a knock-on row, accepted as
