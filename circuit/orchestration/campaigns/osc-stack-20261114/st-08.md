## What to deliver

Read the design review record first (sections D2 and D4 and finding M7), then the geometry candidate and its README, ST-03's mechanics (the disposition of the 87 supports, the forces, the dish estimate and the closing order), ST-01b's post survey, ST-04's post drawing if the launch commit holds it, `design/partition/partition.json` (`supports`, `mechanical_datum_table`), and the board files for the footprints around each site. Cite each limit from the source that defines it, not from this brief. The design authority's row says this task writes `design/stack/fixings/` only; as for every stack task its generator, checker and tests live in `scripts/stack/fixings/`, and nothing else is written.

1. **Fixing inventory.** The 18 power posts, the 12 supports that ST-03 keeps as tie posts and the clear candidate sites of the geometry candidate, with coordinates, the two boards each one ties, and what each is today.
2. **Tie-site selection.** The smallest set of added tie-post sites that puts every pair within 40 mm of a fixing on its two boards, starting from the five provisional sites of D4. A site counts only where it can tie K to the facing front board. Give the coverage before and after, the worst distance per board, and the method (state it; the count must be minimal for the stated candidate set, or say by how much it may exceed the minimum).
3. **Part moves.** For each added site and each post whose hole grows: every footprint inside its keep-out on K and on the front board, by reference, with its nearest free position at courtyard level. The keep-out is the hole, the head or washer envelope and the ceramic rule of item 7.
4. **Adapter standoffs.** Two mechanically fixed standoffs per adapter (O1, O3, O5) at the 12.8 mm gap, chosen from the candidates of the geometry candidate, with the part moves on K and on the adapter.
5. **Pilot pins.** Two tapered pilot pins per front board (JL, JR, P) for coarse entry only: position, diameter, taper, the clearance hole in K, and the proof that they are not locators at the tolerances of ST-03.
6. **Jack screws.** The jack-screw element and where it acts (posts and tie posts double as jack-screw points): thread, engagement length, the load per point for mating at 1.33 N per position and for unmating at 0.98 N per position, the number of points per board, the drawing-down sequence, and K's deflection and strain between points against the 593 microstrain limit, with the model labelled ASSUMED. State the interface this assumes of the ST-04 post (thread, length, clamped faces) so that the two can be reconciled at the gate.
7. **Ceramics near every site.** Every ceramic capacitor within 6 mm of a post, tie post or jack-screw site, by reference with its family and case size; flag every part of 0805 size or larger within 5 mm; for each X5R and X7R family found, the bending specification from its evidence bundle, or the evidence need by exact part where no bundle holds it.
8. **Closing fixture.** What the fixture must provide for the six-step close of DS-12: support of the panel face down clear of the hardware, the platen for the P unit and EL, access for the jack screws from K's rear, and how the same fixture serves a rear-service refit. Requirements with dimensions from the sources; no manufacturing drawing.
9. **Exchange with ST-07.** If `design/stack/trimmers/relocation-plan.json` exists at launch, treat the areas it claims on K's rear face as reserved and report every conflict with dimensions. Write the sites of this plan with their keep-outs to `design/stack/fixings/fixing-sites.json`, so that ST-07 can read them. Neither task edits the other's folder.

Write `design/stack/fixings/README.md` (verdict headings, one table per item), `design/stack/fixings/fixing-sites.json` (every site with coordinates, boards, role, keep-out, part moves and status), and under `scripts/stack/fixings/` the generator, the checker and tests: fault injection for each defect class the checker claims to catch (a pair beyond 40 mm, two overlapping keep-outs, a site on an occupied position with no listed move, a ceramic of 0805 size or larger inside 5 mm, a site inside an area ST-07 claims) and a staleness test bound to the SHA256 of the geometry candidate.
