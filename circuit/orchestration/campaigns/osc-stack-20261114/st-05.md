## What to deliver

Read the plan page first, then `doc/src/content/docs/architecture/osc-rail-ledger.mdx` with `design/power/rail-ledger.json` and `design/power/rail-allocation-input.json`, `architecture/osc-power.mdx`, `architecture/osc-board-stack.mdx`, `verification/osc-release-gates.mdx` (E19: indoor, non-condensing, 0 to 40 °C ambient), `design/partition/partition.json` (`boards`, `enclosure`, `panel`, the footprint-to-board assignment), `design/reports/current/`, and the evidence bundles under `.claude/skills/component-*/` for every part class the screen names. Issue #64 may be read; nothing may be written to GitHub.

1. **Dissipation per board.** Assign every ledger load row and IC package to the board that carries it in the current partition, for the normal planning load and for the continuous contract (1700/1600/300 mA on +12/−12/+5 V). State what the ledger does not cover (output loads that dissipate outside the instrument, the supply, indicator LEDs by state) and how each is treated. The board totals must sum to the instrument totals, with the residual shown.
2. **Enclosure comparison.** Outer surface area of the 34 mm and the 120 mm case from the recorded internal XY and a stated wall allowance; a natural-convection and radiation estimate of the average surface and internal air rise for both, with the coefficient range and its source or its status as an assumption; the sensitivity to orientation (panel vertical, panel horizontal facing up), to a closed against a vented case, and to a supply inside the stack volume against outside (question Q6 of the plan page).
3. **Stack interior.** The air gaps of the proposed stack (panel to the front boards, front boards to K at the three mated heights, K to the rear wall) and what they imply for convection between facing boards; which boards face which, with the dissipation density (W per cm²) of each facing pair. No claim about airflow beyond a stated bound.
4. **Hot parts.** The ten parts or part classes with the highest dissipation or the highest dissipation density, each with its retained thermal resistance and temperature limit where an evidence bundle holds them, and an estimated case or junction temperature at 40 °C ambient plus the internal air rise. Where a bundle holds no thermal figure, report the evidence need by exact part; do not take a value from memory or from a similar part.
5. **Temperature-sensitive functions.** The oscillator cores, the exponential converters and their tempco parts, the references and the sample-and-hold stages: where they sit relative to the hot parts in the proposed stack, and which gradients the thinner case makes larger or smaller. Pitch calibration scope stays 25 °C; this item names risks, it does not predict drift.
6. **Options.** Vent slots, a metal rear wall used as a spreader, conduction through the 18 power posts and the supports, a larger case depth, and a reduced load: for each, what it would change in the screen, what it conflicts with (the 191 retired service apertures, the frame, the supports, EMC and dust), and what evidence it needs. Options only; none is selected.
7. **Acceptance criteria proposal for #64.** Measurable limits and the measurement points a later physical test would use (ambient, soak time, operating state, sensor positions, pass limits per part class), marked as a proposal.

Write `design/stack/thermal/README.md` (verdict headings, one table per item), `design/stack/thermal/thermal-screen.json` (every term with value, unit, status and source) and a small calculator under `scripts/stack/thermal/` with unit tests that recompute every figure in the README from that JSON.
