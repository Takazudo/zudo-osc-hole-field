## Stop: the ruled poses land on GH headers that still exist on the boards

**What I checked.** Before applying anything, I checked the 13 ruled poses against the footprints at their current
board positions. The S8 studies (Parts A to A4) all use the link gate's re-placement model (`links_gate_part2.Field`).
That model does two things that do not hold on the interim boards:
1. It drops the GH headers that the stack links replace (`links.Model.dropped`). In the stack these headers are
   retired, so their area counts as free.
2. It counts the parts the gate re-placed at their paper positions. The boards still carry them at their old
   positions.

**What I found.** The ruled poses would be applied to the interim GH boards from stage 7, where every GH header is
still in place. Applied there, they overlap:

| What they overlap | Count | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GH headers on the same face | 49 | 17 headers: K J900078, J900080, J900088, J900090, J900102, J900104, J900106, J900108, J900153, J900159, J900233, J900235, J900331, J900347; JL J900005; JR J900083, J900085 |
| Gate-displaced parts at their board positions | 3 | C4237 on U4221, U4526 on U4534, R2381 on U2306 |
| Through-hole parts on the other face | 11 | jack bodies; the model allows these, as the floorplan does |

**Which blocks are affected.** 12 of the 13 ruled IC blocks overlap a GH header: U1318, U1415, U4129, U4433, U4502
(with U4647), U4524, U4527, U4635, U4647, U5110 and U5209.

**Why this stops Part B.** These would be courtyard and pad conflicts with real parts, so Part B could not meet "DRC
with no new error-severity items".

**Why A5 is held too.** A5 uses the same model, so its result would carry the same conflict.

**The reason underneath.** The 15 ICs are "open" only in the link-era model: they sit under link slots, tails, fields
and latch margins that do not exist on the interim GH boards. On those boards, each IC is at a legal, routed-for
position today. Moving them onto the GH header area is what makes the conflict.

**Evidence:** `scripts/stack/studies/mc04_s8_replacement/board_conflicts.py` (`--check`) writes
`board-conflicts.json`, which lists every overlap with its board, face and kind.

## Options for the ruling

   - Record the ruled poses, and the A5 result once run, in the link gate's re-placement record and in
     `gate-a-open-items.json` as resolved for the link era.
   - Change no interim board. Gate A applies them when the links replace the GH headers.
   - The interim re-route (Part C) then proceeds on the current boards. Nothing it routes around these ICs is thrown
     away by stage 8, because stage 8 changes nothing on the interim boards.

   at their board positions. That is a different model, and under it these ICs have no reason to move. I do not
   recommend it.
3. **(c) Keep both models.** Run A5 and record link-era poses as in (a). In addition, re-search the 13 against the
   interim boards (GH headers kept) only if the owner wants the interim layout to match the link-era layout ahead of
   Gate A.
