## New finding: four recorded poses fail with block members on their true face

**What is wrong.** The S8 searches (Parts A to A5) put every block member on the block's own face. Four recorded
blocks have cell-local passives on the other face:
- U1415: R1498, R1499
- U5110: R5156–R5160, R5163
- U5209: R5246, R5250
- U5210: R5256, R5261

When those members go on their true face at the recorded pose, every one of the four blocks meets an obstacle:

| Block | Obstacle |
| --- | --- |
| U1415 | the K field of P-K-P-L08 |
| U5110 | C5139, U5111, Q5101, R5138, R2331 |
| U5209 | D5207, and the re-placed R2368 |
| U5210 | U4223, and the K field of P-K-P-L13.01.01.01 |

The open U8409 block also has other-face members (C8403, R8412–R8414). This explains the one in-place overlap that
`board_conflicts.py` now reports: U8412 on R8413.

**Check:** `scripts/stack/studies/mc04_s8_replacement/member_faces.py` (`--check`), writing `member-faces.json`, with
`test_member_faces.py`.

**How it is recorded:**
- Each placed row in `link-era-replacements.json` carries a `member_face_check`.
- `gate-a-open-items.json` now reads: 10 RESOLVED FOR THE LINK ERA, 4 NEEDS RE-SEARCH (U1415, U5110, U5209, U5210), and
  1 OPEN (U8409).

**What fixes it.** The search has to carry each member's own face: kept when the block stays on its face, flipped
when the block flips. Then the four blocks, and the U5210/U8409 neighbourhood, need searching again. This changes
ruled poses, so I have not done it without a ruling.
