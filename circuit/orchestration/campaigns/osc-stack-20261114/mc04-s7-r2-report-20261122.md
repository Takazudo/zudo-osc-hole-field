## Stop

Step 3 asks for the move to be expressed as a region assignment, and to stop if it needs changes to module cell
definitions beyond that. I evaluated the io-partition checker (`scripts/checks/io_partition60.build`) with every
moved block member assigned to the core region (K). The probe is `partition_probe.py`, and its output is
`partition-probe.json`. Region assignment is enough for the three general-output blocks, but not for the
indicator blocks:

1. **22 indicator LED bridge nets become forbidden crossings.** All 22 are `*_LED_A` and `*_LED_K` nets, with the
   reason "no supported buffered/conditioned crossing source":
   - U2119: 8 nets;
   - U8318: 8 nets;
   - U4348: 4 nets;
   - U4347 unit 4: 2 nets.

   These nets have no op-amp output on them, because the op-amp drives them through a diode. The checker's
   crossing rule accepts no other source, so the stage 4 one-span evidence has no way into the partition today.
   Admitting it needs a new, reviewed crossing kind in `io_partition60.crossing_kind`.
2. **The filter cell's own island splits.** `F1/FILTER_LEDS:F1` is set in `design/spec/modules/filter.py`, and
   `test_filter.py` asserts it. It holds U2119, its 16 bridge diodes, RB2114–RB2117 and the panel LEDs
   D601–D604. The checker forbids an island that straddles boards. Moving U2119 means changing that cell's island
   definition: either the LEDs get their own island, or the island rule is relaxed for this case.

The other new errors are expected or mechanical:
- 73 source-versus-assigned region mismatches. These disappear once the source assigns the region.
- Five region-derived `IO_JACK:<instance>` island splits (E3, F1, F2, M4A, O4). The partition source renames these
  islands when it assigns the region.

The 10 general-output `*_DRIVE` nets of U1417, U2218 and U4347 become allowed crossings, as buffered signals.

Two further points for the source change:
- The relocation must be **instance-scoped**. `filter` is one family shared by F1, F2 and F3, so a family-level
  region change would also move F1's and F3's counterparts of U2218 and F2's counterpart of U2119. The existing
  TSSOP retention already clones families per instance, and the relocation can use the same mechanism.
- Everything else in step 2 passes (below).

**Options for the manager:**
- **(i)** Rule a new crossing kind for indicator LED bridge nets. It would be limited to one cable span, based on
  MC04-S4 case 2 and the stage 1b screen. Also rule that the moved indicator parts leave the `FILTER_LEDS` island,
  with the panel LEDs keeping it. Both are reviewed source and rule changes, with tests.
- **(ii)** Move only the general-output blocks (U1417, U2218 and U4347 keep-front) in this stage. The indicators
  U2119, U4348 and U8318 stay unresolved for now.
- **(iii)** Another remedy for the indicators.

I recommend (i) if the manager accepts the one-span evidence as partition-level acceptance. Otherwise (ii) lands
the part that needs no new rule.
