### Class B (IN+, `W_SENSE`) rows

- **Why they fail.** They fail only where the IN- node sits at its own 50 pF pair value. With IN- at 0 pF they
  reach 32 pF (wiper 0.5) and 50 pF (wiper 1.0).
- **K5 is clipping, not oscillation.** In 56 class B records the 10 V-peak sine output clips at the rails, swinging
  beyond ±11 V and up to ±11.97 V at the pair corner. These are all 40 IN+ records at wiper 1.0 with IN- at 50 pF,
  and 16 IN- records at high stray. The K5 "failures" among them, 50 records with a harmonic-fit residual up to
  2.43 V, come from the clipping. The brief requires such an amplitude to be reduced until the output stays 1 V
  inside the rails, with the value recorded. That was not done.
- **Effect.** No label rests on these K5 readings: the same corners fail K3 and K4 at zero stray. `budget.json`
  still lists K5 among the recorded `deciding` criteria for those corners, until the design authority rules on
  clipping.

### Class B (IN-, `SUM`) rows

All eight are C_lim 2 pF, with K7 failing from 6 pF. Their paper estimates are 5.93 to 13.85 pF.

### Class C rows are NOT EVALUATED: the wiper 1.0 K5 window lies on the cell's own RC tail

- **The defect.** At wiper 1.0 the zero-stray time constant is 10 µs, and each K5 hold is 140 µs. The study's late
  window starts at half the hold, 7 time constants after a 10 V step, where the RC tail is still 9.63 mV. The
  quadratic detrend cannot remove an exponential and leaves 3.67 to 3.72 mV. That is the recorded K5 figure, and it
  is not an oscillation.
- **The reviewer's recomputation.** From the cached waveforms, removing one 10 µs exponential leaves 9.8 µV (these
  are reviewer figures, not study results). At wiper 0.5 the window starts 13.6 time constants after the edge, so
  the two corners were not measured alike. The brief's reviewer check 14 requires that no class C row fail on the
  cell's own filter time.
- **The relabel.** K5 at wiper 1.0 is withdrawn, and all ten rows are
  `NOT EVALUATED: K5 late window overlaps the cell's RC settling tail at wiper 1.0`. Each wiper 1.0 corner in
  `budget.json` carries `k5_withdrawn`. No row is promoted.
- **What still stands before any class C limit.** The K6 and K8 tie question (below), the Ruling 6 output pair
  (NOT RUN) and the `IDENTITY OPEN` part on each net.
- **What the reading made obsolete.** The 48 class C full-list points (96 decks) ran only because of the K5
  reading at wiper 1.0.

### Class C at 100 pF passes K6 and K8 by less than the recorded resolution

At wiper 0.5 and 100 pF, the time constant rises by 0.999 % and the 1 mV settling time by up to 0.998 %. The limit
is 1 %, and the recorded `resolution_fraction` is 0.0005 (0.05 %). The brief expected this point to sit at the limit
and ruled that "a tie counts as a fail". Whether a pass by 0.001 %, 50 times smaller than the resolution, is a tie
is a question for the design authority. Until it is answered, no class C corner limit above 40 pF is quoted as a
result.

### The class D row is non-monotonic

`/W1/C629978_W_SENSE` at wiper 0.5 fails K4 at 0 and 1 pF, passes from 2 to 64 pF and fails K7 from 80 pF. At
wiper 1.0 it fails K4 from 0 to 8 pF and passes from 10 to 100 pF. The label follows the lowest fail. Both corners
carry `non_monotonic` in `budget.json`.

## Class G is NOT EVALUATED: no run point had an accepted operating point

18 of the 80 class G points ran: iabc low, trim 0, at 0 to 64 pF. All 36 of their decks are NOT EVALUATED with no
metric. The row label gives both reasons: the sweep is incomplete, and no run point had an accepted operating point.

- **The seed group is valid.** Its two members agree, `V(out)` is −24.79 mV, and the rails test passes.
- **The tightest loop step of the 18 recorded points.**

  | Outcome | Points |
  | --- | --- |
  | 600 s timeout | 7 (5, 6, 12, 20, 24, 32 and 40 pF) |
  | no convergence | 5 |
  | "Transient op" fallback (no metric under C2.7) | 6 |

- **The later steps.** At reltol 1e-5 and the default step, and in both closed-loop steps, the operating point
  misses the 50 µV acceptance.
- **A diagnostic, not a study result.** In deck `921b6372…` the miss is on one node only. That node is `ota_nc8`,
  the unconnected LM13700 pin 8 node, which has no cell element on it. It sits 1.706 mV from the seed at both
  operating points, and every other node is within 1 µV. These figures come from rejected operating points, which
  B2.6 keeps out of every output. Whether a node with no cell element should be held to the 50 µV test is left to
  the design authority.
