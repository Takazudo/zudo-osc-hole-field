## Third run stopped at Arm 0 P6b (STOP 2): one H-REF9 corner's seed is not the DC point its decks reach

P6b passed on three of the four H-REF9 corners. It missed at TMUX output capacitance 100 pF with the Ruling 6 bracket
to AGND.

At that corner the seeds are valid under B2.3. The seeded loop deck and the seeded closed-loop deck at 0 pF then
reach an operating point that differs from the seed reference by more than 50 µV, at every ladder step: three
tolerance steps for the loop deck, trap and gear for the closed-loop deck. None of those decks fell back to
"Transient op". Under B3 a miss is a stop. B2.2 allows the DC ramp only when the first seed fails its validity test,
and this seed passed. So no remedy was open, and nothing ran after P6b.

The seed values point to the cause. No figure is taken from a rejected operating point (B2.6).

| H-REF9 seed (loop deck) | Seed obtained by | `V(out)` | IN- |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TMUX 0, bracket to AGND | `op`, transient-op fallback | 9.0000398 V | 5.0000210 V |
| TMUX 100 pF, bracket to AGND | `op`, transient-op fallback | 9.0001636 V | 5.0000338 V |
| TMUX 0 and TMUX 100 pF, bracket open | `op`, no fallback | 9.0000397 V | 5.0000214 V |

Both bracket-to-AGND seeds came from ngspice's transient-op fallback. The two differ by 124 µV at the output,
although only a capacitor differs between them, and a capacitor cannot change a DC solution. A likely reading,
not tested: the fallback stops integrating when the circuit is close to settled, and with 100 pF more on the output
it stops further from the final value. The seed then passes B2.3 (within 10 mV of 9.000 V, inputs within 1 mV), but it is not the DC point,
and the evaluation decks, started from it as `.nodeset`, solve to the DC point.

The two closed-loop seeds at TMUX 0 differ from their loop seeds by 8 µV for the same reason (9.0000319 V against
9.0000398 V). That stays inside 50 µV, and that corner passed.

## Addendum B was implemented as written; one deck-builder defect was found and fixed first

- **B2: seeds.** For each DC-distinct deck, `runner.run_seed()` runs the evaluation deck's netlist at `C_stray` = 0
  with `op`, `wrdata … all` at `set numdgt=15` (the seed reference) and `wrnodev`. Its `.ic` lines are written
  inline as `.nodeset` lines in every loop and closed-loop deck of that netlist, so they are part of the deck key.
  The seed record carries the nodeset's SHA-256.
- **B2: the ramp.** It is the second method only: a `dc` sweep of the largest non-zero DC source, 0 to nominal in
  20 steps, used only after the first seed fails B2.3.
- **B2.3: validity.** Inputs within 1 mV (not for class G); output at least 1 V inside each rail; and OUT and IN-
  within 10 mV of a value derived from the specification netlist. The derivation uses an ideal amplifier, opens the
  capacitors and leaves out any node without a resistive DC path (for REF9, 9.0000002 V and 5.000 V).
- **B2.5: acceptance.** Every operating point is read at full precision: both in a loop deck, the initial one in a
  closed-loop deck. It must be within 50 µV of the seed reference on every external node. The 0.1 mV agreement
  between a loop deck's two operating points is kept. A miss continues the ladder, and no figure from a rejected
  operating point is recorded.
- **B2.7: seed cap.** At most 180 seeds; reaching it ends a slice as a cap.
- **B4: P7.** `p7_pass()` requires an evaluated K1 failure, and for `/W1/DRIVE_INV_SUM` an evaluated K4 failure. The
  former `res.get("K4_fails", True)` is gone.
- **M12 to M16.** M12 is `budget.py --m12` plus a test that reads its record. M13 to M16 are tests on in-memory
  copies. Unit tests: 30 run, 29 pass, 1 skip (M12).
- **Defect fixed before any recorded deck.** `cells.netlist_lines()` wrote the amplifier as `XU inp …` for every
  cell. Where the specification ties IN+ to AGND, IN+ was left floating. This affected 4 E-control cells, 3 E-audio
  cells and the class G cell (and class I, which is blocked).
  The deck now uses the specification node (`XU 0 …`), and a test checks it. It is a correction of the study's own
  deck builder to match the specification, not a topology change. It affects the second run's `/W1/DRIVE_INV_SUM`
  P7 result, which is not reused.

## Decisions needed from the design authority before S13 can continue

1. **Decide how a seed that came from the transient-op fallback is treated.** These options are reasoned from this
   run's seed records; none was run:
   - Take the seed as a two-pass seed: run `op` again with the first seed as `.nodeset`, and use that second
     solution as the seed reference when it does not need the fallback.
   - Count "Transient op" in a seed deck as a failed validity test, which would open the DC ramp of B2.2.
   - Widen the 50 µV acceptance. That would weaken B2.5 and is not recommended.

   Any of these is a brief change for review. P6b, and through it the whole H-REF9 class, cannot pass as written.
2. **Weigh the risk for other classes.** Any cell whose zero-stray `op` needs the fallback can miss the same way.
   The smoke tests found clean seeds on DRIVE_INV_SUM, C24ce01_SUM and the class G cell. How many seeds need the
   fallback is not known before the sweep runs.
3. **Still open:** MC04-RM1 (it does not gate S13), and the BAS16GW-QX and BCM847BS,115 vendor-model downloads.
   Classes F and I stay blocked until then.

1 nF capacitor does not change the DC solution, so the zero-stray H-REF9 decks probably depend on transient op too.
That is not shown, because no zero-stray deck ran. These are file contents, not a diagnosis.
