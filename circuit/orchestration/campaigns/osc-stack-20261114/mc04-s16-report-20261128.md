## The brief's own rules produce the four differences

### M1: R2 is not exempt at the record slot

The brief exempts the record slot from R4 to R6 only. R2, the retained `slot_moves.Arm1` accept, rejects
L03.01's own record geometry. The member sites are within class distance as follows:

| Site | Distance (mm) | Limit (mm) | Within |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| K-P-16 | 26.1369 | 55 | yes |
| K-P-18 | 16.2421 | 40 | yes |
| K-P-20 | 31.1193 | 55 | yes |
| P-K-E1 | 35.988 | 45 | yes |
| P-K-E2 | 41.4062 | 45 | yes |
| P-K-E3 | 52.075 | 45 | **no** |

The gate builds a link's members by cluster join radius, not by rider class distance, so its record is not
bound by this check.

### M9, M10 and M12: the three sites need 14 circuits

These three cases use "the L13.01.01.01 block", meaning the three sites K-P-19, K-P-39 and K-P-57 at 8 circuits.
Those sites carry four LOGIC and two ANALOGUE lines. Under Ruling P7 the four logic lines need one extra ground,
so the block takes 9 positions. That is more than 8, so the block is 14-way.

The recorded link fits in 8 circuits only because `/E1/STAGE_FALL` of K-P-19 rode out of it. That is the divided
site of DS-121, exclusion c of Ruling P1.

C5 itself is unaffected and passed. The census runs only R1 to R6, so the missing S2 order never enters it.

### The study did not resolve these differences

STOP 7 allows a reading only when it cannot change the verdict. Here the stricter reading is the literal one, and
that reading is what fires STOP 3. Two other readings would each make the vectors match the brief:

- exempt the record slot from R2 as well;
- build the C5 block from the link's recorded signals (`host_signals`, five lines) instead of the site subset.

The development dry run measured both, as observations only. Neither was applied. The design authority decides
whether to revise the vectors or the definitions.
