## Method (Revision 1 item 2)

- **Board state:** I placed the eight poses ruled in item 1 first, with their displaced passives, at the positions in
  Part A's `result.json`.
- **Search:** blocks as in Part A (not shrunk); turns of 0, 90, 180 and 270° on both faces, with the other face
  mirrored; the same grid and the same obstacles.
- **Widened displacement:** any SMD R, C or D up to 12 mm² that is not a panel part, not a decoupler, and not already
  moved by a ruled pose. Each displaced part must find its own legal pose within 24 mm. Options are tried
  fewest-displaced first, and the check is conservative: a displaced part's old courtyard stays an obstacle.
- **Part A update:** I corrected U4647's function. Units 1–3 are general output stages, each through a 499 Ω
  isolation pair; unit 4 is the magnitude indicator driving LED D1801. Part A's `result.json` changed only in that
  text.

## Options for the ruling

1. **Add U4524 (class iii) to the Part B set.** It joins the eight already ruled.
2. **U1318:** 29.1 mm and 13.4 pF on its sense input. It could take a Revision 2 input-buffer acceptance like U5112
   and U4646.
3. **U4417 (logic):** 48.8 mm. The node risk is low (no timing or clock nodes), but eight nets each grow by about
   85 mm. It could take an interim-GH locality acceptance.
4. **U5209, U5210, U8409 and U4647** carry feedback or summing nodes with added runs of 37–144 mm. A locality
   acceptance alone would not cover them. I see three ways out, each needing its own ruling:
   - make room locally by moving non-evictable parts (ICs or decouplers) under their own block rules;
   - move one link slot, which this stage's rules exclude;
   - split the package so only the affected units move.
