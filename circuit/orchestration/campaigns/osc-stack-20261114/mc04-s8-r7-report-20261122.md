## What changed

**Face-aware search.** The flag `Study.face_aware` makes each block member keep its face relative to its IC and flip
with the block. Every check, placement and joint-search rectangle uses the member's own face. With the flag off, the
historical Parts A to A5 reproduce byte for byte.

`test_face_aware.py` uses a synthetic mixed-face block. An obstacle that only the other-face member hits rejects the
pose. The members flip with the block, and the flipped block puts the member on the free face.

**The member check found a second defect.** Applying the 10 confirmed poses and re-checking them face-aware
(`member_faces.py`) failed five Part A class (iii) poses: U4433, U4526, U4527, U4623 and U4635. Part A moved the
displaced passives before placing the block, so passives landed on the block's own pose. Examples: C4402 and R7424 on
U4433; C4551 on U4526. The A2 and later searches place the block first, so they are not affected.

Under the Revision 7 rule ("treat any that now fails the same way"), these five were re-searched with the other mixed
blocks. Five blocks pass the member check (U4129, U4524, U1318, U4417, U4647), plus the U4502 block that moves with
U4647. Those six go first.
