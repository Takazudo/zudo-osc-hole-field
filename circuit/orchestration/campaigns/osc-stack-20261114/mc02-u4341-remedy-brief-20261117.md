## Required preservation and precise missing decisions

Q4304, R4379=22 kΩ, Q4305/Q4306 and R4380=22 kΩ retain the fall current-converter handoff to U4307. Empty MPN fields on these numeric requirements remain unresolved; no substitute is chosen.

The storage node has exactly six source contacts: R4395 pin2, R4396 pin2, C4313 pin1, U4341 pin10, R4397 pin2 and R4398 pin2. `test_envelope.py` requires this sensitive node to remain within ENV_CORE with no panel path. C4313 is the exact retained 1206CG104J500NT 100 nF C0G requirement. The two OTA-current sums and hold/idle clamp resistors are100 Ω. U4308 switching and its RB4335/RB4336/D4334–D4337 control/protection associations must not be omitted merely because they are not directly on ENV_STORAGE.

Ideal behavioural storage limits and ±3% rise/fall timing checks exclude actual device leakage/offset/spread. They do not supply a physical storage-droop allowance. The design reviewer must define admissible added feedback R/C, actual converter timing/error/stability conditions, storage leakage/droop and injection/contamination limits, and whole-package treatment for the shared OTA/clamp units. No trace/via or physical-leakage budget is invented here. See [precision constraints](mc02-precision-constraints-20261117.md).
