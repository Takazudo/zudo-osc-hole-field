## What to deliver

Read the design review record first (section D5 and the owner items), then ST-03's coupon frame and mechanics, ST-01b's connector evidence, the geometry candidate for the real pair counts, gaps and pitch between neighbouring pairs, and `circuit/WORKFLOW.md` for the evidence vocabulary. Cite each number from the source that defines it, not from this brief.

1. **Purpose and decisions served.** Which terms only the coupon closes (D5's table) and what each measurement decides, including the two negative outcomes: C-4 passing only at 0.1 mm or less, and the owner declining the build.
2. **Boards.** Each coupon board as a specification: role (K side, front-board side, the 0.4 mm EL side), outline size, thickness, copper layers and finish as the real boards have them (from the sources; state where a source is silent), and the gaps of 12.8, 17.0 and 18.8 mm set by posts of the ST-04 class. At least 20 pairs per board. One tie-post bay of 80 × 80 mm with pairs at its centre, closed by jack screws.
3. **Variants.** Imposed offsets along the row and across it, separately, at 0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3 and 0.4 mm; two stacker heights per gap ("A" by the D3 rule, and "A" at 4.32 mm); position counts 1×3, 1×5 and 1×6; the CLT with double-row TW-SM pair on the 0.4 mm board with its relief holes; machine-placed against hand-placed stackers that have no pick-and-place pad; sockets that keep their polyimide pads through the through-hole soldering step. State how many pairs each combination gets and why that count is enough.
4. **Electrical test access.** Daisy chains and test points so that every pair's continuity is read without unmating, and a four-wire access for contact resistance on a stated sample.
5. **Measurements.** C-1 to C-8 as procedures: quantity, method, instrument class and resolution, sample size, the record sheet, and the pass number where DS-16 sets one (C-4, C-1, C-2 and C-3, C-5). Where no pass number is decided, say that the value is recorded for the design authority.
6. **Parts and quantities.** Every part by exact ordering code with quantity per coupon set and spares, the status of each code from ST-01b (confirmed, refuted, not listed in the factory channel), its supply form, and the posts and jack screws by the surveyed class. No price is requested from anyone; a price may be cited only from a listing capture that ST-01b retained, with its capture date.
7. **What the owner decides and does.** The build itself; the questions to the assembler and the optional request to the manufacturer (owner items 3 and 4), worded as questions the owner could send; what continues if the answer is yes, and what follows if it is no. Plain statements, no recommendation beyond the design authority's own.
8. **Size of the work.** Counts only: boards, pairs, positions, parts, measurement points, and the drawing work a later task would need. No cost estimate beyond item 6.

Write `design/stack/coupon/README.md` (verdict headings, one table per item, readable by the owner without the other files), `design/stack/coupon/coupon-definition.json` (boards, pairs with their variant keys, measurements, pass numbers and sources) and a checker under `scripts/stack/coupon/` with tests that prove the definition holds every condition of DS-16 and every change of D5 (pair count per board, every offset on both axes at each gap, both stacker heights, the EL pair, the tie-post bay, the polyimide-pad variant) and that fail on each of those when it is removed.
