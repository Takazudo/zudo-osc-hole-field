## What to deliver

Read the plan page first, then `design/partition/partition.json` (`boards`, `supports`, `mechanical_datum_table`, `load_side_terminals`, `enclosure`, `panel`), `design/partition/mechanical-candidate.json`, `design/mechanical/` (`selector-assembly.json`, `core-rear-allowance.json`, `facts/`), the hardware evidence bundles for the jack, pots, toggles, selector and button under `.claude/skills/component-*/`, and the pages `decisions/osc-board-partition.mdx`, `decisions/osc-selector-assembly.mdx` and `verification/osc-release-gates.mdx`. Issues #55, #57, #64 and #65 may be read; nothing may be written to GitHub.

1. **Datum scheme.** For each board (JL, JR, P with EL, O1, O3, O5, K): what sets its Z plane and its XY position in the proposed stack, and what merely follows. State the status of every datum (SOURCED with the source, DERIVED, or ASSUMED).
2. **Closing orders.** Compare "stack first, panel last" with "panel first, K last" by the number of contacts that mate at once, the mating force and its reaction path, what locates the hardware in the 438 panel apertures, and what can be inspected or reworked at each step.
3. **XY budget per board.** Footprint placement and reflow float of an SMT connector without pegs, pin lean of a tall stacker, board fabrication and hole tolerances, and the resulting misalignment at the worst pair of each front board, against `a`.
4. **Panel aperture stack-up per hardware class** (jack, pot, toggle, selector, button, indicator): the position error the panel must accept in each closing order, against the aperture clearances the sources record.
5. **Z stack-up** across the J and P planes with one rigid K: gap error, the board strain it produces over the 15 to 19 mm between the J and P post rows, and a stated strain limit for ceramic parts with its source or its status as an assumption.
6. **Closing and separation.** Screw-driven closing, jacking points for separating K from 777 mated positions, the reaction path for each, and the seating and removal of the 0.4 mm EL board on the P unit.
7. **Supports.** Which of the existing 87 supports, the seam cradle and K's edge posts stay, move or go, with the conflicts the plan page lists (support pairs 1.5 mm apart, collars overlapping K's edge, the cradle inside the J-to-K gap, the second load loop through the frame). Interior fastener candidates: JL 37, P 26, JR 3 by the planning screen.
8. **Coupon definition.** A small two-board specimen that would measure mating force, alignment yield and reflow lean: what it contains and what it measures. Definition only; building it is a fabrication order that only the owner can place.

Write `design/stack/mechanics/README.md` (verdict headings, one table per stack-up), `design/stack/mechanics/stackups.json` (every term with value, tolerance, unit, status and source) and a small calculator under `scripts/stack/mechanics/` with unit tests that recomputes every result in the README from that JSON.
