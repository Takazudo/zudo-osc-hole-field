## Stop

`regen-all.sh` stops in `scripts/partition/regen.sh` at `scripts/checks/connector_packing35.py`
(`IndexError: list index out of range`, at line 121). The io-partition step before it passes.

That script places the JST GH board-to-board headers that are on the boards today: 25 on JL, 27 on JR and their
mates on K. It takes the sites from a fixed list: JL has 25 sites, sliced from a 27-point grid, and JR has 27.
Each 8-contact header carries four signals.

| Pair | Signal nets before / after | Headers before / after | Sites in the list |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| JL-K | 100 / 108 | 25 / 27 | 25 (27 on the grid) |
| JR-K | 106 / 118 | 27 / 30 | 27 |

The move therefore needs **2 more JL-K and 3 more JR-K header pairs**. That means 5 new headers on the front
boards and 5 on K. The brief's cable budget (75 JR and 128 JL spare positions) is the stack FFC link gate, which
has no headers on the boards yet. The GH header list was not part of the plan, and adding headers adds hardware,
so I stopped.

For JL, the grid already has the two sites the list cuts off: `[:25]` becomes `[:27]`, at (142, 122) and
(159, 122). JR has no spare grid point. Its fourth row at y = 150 holds 6 sites, and one more point (295, 150)
fits that pattern, but not three.

Options:
- **(a)** Rule 5 more header sites: the two JL grid sites, plus three JR sites chosen by the manager or proposed
  by me. Their collisions would be checked by the script's own checks.
- **(b)** Carry the new crossings on fewer headers. The 8-contact headers carry 4 signals each, alternating with
  AGND returns. Changing that is a cable-rule change.
- **(c)** Decide that the GH harness is superseded by the stack FFC links for this stage. That is outside this
  brief's scope.

I recommend (a). The connector count rises by 10, and every other part of Part A is in place.

### Item 1: crossing kind `indicator_led_bridge_one_span`

The kind is in `scripts/checks/io_partition60.py`. A net qualifies only when every condition holds, and each is
read from the partition data, not the net name:
1. The net holds one panel LED (role `magnitude_indicator:LED`) and bridge diodes only.
2. Its diodes lead to exactly one `magnitude_indicator:A` op-amp unit, through that unit's two nets (DRIVE and
   SENSE).
3. The unit has a complete four-diode bridge and one R_SENSE.
4. The op-amp is OPA4196IDR.
5. The op-amp, its four diodes and its R_SENSE share one region, and the LED is in the other.
6. The net touches exactly two regions, one of them core (K). One span: front boards meet only at K.

The bridge diodes are the crossing's source nodes, so the connector step can assign a driver.
