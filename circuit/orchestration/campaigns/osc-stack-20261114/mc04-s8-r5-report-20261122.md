## Part A5: the joint search

The set is U5210 and U8409 (the targets) and the neighbours U8412, U5208 and U8408. The A3 and A4 censuses name no
other block.

Every ruled pose is applied first:
- Part A's eight;
- U4524, U1318 and U4417;
- U4647 with U4502;
- U5209 with R8263.

**Search rules:**
- each block takes a pose within 24 mm on a 0.5 mm grid, at any of 4 turns, on either face;
- a neighbour may stay where it is;
- passives follow the A2 rule; C5210 follows the Revision 3 rule;

**The solver.** `solve()` is a complete depth-first search with forward checking. A failure is a proof over this
candidate grid for this set. Displaced passives are re-placed after each assignment. An assignment whose passives
cannot all be placed is rejected, and the search continues.
