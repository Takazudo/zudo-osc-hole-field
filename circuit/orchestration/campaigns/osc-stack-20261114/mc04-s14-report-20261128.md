## Readings made where the brief does not spell out a rule

1. **Directory.** The study directory is `mc04_s14_feedback_capacitor/`, the brief's allowed path. The coordinator's message named `mc04_s14_feedback_cap/`.
2. **Deck titles.** These keep the S13 text (`MC04-S13 <class> <topology>`), so that P10 and the builder identity hold byte for byte.
3. **Ladder scope.** The S6.10 ladder applies to the loop decks. Closed-loop decks keep the S13 trap-then-gear sequence (class G included). The class G timeout of 120 s applies to its seeds as well.
4. **Seeds.**
   - Specification cells other than class G use the pinned S13 seed of the byte-identical zero-stray netlist. It is re-checked offline under S6.4 and must belong to an S13 group within 5 µV. This covers P4, P6b (H-REF9), P7, arm R, the 12 class C points and the arm R pairs.
   - What-if and class G seeds were run in S14, and each group includes the S13 seeds of the same DC circuit.
   - An output-pair point uses the seed of the same cell, corner and kind at the 20 pF output stray, because a capacitor-only change is not DC-distinct (S6.1, S6.6).
5. **Sine residual.** The fit is the mean, the fundamental and harmonics 2 to 10, with no linear term. Without the mean, the DC level of a cell idling away from 0 V would count as oscillation; dropping the linear term is the stricter part.
6. **Clipping.** Clipping is read from the full `t1` transient of OUT (|v| ≥ 11 V). The peak excursion is measured from the seed's quiescent OUT.
7. **K4 settled test.** This is 5 % of the measured output step (the mean of the last tenth minus the pre-edge mean).
8. **S5 resolutions.**
   - Class C K6 and K8 use the recorded `resolution_fraction` (timestep over τ0, 0.05 %) against the relative margin. This reproduces the brief's statement that class C fails both at 100 pF.
   - The absolute K6 (E-control, H-REF9) uses the transient timestep.
   - K3 and K4 (relative), K7 and K9 use the recording quantum of the figures (6 significant digits; 5 for overshoot).
9. **Candidates.** These are the declared list per corner. At class C wiper 0.5, 16 pF has its double (32 pF) tested but is not declared, so it is rejected (stricter).
10. **Corner status.**
    - Any evaluated failure at zero stray among K1, K2, K5, the baseline ceilings or the absolute K6 makes the corner FAILS AT ZERO STRAY.
    - A K3 or K4 excess together with a failing K1, K2 or K5 is a failure, not a relative form.
    - A corner whose only gap at zero stray is NOT EVALUATED is labelled NOT EVALUATED.
    - A value with no accepted operating point makes the row NOT EVALUATED (as in S13).
11. **Combined labels.**
    - A relative-form row that also has PROVISIONAL reasons is labelled `PROVISIONAL n pF: …; BASELINE EXCEEDS K4 (RELATIVE FORM)`.
    - When both K3 and K4 use the relative form, the label reads `K3 AND K4`.
12. **What-if paper estimate.** A what-if row with two nets uses the larger paper estimate.
13. **P7 corner.** P7 uses the first corner, as S13 did: `bracket agnd` for `/W1/DRIVE_INV_SUM`, and `tmux 0, agnd` for `/E2/REF9_FB`.

## Method limits

These are the S13 method limits, plus four that this study adds:

- the capacitor is an ideal 10 pF element;
- its tolerance is not swept;
- class G uses a two-step ladder;
- arm R re-uses the S13 records and waveforms, and the cache binding is by the recorded metrics, not by a data hash.
