## Method

- **Block:** the IC, its decouplers, and its cell-local resistors and capacitors. A part counts as cell-local when it
  is on the same board, all its signal nets are on the IC, and it is within 12 mm of the IC. The block moves rigidly.
  - On the other face it is mirrored about the IC origin.
  - Diodes and parts shared with other cells stay put. Their distance to the IC is reported before and after the move.
  - No 90° turns were searched.
- **Obstacles:** the link gate's re-placement model (`links_gate_part2.Field`):
  - board outline;
  - footprints at the 0.35 mm courtyard growth;
  - panel hardware and holes;
  - the fixing keep-outs, including the ruled sleeves;
  - every placed link's field, tail, slot and latch margin;
  - the parts the gate re-placed, at their new courtyards.
- **Search:** grid offsets every 0.5 mm out to 40 mm, then every 1 mm out to 160 mm, on both faces. Blocks are placed
  one after another in the open-list order. Each found pose is fixed before the next block, so later results depend
  on earlier ones.
- **Class (iii):** the search stays within 24 mm and lets the block displace R, C and D footprints of up to 12 mm²
  that are not through-hole. It picks the pose that displaces the fewest parts. Each displaced part then gets its own
  nearest legal move, up to 25 mm.

## Options for the ruling

1. **Apply (i) and (iii) as studied.** Those are 8 ICs. The displaced passives move 2–8 mm each.
2. **For (ii), choose per IC** between:
   - accepting the far pose, as an interim-GH locality acceptance or under the input-buffer conditions;
   - re-running the study with 90° block turns or a smaller block, which leaves more parts behind;
   - letting (iii)-style displacement reach farther than 24 mm.

   Turns are the cheapest to try. The stage 7 gate search used them for single parts.
3. **Input buffers.** U4647 (97 mm) and U8409 (111 mm) are the two worst cases. Each probably needs its own ruling,
   for example the input buffer staying on its board with its link moved. That is outside this stage's rule against
   moving slots.
