## Item 6: K region E5 overflow (R4547)

**Cause:** R4547 (E5, B.Cu) sat under the K service aperture of the new header JL-K-27-K. The aperture is a

**Fix:** a service aperture now displaces pins on both faces (`header_conflicts(..., apertures=...)`). R4547 is
re-placed near its slot through the near-slot loop, at (159.64, 144.66) B.Cu inside E5, 22.0 mm from its slot.
The K header displacement list is now C205, C206, C213, C235, C4540, DH212, R201, R210, R4361, R4547, U201, U211
and U4523. U201's cluster moves 5.75 mm.

## Step 8: STOP

**What I ran:**
- `scripts/stack/geometry/generate.py` regenerated `stack-candidate.json`. The old one was stale against the new
  partition: `check.py` reported 127 pin-budget errors.
- `check.py` now gives FAIL 112. All are `fixing_reach`, which was already FAIL 108 at HEAD. The four new ones are
  the new pairs JL-K-26 (41.6 mm to POWER-JL-AGND3), JL-K-27 (51.0 mm), JR-K-28 (118.5 mm to JR-SUP-1) and
  JR-K-29 (84.5 mm), against the 40 mm reach. JR-K-30 meets it.
- A pre-c6 gate run on the new candidate, with the hold check disabled for diagnosis only, shows the change below.

**What changes:**
- The link gate clusters the GH pairs into links greedily. The five new pairs change the clusters and the link
  numbering on both JL and JR:
  - the JL row-80 cluster JL-K-15..18 is now JL-K-14..17 plus a single JL-K-18;
  - JL-K-26 joins JL-K-23..25;
  - JL-K-27 and JL-K-9 become one-pair links;
  - JR-K-28/29 join JR-K-1..3, and JR-K-30 joins JR-K-5..7;
  - every JL and JR link ID shifts.
- The cluster that Revision 2's hold names, JL-K-JL-L02 (search centre (133.5, 80), members JL-K-15..18), no
  longer exists. Its ID now names the cluster JL-K-19..22 at (48.5, 122).
- `hold_errors` therefore fails the gate ("the link search centre changed; the hold is stale"), as designed.
- The P-K links keep their members and centres, so the c6 transformation itself is not affected. It cannot run
  until the gate regenerates.

**Options:**
- **(a) Stable link clusters (recommended)**, as Revision 9 did for the GH headers. Keep each baseline link's
  member pairs and ID. The new pairs form new links, or join an existing link only when it has spare positions
  and the join radius allows. The JL-L02 hold then still names its cluster, and c6 gets a fresh attempt on the
  changed signal sets.
- **(b) Accept the regrouped clusters.** Re-rule the case (b) hold for the new cluster that contains the retained
  slot area, or drop it. Then run c6 on the regrouped links.
