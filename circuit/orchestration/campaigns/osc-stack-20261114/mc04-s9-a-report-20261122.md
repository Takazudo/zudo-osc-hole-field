## Arm 1: R1, local P-K slot moves

**Rules.** Candidates come from the retained `links_gate.search`. Each link keeps the record's size, connector, cable
and stock length, so there is no way-4 growth. The search uses the retained 24 mm radius about the link's recorded
search centre (the clustering join radius is 35 mm). It runs on the 2.0, 0.5 and 0.25 mm grids. The cap is 24
evaluated candidates per link and grid, cheapest first. The time ceiling was 4 h; the run takes about 15 min.

A candidate is accepted when:
- its members keep the retained class distances (PRECISION 40, ANALOGUE 45, WIPER 55, LOGIC 55 mm);
- its riders keep their class distances;
- loop ≥ 0;
- compliance ≤ 1.0 mm/N;
- the target block at its recorded position is clear of the moved link's keep-outs grown 0.35 mm.

**Re-placement.** Re-placement is face-aware and stays within 24 mm. An IC moves as its S8 block. Parts that the gate or
S8 had re-placed onto the target's recorded area are re-placed too, as in step 2b. No placed link-era block may move.
No input-buffer part may move: these are packages with a high-impedance-input or remote-buffer unit, their decouplers
and cell-local passives, and the cell parts of those roles (the S8 A3 definition).

## Interpretations made

- "Retained join radius" is read as the retained 24 mm search radius about the link's search centre. Member distances
  are held by the class accept; the clustering join radius of 35 mm is recorded.
- The input-buffer rule uses the S8 A3 definition (see Arm 1). Under that definition, decouplers and cell parts that the
  gate had already re-placed count as part of an input-buffer cell. This decides U8409, and 23 of the U5110 candidates.
- Parts on a target's recorded area are re-placed with the newly covered ones, as in the step 2b precedent.
