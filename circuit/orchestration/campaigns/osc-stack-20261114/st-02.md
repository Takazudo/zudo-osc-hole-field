## What to build

Read the plan page first. Its sections "The current layout already has the geometry of a rigid stack", "Stack order", "Interconnect per board pair", "EL plugs into a P tab", "Power joints", "OSC2 and OSC4" and "Routed drafts" hold the claims to reproduce or correct.

1. **Generator.** Derive the stack candidate from `design/partition/partition.json`, `design/grid/placements.lock.json`, the board files and the retained heights under `design/mechanical/`. Nothing is hand-copied. The candidate holds: board planes; every rigid pair with its two boards, site XY, position count and pin map (signals, AGND, rails), including the JL-to-P nets that pass through K and the EL nets that pass through P; the 18 power posts; the four P tabs (EL tab, power tab, two control tabs) as proposed outlines; the two SUB toggle sites; the retired items (O2, O4, their four GH7 sites, the 191 service apertures).
2. **Checker.** Coaxial sites on both boards; replacement courtyard inside the GH courtyard it replaces, on both boards, for the parameterised land pattern; the 0.7 mm placement window of the SUB pair at OSC2 and the window at OSC4; facing-height collisions in each gap from retained part heights; selector-tail clearance to K; the pin budget per pair; the eight pass-through sites and what blocks them; post coaxiality, including the four offset P-branch pairs and the power tab that removes the offset; both control tabs, the EL tab and the power tab against every board outline, post, support and carrier envelope that the sources record.
3. **Tests.** Fault injection: each class of defect the checker claims to catch is injected into a copy of the candidate and must fail with a specific message. A staleness test fails with the regeneration command when the committed candidate differs from a fresh generation. Bind staleness to a canonical digest of the inputs the candidate uses (footprint placement, courtyard and pad geometry, outlines, the partition fields read), so that a routing-only change of a board does not make it stale.
4. **Reconciliation.** `design/stack/geometry/README.md` with one row per count or dimension on the plan page: page value, checker value, verdict (REPRODUCED, CORRECTED with the reason, or NOT CHECKABLE with the missing source). Courtyard-level and pad-level results are named as such; nothing is called a copper clearance or a tolerance result.

Board files: `boards/osc-core/osc-core.kicad_pcb` (K), `boards/osc-jack-left/osc-jack-left.kicad_pcb`, `boards/osc-jack-right/osc-jack-right.kicad_pcb`, `boards/osc-control/osc-control-routed-draft.kicad_pcb` (P), `boards/osc-stage-optical/osc-stage-optical.kicad_pcb` (EL), `boards/osc-octave-1` to `osc-octave-5`. Confirm each against the partition and the checkpoint before use.

The planning scripts in `/mnt/project-files/stack-plan/` (README there) show how the page's numbers were measured. They are a reference for the obstacle model only, not evidence and not code to retain as is.
