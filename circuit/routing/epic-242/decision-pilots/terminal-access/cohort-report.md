## Effect of diagnostic assumptions and bounded decision

**Center-inside contact is sufficient, not necessary.** The continuous negative
certificate applies to a full-width capsule centered anywhere inside the pad.
An adequate off-pad pad-end contact could avoid that start-cap condition and
must be evaluated without hidden neck-down, tangential-only contact or reduced
clearance. The current results do not prove that every electrically adequate
contact is impossible. The continuous bound uses ordinary floating engineering
enclosures, not directed-rounding arithmetic or native verification.

**Two millimeters and straight templates are diagnostic limits, not physical
limits.** They cannot exclude longer, curved, multisegment or other off-grid
escapes. In particular, no-via counts and K1402 C4163.2's zero-edge count have
only that finite scope. The foreign-pad start-cap certificate is independent
of straight-edge length, but still depends on the center-inside policy.

The bounded pass is complete. F3 K1402 versus two-A is the first useful original
matched comparison to review, with positive signal/return access, a concrete
asymmetric signal launch, and explicit shared rail limitations. E1 remains a
secondary local contrast. Neither full four-demand cohort has all-terminal
access established under this checker. No row is dropped, replaced or credited
as a closure. There is no architecture adoption, electrical relaxation, copper
cut, larger budget or repeat O1 trial. The exact executable native request and
any routing-method adaptation remain unapproved and unimplemented; the present
standalone validator does not silently change the frozen raster router.
