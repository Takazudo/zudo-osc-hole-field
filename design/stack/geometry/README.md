# Direct-stack geometry candidate (ST-02)

Authority: **PROPOSAL (planning)**. Status: **generated planning candidate; unvalidated draft; not adopted**. Every result here is a courtyard-and-pad-level screen. None is a copper clearance, a tolerance result or a physical fit.

This folder replaces the ad hoc measurements behind [the direct board-stack plan](../../../doc/src/content/docs/decisions/osc-direct-stack-plan.mdx) with a generated candidate and a retained checker.

| File | What it is | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `stack-candidate.json` | Planes, 203 rigid pairs with sites and pin maps, 18 posts, four P tabs, SUB toggle windows, retired items, input hashes and the canonical input digest | Generated by `scripts/stack/geometry/generate.py` |
| `check-report.json` | Checker verdict, per-check detail and the reconciliation measurements | Generated by `scripts/stack/geometry/check.py` |
| `land-pattern.json` | **The single connector land-pattern input.** Default: the planning envelope 5.8 mm × (2n + 1) mm. ST-01 replaces it with measured courtyards, per half if they differ | Hand-edited input |
| `parameters.json` | Planning parameters: adapter mated height (sets K's plane), enclosure depth, obstacle growths, tab edge margin, pin-order templates, sweep envelopes | Hand-edited input |

```sh
python3 scripts/stack/geometry/generate.py      # writes stack-candidate.json
python3 scripts/stack/geometry/check.py         # writes check-report.json; exit 1 on any error
python3 scripts/checks/frozen_studies.py discover scripts/stack/geometry   # 39 tests, about 27 s
```

After editing `land-pattern.json` or `parameters.json`, or after any board, partition or lock change, run both commands again. No KiCad and no network are needed.

## The checker passes on the generated candidate

`check-report.json` gives `PASS` with 0 errors on 11 checks plus staleness. The two declared courtyard-level conflicts are the blocked pass-through sites below; they are recorded in the candidate, and the checker fails if they are missing or invented.

| Check | What fails it |
| --- | --- |
| `planes_and_gaps` | A plane, gap or pair height that differs from the sources; a selector tail at or behind K; rear parts behind the enclosure |
| `coaxial_sites` | A pair whose two sites differ in XY, or a site off the courtyard centre or face of the header it replaces |
| `replacement_inside_gh_courtyard` | A land-pattern envelope outside the GH courtyard it replaces, on either board; a stale envelope |
| `courtyard_blockers_declared` | An undeclared obstacle in a site envelope, or a declared one that the sources do not give |
| `pin_budget` | A missing source net, more pin entries than positions, a non-AGND net on a template AGND position, a signal without an adjacent AGND where the rule is required, an unreplaced or a retired harness, a rail that no EL-to-P pair carries |
| `pass_through_sites` | Fewer or more than 8 JL-to-P and 6 EL pass-through pairs |
| `sub_toggle_windows` | A SUB pair outside its placement window; a toggle off its lock centre or across a selector courtyard; a toggle courtyard outside the union of its control tab and P's outline; a toggle courtyard on a P F.Cu obstacle or a toggle pad on a P B.Cu obstacle; a pair outside its control tab. The toggle tails against K F.Cu are NOT CHECKABLE: the retained native envelope of the toggle starts at the board face, so no tail protrusion is retained |
| `post_coaxiality` | A post whose front land is not at the K land, or an offset land not carried inside a tab |
| `p_tabs` | A tab that overlaps P itself, is not attached to P, overlaps a JL, JR or adapter outline, a support collar it does not pass, a seam carrier in P's z range, a kept selector courtyard, or a post land it does not carry; an EL pair outside the EL tab |
| `retired_items` | A pair on O2 or O4, reuse of a retired K header, a wrong aperture count |
| `facing_heights` | Two overlapping facing parts whose retained heights sum to the gap or more, or one part as tall as the gap |
| `staleness` | A canonical input digest or candidate content that differs from a fresh generation; the message names the regeneration command |

The 39 unit tests inject each fault class into a copy of the candidate (or, for the land pattern and the K plane, into a copy of the inputs) and require the specific message. Two staleness tests show that adding a track and a via to a board keeps the digest, and that moving a footprint by 0.5 mm changes it. A third requires both tracked outputs to equal a fresh generation byte for byte; neither file records the commit or the clock, so regenerating at a new commit with unchanged inputs leaves the tree clean.

## The staleness digest covers footprint geometry, not routing

The candidate records the SHA256 of every input file and a canonical digest of the fields it uses: every footprint's reference, library, side, origin, angle, courtyard box and pad geometry on all ten boards, and the partition, lock, mechanical and parameter fields listed in `stackgeom.canonical_inputs`. Tracks, vias, zones and pad nets are excluded, so a routing-only change does not make the candidate stale.

K, JL and JR match the metadata-successor hashes in `circuit/routing/osc-pcb-current-checkpoint.json`. P (`osc-control-routed-draft`), EL and the adapters are not covered by that checkpoint; their hashes are recorded in the candidate. The plain-Python extract of K matched the planning extract footprint for footprint, so no KiCad query was needed.

## Two of the eight pass-through sites on K are blocked

The JL-to-P nets cross K on new 1×3 sites at the JL header coordinates (x, 150) and the P header coordinates (x, 276). Six are clear in place on K F.Cu. (74, 150) is blocked by C1230, DO1205, R1257, R1283, R1286 and RVO1209; (91, 150) by C1215, C1218, C1229, R1287, R2143, RB1210 and RB1211. The plan page named only R1286 and C1218.

## The SUB pair window at OSC2 is 0.7 mm with the planning envelope

With the 7.0 × 5.8 mm envelope along x, the pair clears the toggle pads, P B.Cu and K F.Cu (retired GH7 headers removed) for centres y = 180.15 to 180.85 at OSC2 and 179.05 to 180.85 at OSC4. The candidate places the pairs at the window centres, (31.5, 180.5) and (65.5, 179.95), so the control tabs reach y = 177.1 and 176.55. The window shrinks one for one with the envelope width across the row, so a 6.5 mm wide pattern leaves a single centre at OSC2 (y = 180.5, zero margin) and a wider one leaves none; re-run with the ST-01 footprint.

## Some pin-order rules cannot hold in the planned positions

These are reported in `pin_budget` detail and are not failures, because the plan does not require the rule for these pairs.

- A K-to-adapter 1×5 with four signals and one AGND leaves two signals without an adjacent AGND at each adapter (`/On/OCT_SELECTED`, `OSC_OCT2`).
- An EL-to-P 1×6 with three signals, one AGND and two rails leaves one signal per site without an adjacent AGND (`/En/NOT_FALL`). Each site carries two of the three rails, so each rail reaches EL at four of six sites: 12 rail positions against 18 rail contacts today.

## Facing heights are clear where heights are retained; most heights are not

No facing-height collision exists among parts with retained heights. Heights come only from the native display-model envelopes in `design/mechanical/ic-envelope-native-report.json` (not seated heights), the selector tails in `selector-assembly.json` and the partition bulk reserves. Most overlapping facing pairs involve a part with no retained height: 694 in JL/K, 900 in JR/K, 693 in P/K, and 25 across the three adapter gaps. Those pairs are **NOT RUN**.
