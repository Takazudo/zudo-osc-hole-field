## The rules this run uses

- Field: FH12 at courtyard level, (0.5 n + 5.6) by 6.1 mm with 0.25 mm growth per side; tail 0.5 (n + 1) mm wide. Slot, cable path, search grid (2 mm within 24 mm, four directions), margins and screens as in the option study.
- Sizes per joint: JL-K 40; JR-K 40; P-K 40/26/14/8; JL-P via K 40/26/14/8; on the P joint a link is clustered at the largest size and re-clustered from its own sites at the next smaller size where it finds no place. Adapters first, then JL to K, JR to K, P to K and JL to P through K. Clustering: the option study's greedy rule within 35 mm with the feasibility of the allocation rule.
- Tails: per slot candidate the shortest legal front extension, then the shortest legal K extension, in steps of 2 mm within the spare of the cable; cost = displaced footprints + 0.25 x (slot distance + both extensions).
- Compliance (DS-87): above 1 mm/N on the map no K field is placed; above 0.25 mm/N listed.
- The checker (`links_gate.check`) reads the run's inputs from `flags`, checks joint, front board, size and tail extensions against the sites, the ladder, the step and the spare, rebuilds every geometry, re-screens each placed link against all others with the screens of the search, recomputes the allocation, displaced lists, compliance values, trim lists, cable lengths and latch verdicts, and recomputes the nearest misses and blockers of every link without a place. It proves legality, not that a larger size or another order would not have placed.

- A full reordering or backtracking search: only the bounded improvement pass is run.
