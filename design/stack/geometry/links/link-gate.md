## The rules this run uses

- Field: FH12 at courtyard level, (0.5 n + 5.6) by 6.1 mm; the land chain is ASSUMED inside the 6.1 mm depth. Tail: the cable, 0.5 (n + 1) mm wide. Slot, cable path, search grid (2.0 mm within 24.0 mm, four directions), margins and the screens of attempt a6 as in the option study.
- Ground rule of DS-62 per link: circuits needed = A + Q + max(u - 1, 1) (see `link-gate-options.json`); the order per link is recorded in `positions` (net, class and site, ground or unused).
- Clustering: the option study's greedy rule within 35 mm (ASSUMED), with DS-62 feasibility; no further grouping by class.
- Sizes: JL and JR at 40 circuits; on the P joint a link is clustered at 40 and, where it finds no place, re-clustered from its own sites at 26, then 14, then 8; its sub-links are placed at once. Adapters first, then JL to K, JR to K, P to K and JL to P through K.
- Tails: per slot candidate the shortest legal front extension, then the shortest legal K extension, in steps of 2.0 mm, their sum within the spare of the cable; cost = displaced footprints + 0.25 x (slot distance + both extensions).
- The checker (`links_gate.check`) rebuilds every geometry from slot centre, direction, size and tail extensions, re-screens each placed link against all others, and recomputes the allocation under DS-62, the displaced lists, the trim list and the cable length. It proves legality, not that a larger size or another order would not have placed.
