## A 50 mm same-side cable reaches every placed link: the Z path needs 37.3 to 39.3 mm at the shortest field spacing

Needed length = 2 x 4.0 mm insertion + a + b + V + (pi - 4) x 2.4 mm, with a = b = 6.4 mm from each connector opening to the slot centre (4.0 mm straight stiffened tail, plus the 2.4 mm bend radius; all DERIVED from the stock cable figures of ST-01e at nominal values and the factory bend rule) and V = gap + 1.6 mm K (20.6 mm for JL and JR, 18.6 mm for P). The 10.66 to 12.66 mm of spare length is a slack loop in the gap; whether it rests clear of the parts there is NOT RUN. The search keeps a and b at their minimum, so a cable never limits a placement in this study; a longer offset between the fields is not searched.

## The search is greedy and does not backtrack, so a link without a place may still have one under another order

- Links in a fixed order (joint, link id). For each: every slot centre on a 2.0 mm grid within 24 mm of the link centre (ASSUMED), four cable directions. A candidate is legal when the front field lies on the front board, the slot keeps a 2.0 mm web to the K edge, holes, fixing keep-outs, kept rigid pairs and other slots, the K field lies on K, no field or tail meets another link, and no field, tail or slot lies on panel hardware, a fixing keep-out (18 posts and 12 kept supports), a tab, a SUB toggle, an adapter outline or a kept selector envelope.
- Cost = displaced footprints (front field, slot on both K faces, K field) + 0.25 per mm of slot distance (ASSUMED). The cheapest legal candidate is kept and becomes an obstacle for later links.
- A link without a legal candidate is reported with the blockers that rejected most candidates; it is not forced in. Another order, a wider radius or a different split of the sites may place more; a 40 mm radius placed one more link for the 20-circuit option in a scratch run with the earlier envelope and was not retained.
- The tails between each connector and its slot are listed (`under_tails` in the candidate) and not screened: whether a tail passes over a part or a panel pin is a height question with no retained heights (NOT RUN).
- Latch reach: a K-rear footprint within 1.0 mm (ASSUMED) of a K connector that no link displaces is listed; its height is not retained, so reach is NOT SHOWN there. The front end is plugged with K off and has open access in every placed case.

## Four assumed parameters move the result most: field envelope, clustering radius, ground-rule reading and tail length

- The field envelopes. Main rows: the largest sourced latching body of each pitch (ST-01e/ST-01g at integration commit a3b25d0, read without merging): at 1.00 mm the CKMTW F-FPC1M-NP-B310 slider length n + 7.0 with the HMTCONN FPC1.0-WFX11 depth 6.04 mm, an envelope that encloses the Megastar ZX-1.0FPC-2H-HFJ dual-contact flip lock (n + 4.04 by 5.30 mm); at 0.5 mm the Bossie BX-FPC05-2HNPX flip lock 0.5 n + 4.4 by 5.12 mm, longer than the Hirose FH12 at 40 circuits. Second rows (suffix NL): the earlier non-latching HDGC envelope n + 2.00 by 6.54 mm. The 0.25 mm courtyard growth is ASSUMED; the open flip-lock or slider sweep in plan is not dimensioned. A dual-contact part at one end removes the need for same-side cable contacts (the 30-circuit 0.5 mm stock cable is opposite-side only).
- The earlier ASSUMED 0.5 mm rows (JST FLZ body formula, 1.0 mm lead protrusion) are replaced by the sourced Bossie envelope and are not computed.
- The clustering join radius (35 mm) and the order of the greedy search, which together set how many P links compete for the few windows between P's panel pins.
- The ground-rule reading: "a ground between every two signals" is taken without grounds at the outer edges.
- The fixed tail length a = b = 6.4 mm (stiffener 8 +/- 2 mm and exposed contacts 4 +/- 1 mm at nominal) and the slot of tail width + 1.0 mm by 1.5 mm with a 2.0 mm web.
