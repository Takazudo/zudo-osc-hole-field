## Source datums and bounded candidate

The jack housing's 9 x 10.5 mm drawing envelope is footprint-family evidence, not exact WQP seating qualification. Neither alternative repairs these gaps by assumption.

O1/O3/O5 adapters are at -16; O2/O4 at -29 with 180° rotation and 13 mm custom extensions. O1/O2 shafts are (14.5,185)/(31.5,185), exactly 17 mm apart. Their conservative XY boxes overlap by 1.2 mm, so the 13 mm depth separation is essential. Their opposing z faces leave 1.5 mm nominal; retained growth 0.5 and relative plane error 0.25 allocate only 0.75 mm. This remains #55 physical qualification. Rotation must transform the asymmetric y extent; no coplanar strip is proposed.

The new *allocated* middle component face is -50, rear core face -100, thickness 1.6±0.2, relative plane tolerance 0.5, rear component allowance 5, and inner cover -120 mm. These are candidate constraints, not approved enclosure or board dimensions. They maintain room behind the selector tails at -33. A 9 mm connector envelope and 2 mm clearance are hypothetical procurement constraints. The bend radius must remain at least 9.398 mm, retained from Alpha Wire 6821 BK005 evidence (10 times maximum 0.9398 mm wire diameter); the actual installed cable path is not modeled. JST GH top-entry 7.3 mm is a reference local mating height with no installed tolerance; it does **not** establish any board spacing or certify those allocations.

These checks are bounded empty-envelope arithmetic; copper, actual solder tails, mating travel, latch action and all package solids remain unresolved. A connector that exceeds the bounds invalidates the calculation rather than enlarging the envelope silently.

## Adjacent reservations and access

Each O1/O2 candidate rear card occupies x=center±7.5, y=24..290 at -100. The 15 x 266 mm rectangle gives 2 mm between cards. It is deliberately a falsifiable reservation: **no assertion that 220 O1 packages plus 30 shared packages fit or route within it**. All neighboring O3+ circuitry owns x>=40; a wider core or lateral connector escape cannot borrow that volume without a new whole-neighbor review. Other-function front hardware retains the full locked placement source; this proof is local and cannot qualify their hidden rear volumes.

Six individual rear-facing trim axes are proposed at x=14.5 and y=42,68,94,120,146,172, with O2 translated +17 mm. The report maps all six existing Ref/pins: BASE, RZ, SCALE, HF, SINE_SYMMETRY and SINE_LEVEL. These are new proposed core locations, **not current PCB locations**. Reserve 6 mm diameter axial screwdriver cylinders extending rearward through the open cover; actual actuator orientation, adjustment torque and insulation tool are unselected, and HF trim identity is missing. Four 4 mm probe reservations at y=206,220,234,248 are for existing GND/rail/measurement access. They are not permission to add test-point loads on sensitive nodes: final assignment must use the contract's existing endpoints and a loading budget.

Connector/support reservation centres are in `mechanical-input.json`: y=188/266 for 15 x 12 mm connector banks; y=27/287 for 3.2 mm collar envelopes. Exact pin-count packing is unknown and must be checked against both middle faces and the electrical contract; two connector banks do not mean two connectors. Existing 36 rear terminals remain individually inventoried in the generated report at their original coordinates; no deletion or relocation is assumed. Their supported detachable conversion is a blocker to genuine rear-card removal.

A 20 mm finger cylinder is **3 mm wider than the 17 mm adjacent pitch**. Thus simultaneous finger insertion between adjacent cards fails; a narrow insulated latch tool and sequential rear-facing release is a proposal needing exact connector retention force and tool-stroke evidence. Do not describe this as finger-serviceable. Unplug before releasing card supports. Reserve 35 mm rearward extraction stroke after connectors are detached and 80 mm external workspace beyond the removed cover. Required opening extends across both card rectangles, tools and grips; no actual cover opening/hinge has been selected. The present internal 13.4 mm nominal cover gap cannot permit 35 mm extraction with cover fitted.

No holes in the middle are required by this proposal: trims/probes face rear. This avoids assuming a Swiss-cheese return plane or compromised middle stiffness. Middle zones preserve continuous AGND/return copper; route capacity across connector banks, shared power/reference corridor, mounting clearances and return necks remain unresolved. These have named owners in the interface/electrical packet; empty rectangles cannot demonstrate routing.

## Support and service load paths

Front jack nuts and panel react insertion load; exact nuts/thread engagement remain missing. Bushingless pots need captive insulating body carriers transferring knob torque to enclosure side rails. Selector carriers retain their stepped M9 support, not solder or connector retention. Independently supported front boards use their own enclosure posts; middle has a separate enclosure frame. Rear cards use a guided removable carrier with positive end fasteners, independent of connector friction. The two support centres shown reserve collar space but do not establish guide-rail strength, fastener engagement or board deflection. Factory backing fixtures react connector insertion/withdrawal forces locally.

Retain source load cases: 30 N jack insertion, 10 N lateral knob, 0.2 N·m torque, 5 N cable/button, 10 g transport. Material modulus, rail cross-section, span boundary conditions, exact forces and allowable deflection are unknown. Therefore no beam-strength or vibration pass is fabricated. Owner enclosure/rail constraints and later prototype loads are indispensable.

The core-only alternative retains front boards, carriers and existing loom sites, converts only rear termination to a detachable core carrier, and avoids a new middle's two mating faces. It still needs package-local circuits, shared return/reference integrity, rear-terminal conversion and trim access; retaining old copper does not authorize cutting it into cards. Functional architecture needs front-interface redesign, middle routing and additional service connections. Both alternatives preserve all panel centres, functionality and 3+2 octave mechanics; neither currently proves exact connector fit or removal.

The generator checks reservation containment within parameterized card bounds, same-card bounding-box separation, adjacent reservation separation, and external work depth against extraction stroke. Conflicts remain explicit; no geometry PASS is inferred for actual unmodeled solids. All probe/support/connector dimensions and card y limits drive the SVG. Exact latch/mating sweeps, rail solids, cable curvature in the final path and board deflection are **NOT CHECKED**, pending component selection and physical models.
