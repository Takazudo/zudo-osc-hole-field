## Required transforms in the locked frame

The locked frame is +x right and +y down seen from the front; Stage10 z increases toward the
rear. The female body sits behind A01-middle, so view A looks along −z. The male body sits in
front of A01-core, so view B looks along +z. The drawing-to-board determinants must
therefore be **+1 (female)** and **−1 (male)**. Stage10 gives −1 and +1.

## Candidate: column reversal

`candidate_position(row, n) = Stage10_position(row, 33 − n)`, applied to J17909 and J17910.

- Female matrix: [[0,1],[1,0]] → [[0,1],[−1,0]] (det +1).
- Male matrix: [[0,−1],[1,0]] → [[0,−1],[−1,0]] (det −1).
- Each component-side view becomes the drawing view turned +90°: female from the rear, male
  from the front.
- Physical a1 of both connectors moves to the Stage10 a32 position (301.54, 188.63) and
  becomes the pin-1 mark. c1 moves to (296.46, 188.63), a32 to (301.54, 267.37) and c32 to
  (296.46, 267.37).
- All 96 contacts per connector move. The unlabelled hole set, pitch, board planes (z = 50
  and 69.2) and both female fixing holes (299.3, 183.0) / (299.3, 273.0) are unchanged.
- Female and male physical contact p still coincide in XY.
- Nets stay with the physical contact ID. b1–b8 remain AGND and c1–c8 keep their signals;
  80 contacts remain NC. No net is renumbered.
- Row reversal is rejected because it moves the fixing holes 0.6 mm. No 0.6 mm hole move is
  proposed.
