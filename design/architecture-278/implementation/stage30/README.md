DIN node. The 16 mated pairs keep the fixed 25 mm DIN allocation. The other 34 use the Stage10
`trace_length` model (1.15 × octile XY + 7 mm + |dz|) and are recomputed with the design XY.

## J-PWR route study: no route under existing conditions

This is separate from the DIN correction. Stage28 found that the straight H-J-PWR-1…4
proxies cross the J-SIG middle release sweep. Stage30 screens them, and four waypoint
candidates, against these Stage10 keep-out classes: release sweeps, tool access, the DIN body,
core withdrawal, boards, carrier rails, crossmembers and tabs, handles, A01 bodies (assumed
3 mm high) and the locked foreign panel hardware (A02–A05 jacks/LEDs, E2–E5 switches). Mount
spacers, screw heads, anchor bosses and other cables are not modelled.

The bundle envelope is an **assumption**. A 1.0 mm OD wire in a 4-wire bundle has a 1.21 mm
radius; adding a 0.5 mm margin gives a required centreline clearance of 1.71 mm. A clear
centreline is not physical non-interference.
- **New finding:** the straight proxies of J-PWR (and of all seven J-SIG conductors) also
  cross the A01-middle carrier left rail (x 242–248, z 34–46). The J-PWR middle header sits
  inside that closed ring. The ways in are all too narrow for the bundle:
  - the 2.4 mm slot under A01-middle
  - the 2 mm gap at the jack board edge
  - a rail gap that the C-SIG sweep fills
  - the open front, which lies over locked foreign hardware of unknown depth
- **Candidates:**
  - W1 (bypass the sweep) is blocked by the rail.
  - W2 (the 2.4 mm slot) is blocked: it would need 3.41 mm.
  - W3 (wrap in front) stays UNKNOWN because of the foreign depth (A04, A05, E2, E3) and needs
    about 154 mm of cable.
- **Minimum change, proposal only:**
  - W4 relieves the left rail top to z 43.5 over y 158–177 (2.5 mm deep, leaving 9.5 mm of
    rail).
  - Its centreline then clears with 2.07 mm, but it needs about 111.5 mm of cable under the
    Stage10 release/slack rule (Stage10 cut: 90 ± 3).
  - It rests on a second assumption: the unknown depth of the foreign hardware ends at the
    middle carrier front, z 34. If that depth reaches z 34.29, W4 also meets it (0.29 mm
    margin).
  - Its first bend can be at most 2.07 mm (any route that turns directly toward the ring below
    A01-middle: ≤ 2.79 mm), below the assumed 5 mm.
  - The rail stiffness, the cut, the bend radius and the J-SIG crossing remain open.
- **Not adopted:** no new hole, fixture or anchor. The service sequence is unchanged:
  disconnect K-PWR, then withdraw the core 40 mm.
