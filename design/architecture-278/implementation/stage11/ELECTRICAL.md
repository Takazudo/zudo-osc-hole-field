# A01 electrical qualification study

This is a conditional source calculation, not electrical acceptance. Reproduce with `python3 scripts/architecture278/implementation/stage11_electrical.py` and its `--check`; run `python3 -m unittest discover -s scripts/architecture278/implementation -p test_stage11_electrical.py`. Stage10 sources and geometry remain intact.

The original partition allowance was **0.5 mΩ for combined J-or-P and K ground spreading/necks, including vias/shared necks**, and **1 mΩ for rail planes**. `partition35.py` adds wire and termination terms separately; the source expressly excludes the common PCB term from its worst GH path. Architecture revision48b7847a and issue282 use broader combined branch/whole-transfer language including connectors. These domains are not interchangeable. Both requirements are retained; their mapping to A01 remains unresolved. Neither is a measured instability threshold or manufacturer contact specification. A GH maximum exceeding the broader architecture number does not prove violation of the original plane-only allowance.

The exact nominal B104 manual-offset source, 1 kΩ series resistor and 10 MΩ fail resistor produce about **5.094 mV** maximum sampled loading nonlinearity. Candidate resistor tolerance corners and retained Bourns ±20% end-to-end resistance increase this finite-grid result to **6.113 mV**. At a 5 V input, the attenuverter's twice-wiper coefficient gives **2.171 mV** nominal sampled output error and **2.489 mV** at candidate tolerance corners. These exclude track linearity, contact noise, amplifier offsets, temperature drift and source/return errors. Midpoint Rth and full-scale voltage are not combined as an attainable common operating point. The offset's existing 10 nF filter gives a nominal maximum simple time constant about259 μs; intended modulation response must be compared to this transfer, not an arbitrary flat20kHz criterion.

The candidate independent0.1% R7101/R7102 tolerance corners give REFN5 inversion gain0.998002..1.002002, or up to10.010mV error relative to ideal−5V before buffer/reference errors. This term is additional to the ideal-reference wiper sweeps; no matched-ratio or drift correlation is assumed.

REF5 and REFN5 are buffered branches. The ideal unloaded reference fanout is about0.641mA sourcing and0.600mA sinking, with actual pot-position loading retained separately. The raw REF5050 output feeds the buffer input; these fanout loads are not directly assigned to its raw output. The recovered exact TI REF50xxAI table supports conditioned ±10mA operating output and1.2mA maximum full-temperature quiescent current. It does not qualify the assembled circuit: C7102's effective capacitance/ESR and the input bypass application remain unresolved in the identity report. Buffered GATE_REF is only connected to U7104's own feedback in A01; ADG enables instead use +5V through1kΩ/100kΩ.

The81-state arithmetic retains positive and negative inputs, offset and attenuator positions, including overrail mathematical demands. Its largest **incomplete** AGND magnitude demand subtotal is about7.947mA (about6.555mA when the ideal output lies inside supply rails, which still does not establish linear headroom), without current cancellation or equal-sharing credit. All IC ground currents, reference/summer/remote-buffer load currents, clipping, startup, faults and externally imposed returns are not bounded. Quiescent amplifier current between rails is not automatically AGND current. The rail tables are explicitly incomplete conservative magnitudes, not simultaneous signed KCL solutions.

Reference/offset errors, noise, slew and comparator timing govern that behavior; pitch-error limits do not apply to digital edges. No universal300mm rule or short-path stability claim is used.
