# OPAx196 model evidence and analytical refinement

The official [TI SBOMAF9 archive](https://www.ti.com/lit/zip/SBOMAF9) is available and inspected. Its plaintext `OPAx196.LIB` identifies Final1.5, dated29AUG2022, and explicitly covers OPA196, OPA2196 and OPA4196. The five macro terminals are `IN+ IN- VCC VEE OUT`. Source binding maps all120 captured OPA4196IDR channels, including unused channels, to their actual package pins and supplies. No OPA4197, comparator, HC or AS3340 model is substituted.

Archive SHA-256: `9c264813a36536f743c166b6a101904b76ed08f049f2eeb127a438cc6e8ccdf5`. Library SHA-256: `31142f5dbd71b758d887ee8a411692fc4c1309c8f47100af4ba5bed7e173c430`. The archive and library are in the ignored `.circuit-cache/sources/architecture278-SBOMAF9/` cache. `opax196-evidence.json` records every archive member hash, inspected source lines, the model interface and limitations. Parameter values are parsed from the retained model lines, and offline reproduction rejects disagreement between those lines and the numeric evidence fields. The generator's `--verify-archive` independently reproduces that evidence from exact bytes and rejects a changed archive instead of silently updating hashes. Offline `--check` uses the committed inspection record and does not pretend the raw cache exists elsewhere.

This closes the manufacturer-family **source availability** gap. Simulator dialect compatibility, convergence, reproduction of TI reference cases, validation against the actual application and any separately required native admission remain open. PSpice behavioral functions, VSWITCH and temperature/noise constructs were inspected, not run. TI's model is supplied as-is. It models individual channels; package thermal/crosstalk and assembled interconnect behavior are not qualified by its existence.

The two-node transfer is `Vsense/Videal = 1/(a*s²+b*s+c)`, with `R0=Rpot*x*(1-x)`, `a=R0*Rs*Cwire*Cfilter`, `b=(R0+Rs)*Cfilter+R0*(1+Rs/Rfail)*Cwire`, and `c=1+(R0+Rs)/Rfail`. Closed-form roots and stationary-point DC calculations are ordinary analytical arithmetic, not simulator execution. The report retains zero-wire-capacitance and endpoint-position single-pole limits, and tests compare the stationary-point maximum with a dense position scan.

The manufacturer's source declares modeling of open-loop gain/phase, frequency-dependent output impedance, load response, slew, bias/offset and related behavior. Those declarations support preparing an exact-model characterization later; they provide no measured phase margin or current guarantee here. The macro's internal100Ω input resistors and100MΩ differential resistor must not be mistaken for board resistors or guaranteed device input impedance. Stage5's conditional guaranteed-data sensitivities, unknown worst-case OPA4196 specifications, nine aggregate shared-capacitance obligations and five selected1k/100nF switching loads remain separate. The shared reference drivers are OPA4197, so this OPAx196 acquisition does not resolve their25mA idealized switching-demand analysis.
