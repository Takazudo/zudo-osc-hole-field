# Minimum physical measurement checklist

All items **NOT RUN**. Unpowered mechanical/inspection checks only; no energization is part of this list.

| ID | Where | Method | Acceptance | Record |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M-01 | Both connectors, unsoldered samples | Print the candidate drilling patterns 1:1 (Stage10 labeled and proper-handed candidate) on paper or a blank board; set an unsoldered sample of each part on the component side as viewed in the drawing; photograph pin-1 markings against labels | Physical a1/c1/a32 of each part land on the labeled positions of the chosen assignment; female fixing holes align without force; record on which side of row b the female fixing holes sit (row a or row c) | part lot, pattern variant, photo IDs, observed a1/c1/a32 positions, fixing-hole fit, fixing-hole offset side |
| M-02 | A01-middle and A01-core bare boards at the DIN pattern | Pin gauges or optical measurement on first-article bare boards, all 96 holes per board, before assembly | Every contact hole finished diameter within 0.90-1.10 mm (go 0.90 / no-go 1.10 pin gauges), unless Q-M02 changes the interval | board serial, hole label, go/no-go result, gauge IDs |
| M-03 | Same boards as M-02 | CMM or calibrated optical measurement of all 96 hole centres; best-fit grid; report radial deviations | Each hole centre within 0.025 mm radial of the best-fit basic 2.54 grid (Ø0.05 zone), unless Q-M03 states a datum | board serial, method, max radial deviation, per-hole table |
| M-04 | A01-middle female fixing holes | Pin gauges | Both fixing holes 2.80-2.90 mm (go 2.80 / no-go 2.90) | board serial, hole, result |
| M-05 | A01-middle | CMM/optical, same setup as M-03 | Centre distance 90 ±0.1; symmetry within 0.1 to the contact pattern; offset 0.3 ±0.1 from row b on the side confirmed by Q-M04 | measured centre distance, symmetry, offset and side |
| M-06 | A01-middle and A01-core at the DIN | Micrometer at 4 points around the DIN pattern | Within the thickness range from Q-M06/Q-A02 | board serial, 4 readings |
| M-07 | Assembled middle + core pair | Unpowered mated stack on its spacers; feeler/height gauge at both ends of the connector | Mated mounting-surface distance within the Q-M07 window (catalogue 18.3-20 mm; Stage10 19.2 mm); full mating without board bow | spacer stack lot, distance at both ends, board bow |
| M-08 | Assembled stack in carrier | Force gauge on core handles for insertion and 40 mm withdrawal, unpowered; inspect joints afterwards | Force within manufacturer limit (Q-M08); no solder-joint cracking; carrier deflection recorded | peak insertion N, peak withdrawal N, cycles, joint inspection |
| M-09 | All 34 conductors | On the unpowered mechanical mock with all boards seated, lay a flexible tape along the intended path from seated housing exit to seated header (GH) or land to land (tails); measure with the rear core in place and, for links staying connected during service, at the service position | Measured path length + service slack &gt;= straight distance; chosen cut length documented per conductor; bend radius not below wire maker limit | conductor ID, path description, measured length mm, slack mm, minimum bend radius, photo ID |
| M-10 | Eight GH headers | Attempt each GH housing on each other header by hand without tools, unpowered | No housing of another link can be mated or partially mated to a wrong header; 1:1 reversal of each GH cable is harmless | housing, header, result |
| M-11 | Each harness and each assembled stack | Fixture-based continuity and isolation test against harness-ledger.json; mated DIN tested across the stack | Continuity for every ledger conductor and assigned DIN contact; no continuity between any two different nets; insulation resistance at the agreed test voltage meets the agreed limit | harness serial, conductor ID, resistance, isolation matrix, IR value and voltage |
| M-12 | A01 stack in carrier | Service rehearsal on the unpowered mock following Stage10 service procedure | Rear core withdraws 40 mm after K-PWR disconnection without wire tension or snagging; jack/control service can disconnect J-SIG/J-PWR/C-SIG through the planned access; no harness crosses a mount tool path or connector release sweep | step, observed interference, photo ID |
