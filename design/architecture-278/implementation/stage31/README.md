## Routes are modelled as filleted envelopes, not physical clearance

- **Shape:** each cable leaves its housing exit along the housing axis and keeps a straight
  lead. Every waypoint corner then turns with a 5 mm circular arc.
  - Length, clearance and the cable gap are computed on the arcs, sampled every 0.2 mm. The
    sampling deducts 0.1 mm (0.2 mm cable to cable), so the exact spare is up to that much
    larger.
  - The 5 mm radius is the Stage30 assumption (5 × OD); JST states no bend radius.
- **Envelope:** round bundles of 1.0 mm OD wire, the JST GH maximum. The J-SIG bundle radius
  is 1.5 mm (7 wires, 3 × OD) and J-PWR is 1.207 mm (4 wires, (1 + √2) × OD). A flat leg is
  n side-by-side wires.
- **Margin:** routes keep a 0.5 mm surface margin to every known keep-out and to the other
  cable. Release sweeps keep the same 0.5 mm to known obstacles and to foreign XY. The
  keep-outs are the Stage30 classes plus mount spacers and screw heads.
- **Foreign depth:** the Stage30 cap (z 34) is dropped. For each foreign part under a route or
  a sweep, the study reports the highest top z that part may reach. Foreign PCB outlines are
  not known; only per-part XY is.
- **Release:** the housing moves 15 mm along its axis (Stage10). The nearest loop top or hook
  moves just far enough to keep the lead and an r 5 bend. Design length = installed + the
  largest release extra + 10 mm service slack. It is not a measured or cut length.
- **Rail pieces:** they are named by y range, e.g. `rail:middle:0:y173.8-180.9`. `:N1:floor`
  is the rail left under notch N1.

## Recommended: B, middle headers moved and turned 90°, with two shallow rail notches

B is the only candidate that meets all four criteria ([design-input.json](design-input.json)).
- **Header moves:**
  - J-SIG:middle moves from (260, 150) to (268.0, 171.5) and J-PWR:middle from (283, 150) to
    (288.0, 174.7). Both turn 90°, so the pin rows run along y.
  - Each hook then bends about its pin-row axis, so all wires of a cable share the bend
    radius.
  - Both release sweeps lie in the strip between the J*10 jacks and the SW*01 switches. They
    stay at least 0.575 mm from every known obstacle and foreign XY, the closest being
    SW1701. The two sweeps are 9.9 mm apart.
  - Nets, pins, MPNs, board and board face are unchanged.
- **Rail notches in `rail:middle:0`:** proposal, not adopted.
  - N1 (J-SIG): y 168.6–173.8, top relieved to z 43.7, 2.3 mm deep.
  - N2 (J-PWR): y 180.9–185.1, top relieved to z 44.2, 1.8 mm deep.
  - The rail keeps 9.7 and 10.2 mm of its 12 mm depth (local inertia ratio 0.53 / 0.61).
  - Each notch is the minimum the route envelope needs plus 0.3 mm, and the generator checks
    this. Rail strength is NOT_CALCULATED.
- **Routes:**
  - Each cable makes a free loop left of the A01-middle edge and runs just under A01-middle.
  - J-SIG shifts from y 164.4 to 171.5 between x 234 and 240. Each cable crosses its notch,
    then drops and hooks up into its header.
  - J-SIG has 8 bends and J-PWR 7, all at r 5. The straight lead is 7.5 / 7.6 mm at the local
    end and 2.0 mm at the middle end.
  - Clearance:
    - The minimum surface clearance is 0.70 mm (J-SIG, notch N1) and 0.69 mm (J-PWR,
      A01-jack M4 tool column), against 0.5 required. That is 0.19 mm spare after the
      deduction, against the 0.15 mm K4 threshold.
    - The cables are at least 5.69 mm apart.
    - The core motion box is at least 2.39 mm away.
- **Design lengths:**
  - Installed: J-SIG 108.7 mm and J-PWR 119.6 mm.
  - Release extra: +15.0 mm (middle), +4.0 / +3.8 mm (local) and +19.0 / +18.8 mm (both).
  - Proposals: J-SIG 138 mm and J-PWR 149 mm (Stage10 cut 90 ± 3). The extra length has to
    sit in the free local loops (M-12).
- **What B still requires:**
  - Only J-PWR passes over foreign parts: SW1401, SW1501 and SW1601 must stay below z 44.49,
    i.e. 22.9 mm above their PCB rear.
  - During a 15 mm middle release the hook envelope dips to about z 17.6, through the
    foreign PCB plane (z 20–21.6), whose outline is unknown. A shorter real unmating travel
    (M31-07) reduces both this dip and the lengths.
  - Wire OD ≤ 1.0 mm.
  - A bend radius of 5 mm or less: the waypoints admit no larger uniform radius.
  - Retention: a saddle and strap in each notch (R1/R2), dimensions null.
- **Copper (allocation model only):**
  - The 19 copper edges at the moved headers change by −31.3 to +2.1 mm.
  - 6 signal paths get 20.9–26.9 mm shorter, and 28 power/return paths get 23.0–31.3 mm
    shorter.
  - Routed copper is NOT RUN.

## A and C rejected on their criteria

- **A (headers kept, notches N1 y 162.1–166.7 and N2):** REJECTED_BY_CRITERIA (K2, K3). Its
  routes clear the modelled keep-outs, but:
  - Both Stage10 middle release sweeps overlap foreign J1509/J1510/J1609/J1610 and
    J1709/J1710 XY.
  - Its hooks need D1510 below z 32.0, D15101 below z 32.1 and J1710 below z 32.3, i.e.
    10.4–10.7 mm above the foreign PCB rear.
- **C (headers translated to (265.0, 165.0) / (283.0, 174.2), flat single-layer wires through
  the existing 2.4 mm slot, rail unchanged):** REJECTED_BY_CRITERIA (K4).
  - The slot leaves 0.7 mm per side, 0.2 mm beyond the margin, or 0.1 mm after the sampling
    deduction. A liner, tape or OD tolerance would use it up.
  - The fan-out and gather of the flat layer are not modelled, and the slot cannot be
    enlarged without a rail change, which is B.

## Stage30 candidates stay rejected

The Stage31 checks reject W1–W4 again:
- All four: the first 5 mm bend does not fit behind the J-PWR local housing (2.1 mm leg), and
  there is no J-SIG route.
- W1 also hits the rail, W2 the rail and A01-middle, and W3 needs foreign parts below the
  12.4 mm allowance.

The straight proxies of all 11 J-SIG/J-PWR conductors still cross `rail:middle:0`.
