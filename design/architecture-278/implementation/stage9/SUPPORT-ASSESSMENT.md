# Elevated X1 support comparison — authored source assessment

This assessment accompanies my authored support generator/input; independent review is supplied by the root and code/electrical reviewers. It is one proposed arrangement, not hardware acceptance.

The 49 nonfixed packages retain Stage8 XY, rotations, pins and units on two continuous substrates at Z35/36.6. Seven fixed packages retain their original nominal terminal datums and centres. Three 6 mm square substrates support the actual 0402 LEDs. Fifteen permanent factory wires reach independent elevated-board lands; three jack TN terminals remain NC. There are no added mating interfaces. The complete jack group and selector are two separately replaceable factory subassemblies, each using its two existing service ports.

The main boards need no foreign-pin precision passages. LED islands reserve 0.4 mm ordinary outline boundary allowance and retain 0.3 mm landing-to-edge margin. Three island/jack body projections remain explicitly UNKNOWN because courtyard projections do not specify the actual body depth/profile. All other hardware depth beneath the proposed carrier must be established; the modeled requirement is a maximum Z29 mm, not an observed dimension.

Backing ribs route around the actual panel-side package courtyards, including D3311. Four developed force routes are 9.5–39 mm. The proposed 5 mm wide carrier occupies Z29.5–34, with insulating contact shoes at Z34–35. Permanent wire approaches pass through explicit guide bores: nominal diameter1.2 mm, proposed ±0.2 diameter and0.3 radial location allowance produce a2 mm maximum material-removal envelope and1 mm minimum opening. Wire insulation/insert fit, machining acceptance and retention remain unselected. The neighboring bore envelopes leave at least0.54 mm control and1 mm jack ligament; these reduced widths, rather than gross rib width, enter the planning model.

At the named5 N load and retained70 GPa aluminum modulus, developed-length cantilever surrogates give maximum0.186 mm deflection and64.472 MPa bending stress, below the proposed0.5 mm/80 MPa planning screens. These are not qualification of tortuous-frame joints, torsion, bore stress concentrations, clamp bearing, or a minimum universal ligament criterion. Exact split-saddle geometry must retain all three jacks/LED holders as a complete carrier group; currently only the four port-to-reaction routes are numerically screened. Positive reaction is conditional on actual bushing/shoulder/thread engagement. Header solder/hold-down integrity is not proven by backing the PCB.

All15 terminal wires use explicit Z27 orthogonal doglegs and35±3 mm cuts, with minimum10.95 mm proposed slack. Actual tail solder positions and neighbors at that plane remain unverified. The15 existing service wires are recalculated using new header padXYZ and four0/15 mm unplug cases; minimum proxy slack is23.381 mm. Actual wire exits and bend/strain envelopes remain unqualified.

The four new middle windows provide connector/latch access only. Routine replacement requires power-off/discharge, rear-cover and obstructing rear-card removal, access through those windows, and opening/removing the proposed removable front panel before releasing fixed-hardware nuts and withdrawing the complete supported unit. No assembly is claimed to pass through a jack hole or connector window; no middle covers are assumed. Other-function harness support and clearance during panel opening remain required. Ordinary OSC calibration still does not require middle removal.

Fourteen focused tests pass. All ten modeled collision/exclusion lists are empty and fail closed; mutations reject shortened wires, omitted guide/outline allowance, undersized LED substrate, blocked carrier routing and the earlier DIN-window collision. Native KiCad, ERC/DRC, simulation and physical tests were NOT RUN. Next evidence is the exact fixed-hardware clamp stack and factory terminal/carrier construction, followed by complete service solids—not another precision-cutout assumption.
