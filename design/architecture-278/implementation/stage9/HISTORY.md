# Enclosure and service authority audit

**The retained 318 × 298 mm dimensions describe the front panel. No accepted numeric inner or outer enclosure envelope was found in the reviewed history.** This does not establish unlimited free volume. A wider or deeper rear enclosure must still be proposed, supported and checked against actual neighboring functions, assembly and service.

The evidence ledger separates explicit USER decisions, current issue acceptance obligations, engineering proposals and unknowns. GitHub posts made through the repository owner’s account are not treated as direct human approvals merely because of the account name. Exact excerpts, immutable commit/file/line links, retrieved issue-body hashes and update timestamps are in `history-evidence.json`.
