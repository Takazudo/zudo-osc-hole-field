# Unpowered measurement and decision checklist

Do not fabricate or order from this study. Use existing loose parts/unpowered
mock-up only after parent chooses to investigate the failed interface. Record
instrument, datum, uncertainty, min/max, photographs and both assembly directions.
No energization, forced mating, shortened cable or enclosure modification is authorized.

1. **GH capture first:** obtain manufacturer-approved latch displacement, housing
   keeper bearing faces, extraction/mating force limits and XY/angular capture
   window for exact 4/7-way parts. Record actual housing/PCB positions and latch
   tool space. **Stop** if keeper force would pass through wires/crimps, latches
   cannot all release while supported, or limits remain unknown.
2. **Registration/bow:** measure four exits relative to proposed A/B/C in both
   supported fits, including signed board profile and local tangent angle.
   Each-axis translation must stay within ±1 mm and rotation within ±0.5°;
   signed bow within ±1.282127 mm. Those allocations alone do not permit mating:
   combined relative errors must also fit the documented GH capture window.
3. **Guide motion:** record endpoint trajectory throughout 0..15 mm and reverse,
   with worst supported load and all keepers retained. Required proposed path
   deviation ≤0.25 mm including uncertainty. C36's nominal play already permits
   2.044 mm; do not count a favorable hand-held sample as a tolerance pass.
4. **Surroundings:** survey each analysis.json neighbor obligation against its
   locked XY footprint; include board, tails, components and harness. Record z
   maxima with uncertainty and clearance. Any proposed cap below the source board
   lower surface is a repartition decision, not a pass. Measure actual cover
   aperture, ribs, fasteners and 105 mm inner face; no drawing is assumed.
5. **Full sequence/access:** demonstrate K-PWR-first and supported core40 path
   with cassette/roots installed; one-at-a-time screw/stud replacement with D6
   tool clearance; supported common15 withdrawal. Do not attempt +75 removal: the D6 stud head
   cannot pass the closed D4.4 bushing and stops it at +32 mm. A later separately
   authorized disassembly interface would need assessment before full removal/reverse.
   Record continuous minimum clearance against 0.5 mm plus complete tolerance
   stack and K4≥0.15 mm, including new internal keeper/sleeve/tool interfaces.
   Stop on any interference, unsupported end, inaccessible latch or forced fit.
6. **Structure/wire:** supply exact root material/joint and fastener ratings,
   post/thread/pullout evidence meeting50 N; measure/validate mounting stiffness.
   Identify wire/crimp/sleeve MPN, maker repeated-flex radius, straight exit,
   finished/cut/strip tolerances and current/temperature data before selecting wire.
   R12 is a geometric target only; no current or repeated-flex qualification here.

Minimal next decision: accept C36 as a failed concept and stop, or supply the GH
capture/keeper interface and required relative registration contract for a later
separately authorized study. There is no source-only reason to select another
mechanism now. Unknown neighbor caps can be reconciled independently once actual
board/cover drawings exist, without claiming C36 service is feasible.
