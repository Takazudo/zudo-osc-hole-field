## What primary drawings resolve

3M TS0770 Rev U (2026-03-04), sheet2, controls dimensions in inches; metric annotations are reference. The .390 inch header height ends at the molded seating feet. Do **not** add the .01 inch standoff note again. Cavity floor is .390−.253 = 3.4798 mm nominal above a PCB seated against those feet; independent default tolerances give 3.2258–3.7338 mm. Pin exposure above that floor is 6.0452–6.2992 mm. TS0449 Rev M, sheet3, requires at least 5.842 mm: necessary exposure surplus is 0.2032 mm. No maximum socket insertion or actual contact seating depth is established.

The 02 tail is .112±.011 inch from the same feet plane: 2.5654–3.1242 mm below the PCB top. At **assumed nominal** 1.6 mm PCB thickness, protrusion is 0.9654–1.5242 mm; thickness tolerance and solder fillet are not included. The screen does not confuse the entire tail with protrusion below the board underside.

Worst-case drawing arithmetic leaves total cavity/body width gap 0.0508 mm, total length gap 0.3302 mm for each count, and bump/notch width gap 0.254 mm. These are necessary dimensional comparisons, **not a guaranteed mating fit**: alignment, key depth, seating, pin position, molding and contact travel remain dependencies. Parenthesized header pitch/pin-width references do not become tolerance guarantees.

TS0449 sheet2 shows the closed 891 socket without optional strain relief at **(.41 inch) [10.5 mm], both reference**. The exact inch conversion is 10.414 mm; the annotation differs by 0.086 mm. Preserve both rather than silently choosing a guaranteed maximum. If the socket mating face reached the header cavity floor, its conditional reference top would be 13.8938 mm above PCB. This is not a qualified maximum, nor evidence that the socket bottoms on that floor. Cable-exit center height and straight-exit requirement remain undimensioned. Compared with the previous open 18.3 mm Würth model, this is a better documented closed-state reference, not proof of a smaller installed envelope.

The selected `-0101` sockets omit optional strain relief; `-0103` is a different configuration. Omission removes the earlier open-relief-model ambiguity but transfers strain management to an **unresolved external cable clamp/support**. Never pull the cable to unplug. Exact ribbon selection, clamp location, cut tolerance, bend radius and repeated-flex performance remain open; neither a generic ribbon sketch nor historical rolling-flex data supplies these limits. The socket supports maker-specified 26/28 AWG stranded termination; that alone does not select this instrument's wire or establish current capacity. No new current allowance is credited.

This is a standard-family candidate rather than a custom cable connector. A read-only distributor snapshot showed D2510-6002-AR at $1.80 and 89110-0101 at $1.32 each; these are dated observations, not quotes or guaranteed stock for all counts. Another distributor called the 10-way socket discontinued, so lifecycle/availability remains to confirm. Samtec IDSD/HCSD catalog alternatives carry nonstandard/nonreturnable cautions and reference heights; HCSD also lacks the required 8/14 counts. No procurement occurred.

## Service screen and explicit failures

`generate.py` reads the prior bound source geometry, substitutes toleranced header rectangles, retains all other known obstacles and all 85 occupied/unknown neighbor projections. Other candidate connector columns remain occupied to the cover because their maximum heights are unknown. The 1 mm XY allowance, 1 mm vertical clearance, extra 2 mm housing-grip width and 15 mm withdrawal are **planning reservations**, not measured carrier registration, bow tolerance, ergonomic clearance or maker disengagement requirements. Full cable and tool sweeps remain unmodeled. The numerical ceilings are necessary conditions only; null actual maximum height always prevents a fit verdict.

- **J17902:** installed height ceiling 23 mm, but only **8 mm** above-board height is available during 15 mm withdrawal with the core still installed. That is below even the header's approximately 9.9 mm height and the conditional 13.8938 mm assembly reference. This proposed straight withdrawal cannot be accepted. Proposed sequence: remove/park the core first, then support the middle PCB and grip the C-SIG housing; core disengagement, lifting clearance and parking still need their own full sweep before accepting this sequence. No new board extension or connector move is silently adopted.
- **J17901:** the symmetric grip/withdrawal reservation intersects the proposed control side supports (necessary ceiling −9.6 mm). Reject this grip scheme. A narrower purpose-designed housing tool or local support revision is a future proposal requiring access and stiffness checks; the candidate does not claim fingers can reach it.
- Other four connector ceilings in the 15 mm trial are 39 mm on the middle and 52.4 mm on the jack board. These are not passes: actual assembly bounds, nearby unknown geometry, cable routing and grip remain unresolved.
- No known tail/support intersections occur under the stated nominal PCB and XY assumptions. Solder/board-bow allowances and unknown neighbors are excluded from that limited result. Unknown projected references are enumerated per connector, including RV1408 at the control grip and RV1608/1708 at J17902. Retained carrier conflicts/unknowns are copied unchanged.

The existing inner cover remains z105. No deeper cover is proposed from an unbounded cable route. This candidate neither repairs nor overrides Stage35's earlier discrete-wire findings. Panel artwork is independent of internal connector work, but fixed hardware's unknown installed depth still blocks fit conclusions.

## Unpowered mock-up decision checklist

1. Verify exact 8/10/14 part labels, socket option and actual ribbon construction. Compare pin1/key faces with generated coordinates; check every numbered conductor and all isolated spares with continuity measurement. Any swapped, shorted or untraceable pin rejects the assembly.
2. With headers fully seated, record PCB thickness, seating gap, tail protrusion and solder height. Record highest closed socket Z, exit center/direction and variation for each exact count, including assembly tolerance. A nominal reading alone cannot establish a production maximum. Enter the conservative maximum into the generated necessary ceilings; exceedance rejects that reservation.
3. Obtain actual cable maker static and repeated-flex constraints, straight housing-exit allowance and permitted termination/clamp practice. Until these exist, do not set cut lengths or call a 15 mm cable withdrawal feasible. Trial external strain support while gripping the housing; no load may transfer to IDC conductors during service. Force and cycle acceptance values need maker/design authority, not invented thresholds.
4. Measure carrier registration and board bow in installed and supported-unplugging states; replace the planning allowances with defensible bounded values and recompute. Record occupied rear-seat/tail/harness Z for the specific neighbor refs in JSON. Unknown remains occupied; any overlap rejects the affected branch.
5. Reject core-installed J17902 withdrawal and the current control grip. Test a separately documented core-first sequence with all fasteners/tools, supported free end and parking volume. Sweep the complete assembly and cable, not only endpoints. Then test jack-board removal with remaining supports and ribbons. No unsupported PCB bending or pulling on ribbon is acceptable.
6. Keep the existing 50 N support target; demonstrate load path/retention by justified calculation and later unpowered testing. No stiffness or strength pass follows from this study. Electrical verification, energization, manufacturing checks and release remain separate and NOT RUN here.

Next cloud-capable work: design a bounded control-housing tool/support alternative and core-first access sequence using these failures, then recompute against the same occupied/unknown neighbors. Actual exit/ribbon data is required only for the dependent cable route, not for independent source work.
