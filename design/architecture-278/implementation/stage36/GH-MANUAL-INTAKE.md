## Resolved evidence and limits

- **Applicability:**2149 p2 names GHR-**V-S, SSHL-002T-P0.2 and BM**B-GHS-TBT
  (LF)(SN)(N); the wildcard denotes circuit count, encompassing04/07. The table
  prints an additional(N); do not silently equate every suffix.2111 pp2/9 likewise
  covers GHR circuit-count variants/contact and BM/SM family mates. These are GH
  family handling instructions, not separate toleranced04/07 drawings or proof
  that a particular delivered part revision is identical.
- **Mating contact area:**2149 p5 Figs1–2 calls for distributed pressure across the
  socket housing, aligned to the header; loading just the ends can bow a long
  housing. The drawing illustrates the load distribution, not dimensioned
  bearing pads, allowable pressure or a positively captured extraction ledge.
- **Latch/unmating:**2149 p6 Figs3–4 shows depressing the latch protrusion fully
  before withdrawal. All wires must share the grip; pulling only edge wires can
  pry the connector even when the nominal pull direction is axial. No latch
  displacement, actuator force or withdrawal stroke is specified.
- **Grip orientation:**2149 p7 Fig5 and2111 p10 photographs distinguish squeezing
  the wire row from its two broad faces from gathering wires inward along the
  pitch direction. The latter is discouraged. The page's word 'vertical' is
  figure-relative, not permission to assume a global enclosure axis. No numeric
  grip length, jaw geometry or permissible clamping pressure is established.
- **Angle:**2149 p6 and2111 p10 specify withdrawal within20° of the mating axis,
  aiming straight, after complete unlocking. This GH-specific instruction
  resolves the earlier generic15° evidence gap. Existing study15° is retained
  as a stricter planning bound; neither value is a lateral capture tolerance,
  a guaranteed initial mating misalignment nor a reason to relax geometry.
- **Routing/support:**2149 p8 Fig6 requires avoiding bend-induced load at the
  connector and restraining wiring movement that would disturb contacts.2111
  p8 places harness ties at least30mm away in its assembly/bundling context.
  That is not a demonstrated sleeve clearance or a repeated-flex qualification.
- **Forces:**2111 p4 gives crimp tensile acceptance by gauge (26/28/30AWG:
  minimum20/10/5N). These are crimp-quality test thresholds, **not** connector
  unmating force, permitted service tension, housing/keeper strength or the50N
  structural criterion. Neither manual supplies numerical connector mating/
  extraction force, latch-force or keeper-load limits.

## Sequential service and the next bounded decision

Simultaneous four-end motion was C36's assumption. Stage34 design-input explicitly
sets one end at a time and describes middle-then-local harness replacement;
Stage33's 'all four ends' refers to rear accessibility, not simultaneity. The new
manuals support controlled individual connector handling; they do not require
four housings to move together. C36's guide-play and head/bore removal failures
remain unchanged, and its proposed positive keepers remain unqualified.

**Proposed next scope, for parent review only:** assess one sequential single-end
service operation using the maker-described full-row wire grip and complete
latch release, preserving support of the opposite end and both bend regions.
First reconcile actual hand/tool access and support handoff with continuous
0–15mm withdrawal and reverse motion, slack, the retained R12 target, unknown
neighbors and K-PWR-before-core40 sequence. Include eventual guide removal and
jack-board tool access. Stop if it needs unbounded grip forces, an undefined
bearing interface or unsupported wire motion. No mechanism or new geometry is
selected by this intake; these instructions do not establish a feasible design.

Still needed: exact04/07 toleranced housing/header drawings and suffix confirmation;
latch travel, useful grip/access dimensions and stroke from authorized data or
an unpowered measurement; manufacturer force/load guidance before force-based
fixture qualification; selected wire/repeated-flex evidence, registration/bow,
neighbor/cover dimensions and structural proof. The manuals are sufficient to
correct handling assumptions and scope a later source study, not qualify hardware.
