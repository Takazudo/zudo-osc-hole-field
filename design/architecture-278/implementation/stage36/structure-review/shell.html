Visible layers

Earlier cards are wireframes from revision 2, not a reconciled whole-instrument layout. Enabling them does not establish clearance.

What is established?

Panel XY is locked: 180 jacks, 144 controls and 114 LEDs. Marker diameters are illustrative. All internal geometry shown is unadopted design-study data. Board rectangles are outer envelopes: holes, access notches and detailed supports are omitted.

Front A01: control z10–11.6; jack z35–36.6. Middle z48.4–50. Rear core z69.2–70.8 mm. Positive depth is behind the panel. Panel XY is a coordinate projection viewed from +z, preserving x-right/y-down; it is not a front-face photograph.

Important omissions

The rest of the front/middle/rear board system is incomplete. Empty-looking space is unknown, not free.

R33 J-SIG/J-PWR paths retain 133/149 mm design lengths and R12. Retention, wire and service remain unresolved. C36 is rejected and is not displayed; RG34 and shorter-cable alternatives are not adopted.

C-SIG/K-PWR routing, other function boards, component population, protection/inlet and enclosure solids are omitted. The 320 × 319 × 105 mm enclosure is a candidate requirement, not a rendered enclosure design. A01-control extends below the panel outline in the retained proposal.

Ordering

This view is not a fabrication or ordering release. Native fit, electrical acceptance and physical tests are not established.
