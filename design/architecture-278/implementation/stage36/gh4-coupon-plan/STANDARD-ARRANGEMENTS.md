## Verified rules and explicit limits

Official pages inspected2026-10-18; no vendor files redistributed:

- [JLC assembly capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities), Dimension/Order Volume/Edge Rails/Fiducials/Delivery Format: Standard single-board minimum70×70mm, panel minimum70×70mm and panel maximum250×250mm; published order volume2–80000pcs. Rails and fiducials required. Standard accepts mouse-bite and V-cut panels. Published normal reflow240±5°C remains a candidate, not a body-temperature guarantee.
- [JLC PCB Panelization](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-panelization), updated2026-09-09, V-cut rules1–8: adjacent subboards have zero spacing; cuts are straight horizontal/vertical and traverse the whole panel. V-cut requires at least70×70mm and is available for Standard assembly. Parallel cuts must be at least2mm apart. Panel-by-JLC with rails adds rail fiducials/tooling holes by default, not local fiducials on each coupon. Thus a frame cannot depend on blind/interrupted V-cuts.
- [Capability page depaneling FAQ](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities): assembled panels normally ship with rails attached; a panel-removal service can be selected later. Availability does not establish an acceptable separation method/load for this header.
- [JLC stencil fiducial guidance](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/how-to-include-fiducial-marks-on-an-smt-stencil), updated2026-09-01: PCB fiducials are copper features with mask clearance; stencil fiducials are separate process features, not normal paste apertures. This page does not supply a complete Standard rail/fiducial dimension contract.

**Still unknown:** exact acceptable rail width/placement, fiducial count/diameter/mask opening/edge setbacks, tooling-hole dimensions, whether the70mm minimum is evaluated including rails for the chosen order mode, local coupon fiducial need, tab/slot geometry, permitted residual web and cut/part/copper keepouts, supplier separation tooling/support, quantity units and selective-population policy. Do not fill these gaps from generic PCB practice. The5mm rail examples below are arithmetic assumptions, **not verified JLC rules**. Their equality to a published minimum has no dimensional/tolerance margin.

The array rows use **V-cut arithmetic only**. Routed mouse-bite arrays require nonzero routing lanes and tab/slot design; the dimensions cannot be transferred unchanged. Exact routing lane, tabs, placement and separation constraints are not established. Do not adopt routed tabs as a cure without supplier requirements. A large unbroken two-header board is also not substituted: it would mechanically link the endpoints and cease to be two independently clamped identical coupons.

For a large coupon, keep the header local access problem explicit: full-row broad-face grip, latch depression, near-axial withdrawal≤15° and uncluttered tool sweep still require actual parts/fixture measurements. Do not simply scale the30mm coordinates or move its header to an edge and claim clearance. Extra blank area can carry board-clamping regions and fiducials, but separated board support must react handling without levering joints. No qualified load credit is assigned to hold-down solder, regardless of area. Record whether rails are retained during measurement; never compare different fixtures as equivalent.
