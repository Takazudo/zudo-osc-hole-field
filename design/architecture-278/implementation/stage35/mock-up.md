# Unpowered mock-up: measure inputs before choosing a cable or cover

**NOT RUN.** This is a checklist for a separately authorized mock-up using available
parts, not permission to fabricate, order or energize. No values below are measured
results. Record revision, exact part markings, instruments/resolution, raw readings,
photos with scale, orientation, operator and uncertainty. A result within measurement
uncertainty of a limit is unresolved; do not round it into a pass.

| ID | Measurement and method | Decision criterion / consequence |
|---|---|---|
| M35-01 | Establish panel rear z0; measure actual local/middle housing exit XYZ and axes, rear-carrier datum, guide surfaces and cover inner face. Repeat carrier removal/reinstallation and six gravity orientations. Record pairwise vectors and rotations, not one maximum number. | Resolve whether the 1 mm allowance is radial, per-axis, pairwise or per-carrier. Feed measured extrema plus instrument/process uncertainty into the interval model. No support/cover pass until every required dimension lies within the chosen allocation. Rotation may not be set to zero without evidence. |
| M35-02 | On the supported unpowered A01-middle board, measure a grid including both headers, mounts and the interior; record upward/downward bow and header tilt relative to the mounting plane. | 1.282127 mm is a study allowance, not a measured bound. If either sign or local slope exceeds the implemented allocation, stop and recompute. Do not reuse the downward-only interval for upward bow. Jack-board bow and loaded mating deflection also need a bound. |
| M35-03 | Identify exact wire, contact and housing; measure insulation OD at several positions, dressed bundle envelope and conductor mapping. Record contact insertion, stripped length and crimp take-up separately for every conductor. Measure finished exit-to-exit path and the inherited +14.6 mm accounting surrogate with a traceable jig. | No raw cut dimension from 130 mm. Establish conversion and finished-length capability first. The full OD window must remain within JST 0.76–1.0 mm with uncertainty; bundle radius <=1.75 / 1.457107 mm is required for this study. Finished surrogate +/-0.5 mm must be demonstrated or the interval widened/recomputed. |
| M35-04 | With a transparent or open inspection fixture, measure every housing exit straight, fan-in transition and bend inner surface installed and at incremental 0–15 mm local/middle withdrawal. Use radius gauges or calibrated images; inspect all conductors, not just the bundle centre. | At least 10.389297 / 6.905617 mm straight/fan-in allocation and R12 bundle centreline are the model basis. Maker wire bend convention and real conductor mapping must agree. Never infer repeated-flex rating from static radius. Kinking or tighter bends stops the trial. |
| M35-05 | Set the former on the installed arch. Measure its gap, channel play, closed-lid/parting gaps and cable excursions in six orientations. During release, measure former displacement and gap versus housing travel, including reverse movement. | Installed setting gap <=0.25 mm, wall play <=0.3 mm and closure gap <=0.2 mm are assumptions to verify. A tracking design requires displacement t/2 over 0–15 mm with <=0.25 mm contact gap and positive support; a hand holding the arch is not that support. Stop if a sleeve opens an uncontrolled escape path. |
| M35-06 | Measure the effective supported span with the serviced sleeve withdrawn, latch side/release stroke, grip volume and actual hand/tool approach. Record swept volumes of sleeve, lid, latch tool and connector. | Preserve full 15 mm study travel unless separately justified. A tracking former alone is insufficient: the J-PWR open-span screen misses K4. Revise the sliding/split sleeve and recompute its whole sweep, including at t=0, before claiming guided service. No maker unmating force or allowable side load is inferred. |
| M35-07 | Map foreign board outlines, support planes, rear-side component heights and all wiring, and internal cover ribs/fasteners. Where inaccessible, keep an explicit unknown solid. | Every proposed cable/guide/access/removal envelope needs >=0.5 mm margin plus its full tolerance stack plus 0.15 mm K4. Unknown geometry is a hold, not a pass. Resolve the foreign-depth columns listed in analysis.json; a photograph without a datum/height is insufficient. |
| M35-08 | After a defined support/rest concept exists, rehearse unpowered assembly and reverse removal, recording the swept envelope of every guide part, tool and attached cable. Remove cover; disconnect K-PWR before the unchanged 40 mm core withdrawal. Verify controlled support before releasing any guide or harness end. | The core 40 mm path, fastener/tool access and rear aperture must all clear with the same margins. The jack M4 column cannot be used with the current guide in place. A required twist, forced bend, unsupported loose harness or undefined fastener path is a failure/hold. Reverse assembly must also work. |
| M35-09 | Measure cover inner surface and all internal projections relative to the rear carrier, including screw seating variation. Compare the entire cap/sleeve/channel envelope, not just one peak. | Keep z105 first. The inherited +1.211945 mm deeper-cover number is a height-only lower bound, not an order dimension. Recompute after datum/bow allocation; allow positive length/depth reserve. Any new cover must also pass aperture/removal and fastening checks. |
| M35-10 | Record selected guide material, root/joint geometry and planned hand loads. Establish an approved unpowered load-test method before applying loads. Measure deflection/permanent set and fastening slip under that method. | Current conditional post allowable 39.836642 N fails the 50 N sensitivity. Do not apply 50 N as an informal hand test or call the assembly qualified. Root overhang, joints, walls and fixation need analysis and separately approved testing; no stiffness is assumed. |
| M35-11 | Once a wire and a supported service fixture have been separately accepted for trials, record withdrawal/remating forces, insulation wear, pinching, contact seating and dimensions before/after the specified cycle schedule. | Cycle count, allowed force, wear and electrical/contact acceptance criteria are not established. Obtain primary repeated-flex/contact evidence and define those criteria before a qualification run. Visible damage, snagging or loss of support stops any exploratory unpowered trial. No energized test is included. |

Minimum inputs to resume a complete source solution: M35-01/02 datum and bow
bounds; M35-03/04 wire/path/cut convention; M35-06/07/08 actual access, neighbours,
fastener and aperture envelopes. M35-05/10/11 are needed before physical service
or strength claims. Source work can meanwhile design and review a tracking/split
sleeve mechanism, a support/rest sequence and a complete fastening interface;
its geometry remains a proposal until reconciled with those measurements.
