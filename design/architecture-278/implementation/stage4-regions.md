# Stage 4 strict-envelope sensitivity domains

These calculations preserve the historical complete-path 300 mm model for comparison. They do not establish that 300 mm is a physical failure threshold, nor that changing topology is required. See `stage4-requirements.md` for the definition and rationale review. No canonical limit or historic result is changed.

`stage4_regions.py` derives necessary endpoint octagons before searching placements. The octile norm is `max(abs(dx),abs(dy)) + (sqrt(2)-1)*min(abs(dx),abs(dy))`. A linked rear path requires `O(anchor-DIN)+O(DIN-rear) <= (300-front-63-15-25-14)/1.15`; a shared-reference path replaces the numerator by `300-25-14`. OCT paths have no DIN and require `O(header-shared) <= (300-front-63-15-7)/1.15`. Thus no connector allowance is accidentally charged to OCT and no moving shared OCT source is silently left at its old pad.

Each endpoint domain supplies eight explicit halfplanes. Translating a complete cluster intersects every endpoint's domain, preserving its current orientation and internal placements. Free-connector domains use the triangle inequality; surviving polygons are necessary only. Fixed-connector domains are exact for the scalar path allocation, still without geometry or electrical qualification. Empty-domain witnesses retain exact actual identities and inclusion-minimal sets of conflicting rows, not claimed minimum-cardinality proofs.

For both baseline and bypass-refined variants every free-connector cluster domain is nonempty. Current fixed connectors make baseline O4/fm-pwm and O5/fm-pwm infeasible under the strict model; refined placement additionally makes O5/core-timing infeasible. These are conditional results with the other endpoints fixed.

`connector_translation_domain` supplies the complementary exact continuous scalar domain: each two-focus octile inequality is expanded into 64 halfplanes, intersected across all 39 moving rows of each oscillator, with the current rear pads/common pin assignment fixed and the shared source translated +6 mm in X. O1 and O2 domains are nonempty and already contain zero translation. O3, O4 and O5 are empty for both placement variants; each has a single-row triangle-inequality witness. Connector-only motion therefore cannot close those fixed rear placements under this historical strict model. This is not proof that such hardware is unsafe or universally infeasible.

Translating all 30 shared packages +6 mm in X recalculates all 65 shared paths, including the 30 octave rows. Baseline maximum becomes 253.875510 mm and refined 260.024593 mm, with no shared path exceeding 300 mm. Overall maxima and counts remain 334.018298 mm/38 and 336.198722 mm/50. All nine shared aggregate capacitances remain unknown, including the distinct five selected 1 kohm/100 nF receiver dynamics. Bodies, mounting, supports, returns, clearance and service volumes still require independent checking.

The proposed no-regression comparison is engineering study guidance, not a new acceptance requirement: retain every baseline rear nonrail pair span, bypass supply leg and dedicated return two-leg sum componentwise. The refined variant worsens some spans and two supply legs; an improved global bypass maximum cannot offset those losses. Rigid transforms preserve within-cluster metrics but not intercluster connections. The full comparison remains in the JSON.

API: `endpoint_domain(row, connector=None)`, `translation_domain(rows, moving='rear'|'shared', connector_by_instance=None)`, `connector_translation_domain(rows, shared_translation=(6,0))`, and `evaluate_paths(rows, other_pads, connector_pads, shared_translation=(0,0))`. The latter accepts all225 rows and dictionaries keyed by `signature(row)`; target OCT pads must include the shared translation. Identity, fixed front geometry and front budget mutations are rejected. It returns scalar lengths only, without contact injectivity, return or mechanical qualification.
