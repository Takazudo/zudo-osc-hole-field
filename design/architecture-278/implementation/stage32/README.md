## B32 is the recommended working design, conditional on a wire with a small bend radius

B32 keeps the Stage31 B topology and meets S1–S8 under the stated conditions. It is a proposal:
nothing is adopted.
- **Header moves:**
  - J-SIG:middle (260, 150) → (268.0, 171.5), turned 90°, as in B.
  - J-PWR:middle (283, 150) → **(288.0, 174.0)**, turned 90°. It moves 0.7 mm toward −y versus B, so
    its sweep gap to SW1701 grows from 0.575 to 1.275 mm.
- **Runs:**
  - Both runs drop to z 44.3 (J-SIG) and z 44.4 (J-PWR) under A01-middle; in B they were at z 46.0 and 46.3.
  - The J-PWR run moves to y 183.6 (B: y 183).
  - The J-SIG jog ends at x 240, so each cable is straight through its guide.
- **Guides R1/R2 in notches N1/N2 of `rail:middle:0`** (see the next section):
  - N1: y 168.8–174.2, floor z 41.7, 4.3 mm deep; the rail keeps 7.7 of 12 mm.
  - N2: y 181.2–186.0, floor z 42.1, 3.9 mm deep; the rail keeps 8.1 mm.
  - The notches follow from the guide geometry and the tolerance stack.
- **Clearances:**
  - Static minimum 0.99 mm (required 0.5); the cables are 4.29 mm apart.
  - Core-motion clearance 3.35 mm.
  - Tightest robust spare 0.30 mm after the worst-case tolerance stack (K4 needs 0.15).
- **Design lengths:** J-SIG 136 mm and J-PWR 150 mm, from installed 106.7 / 121.2 mm, the 15 mm
  release extra (19.0 / 18.8 mm) and the 10 mm service slack. They are not cut lengths.
- **Conditions it still carries:** see "Open conditions" below. The first one, the bend radius, is
  not met by any wire identified so far.

## Stage31 B does not hold once tolerances are explicit: the 0.04 mm K4 margin was not real

Stage31 applied K4 to sampled clearance minus the 0.5 mm placement margin only. Stage32 keeps both
the 0.5 mm margin and the 0.15 mm K4 threshold. It then also subtracts an explicit worst-case
tolerance stack ([design-input.json](design-input.json) `tolerances`):
- A01-middle bow: a half sine of 0.75 % over the 143 mm M1–M3 span (IPC-6012 limit; the standard
  is not retained, and JLC states no bow value).
- Spacer length ±0.1, carrier faces ±0.1, saddle seat ±0.1 and strap ±0.05 (design assumptions).
- PCB hole position ±0.075 (JLC) and mount-hole float ±0.1.

Result:
- B31 (B as committed) fails: its cables sit 0.79–0.80 mm under A01-middle, and the bow alone
  takes 0.57–0.79 mm of that. Tightest robust spare is −0.69 mm (J-PWR under `board:A01-middle:1`).
- B31 also has no retention geometry; Stage31 R1/R2 were null.
- No value was rounded and no threshold was changed to reach this result. The tests check that the
  spare equals clearance − 0.5 − tolerance exactly.

## Retention R1/R2 is a captive guide that lets the bundle slide

Each guide is a saddle seated in its notch with an integral strap. Dimensions are in
`model.json` → `candidates.B32.guides`; the rules are in `design-input.json` `retention_rules`.
- **Saddle:**
  - 8.0 mm along the cable (rail 6 mm plus 1 mm overhang each side), with an end flare of R 2.0.
  - Seat radius = bundle radius + 0.25: J-SIG 1.75 mm, J-PWR 1.457 mm. Seat ≥ 0.84 mm thick, walls 0.8 mm.
  - Material: PA66, UL94 V-2 or better (design assumption).
- **Strap:** 3.0 × 0.6 mm, integral, closed by a latch tab on the −y side that reaches x 251.
  - Strap tops are at z 46.65 (R1) and 46.46 (R2).
  - Robust spare to A01-middle is 0.33 / 0.30 mm.
- **Fixing:** snap legs 0.8 mm thick reach 3.0 mm below the notch floor on both rail flanks. Their
  barbs engage 0.4 × 1.0 mm flank grooves, which the strength model deducts.
- **Fits, bend and abrasion:**
  - The bundle has 0.25 mm radial clearance and is straight through the guide and 1 mm beyond it,
    so it does not bend over the flare.
  - Release slack is drawn from the local loop through the closed guide: +15 mm at the middle,
    +19 mm with both ends withdrawn.
- **Tension:** the guide does not clamp, so the headers take no strap load. Equally, nothing anchors
  the cable against a pull on the local loop. JST asks for slack at the joint (S-JST-HANDLING p.960),
  and the loops provide it.
- **Not modelled:** slide force, abrasion over repeated release and strap retention are not
  calculated (M32-07).
- **Access to the R2 latch:** its panel-side tool corridor hits `crossmember:jack:1` and the SW1501
  column. Close R1/R2 before A01-middle is fitted, and reopen them only with A01-middle removed or
  with front access.

## The 15 mm release crosses an undesigned volume; ≤ 10 mm would clear the code-default plane

- **No foreign board exists:** no A02–A06 / E2–E5 board outline, plane, thickness or support exists
  in architecture-278. The z 20–21.6 "foreign PCB" is a default in `assembly.py`. Stage32 treats
  z 2–34 below the A01 carriers as **UNKNOWN, not free** (`neighbour.json`).
- **Release dip:** with the Stage10 travel of 15 mm, the hook envelope falls to z 17.6 (J-SIG) and
  17.9 (J-PWR):
  - NB-278 (code-default plane z 20–21.6): conflict from 12.5 mm travel. At 10 mm the dip clears the
    plane by 0.9 mm.
  - NB-CAN (incumbent canonical stack: EL z 4.2–4.6, P z 11.8–13.4, EL GH boxes to z 12.6): this is
    another architecture, used as evidence only. The dip clears the recorded solids by 4.9 mm at
    15 mm, but their rear-side part heights are unrecorded.
- **Travel basis:** JST states no unmating stroke. The geometric separation 4.05 − (7.3 − 5.7) = 2.45 mm
  is derived from reference dimensions and does not shorten the 15 mm design basis. The generator
  rejects a shorter release travel.
- **Proposed reservations (owner decision, not adopted):**
  - **RV-DIP-J-SIG:** XY [260.5, 162.65, 271.6, 180.35], z 17.1–48.4.
  - **RV-DIP-J-PWR:** XY [280.5, 167.025, 291.6, 180.975], z 17.39–48.4.
  - **RV-ACCESS-J-SIG / -J-PWR:** the same XY, z 2.0–48.4. These are only needed if the middle
    connectors are serviced with the neighbouring front boards fitted; otherwise those boards must
    come out with the front panel.
- **Requirements on foreign parts:** SW1401/SW1501/SW1601 tops ≤ z 42.59 under the J-PWR run, and
  SW1501 ≤ z 38.60 under the R2 snap leg.
  - The 2MS3T1B1M2QES-5 drawing (rev A 2010-01-26) bounds the terminal tips at z ≤ 16.63. That holds
    only for a panel-bushing mount with the front nut fully engaged and no foreign wiring behind the
    switch.
  - The exact-MPN gate (-5 suffix vs. the unsuffixed MPN in the lock) stays OPEN.

## Every identified wire states a 10× OD bend radius; the 5 mm route is below it

Primary documents (`sources.json`):
- **JST eGH:** AWG #30–#26, insulation OD 0.76–1.0 mm, 50 V, −40 to +105 °C. JST states no bend radius,
  stroke or force. The drawings and handling manuals CHM-1-2149/2111 sit behind a registration form;
  nothing was submitted.
- **Alpha Wire 1852 / 1853 / 2843/7** all state "Bend Radius 10X Cable Diameter":

| Wire | AWG | OD range (mm) | In JST window | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1852 | 28 | 0.838–0.940 | yes | 600 V, 105 °C |
| 1853 | 26 | 0.940–1.041 | no | 600 V, 105 °C |
| 2843/7 | 26 | 0.737–0.838 | no | 250 V, 200 °C; silver-plated, outside JST's "as a rule" wire |

Required centreline radii (inner conductor of the round bundle):
- 1852: J-SIG 10.3 mm and J-PWR 10.1 mm.
- Any wire at the JST maximum OD: 11.0 / 10.7 mm.

Consequences:
- The B32 waypoints admit only r 5.
- A route with R ≥ 10 that leaves the middle housing along −z must fall at least R before it turns
  back up. The installed hook then reaches z ≤ 27.6, and z ≤ 12.6 during the 15 mm release.
- This stage did not develop an R ≥ 10 layout.
- No wire is selected (Q-H02). The bend condition is open, and it decides whether B32 can be used
  as drawn.

## The notched rail meets the planning load only; it is not a strength result

`rail:middle:0` is modelled as a 6 × 12 mm 6061-T6 bar:
- **Supports:** a cantilever from the top crossmember (y 142, joint not evaluated) to a free end at
  y 274. The notches sit 105 / 93 mm from the tip.
- **Material:** proof stress 240 MPa minimum (supplier), with a safety factor of 1.5.
- **Stress concentration:** an Inglis upper bound, Kt = 1 + 2√(depth/ρ), for an end-milled corner
  ρ 0.8 mm. This is conservative, not a chart or FEA value.
- **Allowable tip load:** N1 15.7 N vertical / 11.9 N lateral; N2 20.5 / 14.8 N.
  - The Stage10 planning load of 5 N is met.
  - At 20 N, N1 fails for every corner radius in the sensitivity grid; N2 fails laterally.
- **Tip deflection at 5 N:** 0.063 mm without the notches, 0.084 mm with them.
- **Not covered:** the root joint, fatigue, vibration, impact/handling loads and certified machined
  material. Measurement M32-06 stays open.

## Assembly and service keep the core 40 mm motion untouched

The steps are in `summary.md`. In short:
- **Assembly:** close R1/R2 with the rail top exposed. Mate the middle housings on the bench, then
  lower A01-middle along −z before the rear (core) carrier is fitted.
- **Core service:** disconnect K-PWR, then withdraw the core 40 mm. Neither cable nor guide is
  touched, and the clearance is 3.35 mm.
- **Middle connector service:** press the GH latch with a flat tool and pull the bundle within 15° of
  −z (S-JST-HANDLING p.961). Only 2.0 mm of straight bundle sits below each housing before the hook
  bend, and JST states no grip length.
  - Both long faces are at least 11.9 mm from known obstacles, but which face carries the latch is
    unconfirmed.
  - CAD gaps are not finger operability: M32-02 and M32-03 stay open.

## Open conditions and owner decisions

- **Wire and bend (decision):** select a wire whose maker states a minimum bend radius ≤ 4 mm for
  the conductor, which B32 needs. Otherwise authorise an R ≥ 10 re-layout, which needs a deeper
  reservation (≤ z 27.6 installed).
- **Neighbour volume (decision):** approve RV-DIP and RV-ACCESS, or keep the release travel ≤ 10 mm
  once a maker stroke or a coupon supports it. Alternatively, confirm that the neighbouring front
  boards come out with the panel.
- **Foreign requirements:** SW1401–SW1601 ≤ z 42.59 and SW1501 ≤ z 38.60. These depend on a foreign
  design that does not exist yet.
- **Registration:** the sweep-to-SW1701 gap allows 0.35 mm of panel-to-carrier registration, and
  J1610 allows 0.975 mm; the registration chain is UNCONFIRMED.
- **Measurements M32-01…09:** all NOT RUN (`report.json`).
- **Also NOT RUN:** M-01, M-09, M-12, native KiCad, ERC/DRC, simulation, fabrication output and
  energisation.
