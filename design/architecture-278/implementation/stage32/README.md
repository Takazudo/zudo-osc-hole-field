## B32 is recommended, pending a wire that meets the bend radius

B32 keeps the Stage31 B topology and meets S1–S8. Its status is
**CONDITIONAL_PENDING_WIRE_SELECTION**: S9 (maker bend rule) fails for every wire identified so
far. It is a proposal: nothing is adopted.
- **Header moves:**
  - J-SIG:middle (260, 150) → (268.0, 171.5), turned 90°, as in B.
  - J-PWR:middle (283, 150) → **(288.0, 174.0)**, turned 90°. It moves 0.7 mm toward −y versus B, so
    its sweep gap to SW1701 grows from 0.575 to 1.275 mm.
- **Runs:**
  - The runs under A01-middle sit at z 44.1 (J-SIG) and z 44.2 (J-PWR); in B they were at z 46.0 and 46.3.
  - The J-PWR run moves to y 183.6 (B: y 183).
  - The J-SIG jog ends at x 240, so each cable is straight through its guide.
- **Guides R1/R2 in notches of `rail:middle:0`** (see the next section):
  - N1: y 168.8–174.2, floor z 41.5, 4.5 mm deep; the rail keeps 7.5 of 12 mm.
  - N2: y 181.2–186.0, floor z 41.9, 4.1 mm deep; the rail keeps 7.9 mm.
  - The notches follow from the guide geometry and the tolerance stack.
- **Clearances:**
  - Static minimum 0.99 mm (required 0.5); the cables are 4.29 mm apart.
  - Core-motion clearance 3.55 mm.
  - Tightest robust spare 0.199 mm after the worst-case stack, against the 0.15 K4 threshold.
- **Lengths (not cut lengths):**
  - Design lengths are J-SIG 136 mm and J-PWR 150 mm: minimum installed 106.7 / 121.2 mm, plus the
    15 mm-release extra 19.0 / 18.8 mm, plus 10 mm service slack.
  - The model installs each cable **at its design length**. The ~29 mm surplus sits in the local
    slack loop above A01-jack, whose top reaches z 71.0 / 70.9. Every check uses that state.

## Stage31 B does not hold once tolerances are explicit: its 0.04 mm K4 margin was not real

Stage32 keeps the 0.5 mm placement margin and the 0.15 mm K4 threshold, and does not round. It
also subtracts an explicit worst-case stack at each sample. For each obstacle the reported spare is
the minimum over samples of clearance − 0.5 − stack (design-input `tolerances` and
`tolerance_rules`).
- **Board bow:** A01-middle bows toward the carrier as a half sine of 0.75 % over its 150 mm length
  (y 142–292).
  - Source: the IPC-6012 limit (standard not retained). JLC states no bow value.
  - Strap rows take the largest bow over the strap and latch-tab footprint.
- **Part tolerances:**
  - Spacer ±0.1, carrier faces ±0.1, saddle seat ±0.1, saddle seating ±0.1, strap bore ±0.1 and
    strap ±0.05 (design assumptions).
  - PCB hole position ±0.075 (JLC) and mount-hole float ±0.1.

Results:
- **B31 (B as committed) fails.** Its cables sit 0.79–0.80 mm under A01-middle, and the bow there is
  0.60–0.85 mm. Tightest robust spare is −0.76 mm (J-PWR, `board:A01-middle:1`).
  - Its SW1701 sweep gap is also below K4 after known tolerances (S4).
  - It has no retention geometry (Stage31 R1/R2 were null).
- **B32 meets K4** at 0.199 mm under the base bow model, and at 0.269 mm with a half sine along the
  M1–M3 line.
- **B32 does not meet K4 if 1.282 mm of bow is applied everywhere** (spare −0.382 mm). That case is 0.75 %
  of the board diagonal, the interior bow a four-corner-supported board could reach.
  - It stays an open condition: measure the bow (M32-04), or add a non-fastened rest pad on the rail.
  - The rest pad would be a carrier change that needs approval.

## Retention R1/R2 is a captive guide; the bundle slides and the local loop gives up the length

Each guide is a saddle seated in its notch with an integral strap. Dimensions are in
`model.json` → `candidates.B32.guides`; the rules are in `design-input.json` `retention_rules`.
- **Saddle:**
  - 8.0 mm along the cable (the 6 mm rail plus 1 mm overhang each side), with an end flare of R 2.0.
  - Seat radius = bundle radius + 0.25: J-SIG 1.75 mm, J-PWR 1.457 mm. Seat ≥ 0.84 mm thick, walls 0.8 mm.
  - Material: PA66, UL94 V-2 or better (design assumption).
- **Strap:** 3.0 × 0.6 mm and integral, closed by a latch tab on the −y side that reaches x 251.
  - Strap tops are at z 46.45 (R1) and 46.26 (R2).
- **Fixing:** snap legs 0.8 mm thick reach 3.0 mm below the notch floor on both rail flanks. Their
  barbs engage 0.4 × 1.0 mm flank grooves, which the strength model deducts.
- **Bend and abrasion:** the bundle has 0.25 mm radial clearance. It is straight through the guide
  and 1 mm beyond it, so it does not bend over the flare.
- **Slide:** every release case is modelled at the constant design length.
  - The hook follows the withdrawn housing under the Stage31 bend rule, and the local loop rises or
    falls by half the difference.
  - The bundle slides through the closed guide by exactly the middle travel: 15 mm at 15 mm. A
    local-only withdrawal does not move it through the guide.
  - No case runs out of slack.
- **Tension:** the guide does not clamp, so the headers take no strap load. Equally, nothing anchors
  the cable against a pull on the local loop; JST asks for slack at the joint (S-JST-HANDLING p.960).
- **Not modelled:** slide force, abrasion over repeated release and strap retention are not
  calculated (M32-07).
- **Strap latches cannot be reached from the front:**
  - R1's tab lies over the rail.
  - R2's panel-side corridor hits `crossmember:jack:1` and the SW1501 column.
  - Both are closed with the rail top exposed, before A01-middle is fitted, and reopened only with
    A01-middle removed (+z).

## The 15 mm release crosses an undesigned volume; ≤ 10 mm would clear the code-default plane

- **No foreign board exists:** no A02–A06 / E2–E5 board outline, plane, thickness or support exists
  in architecture-278. The z 20–21.6 "foreign PCB" is a default in `assembly.py`. Stage32 treats
  z 0–34 below the A01 carriers as **UNKNOWN, not free**. The panel rear is z 0 (Stage10); the front
  is z −2.
- **Installed hook:** it already reaches z 32.6 (J-SIG) / 32.9 (J-PWR), below the carrier bottom z 34.
- **15 mm release:** with the Stage10 travel the hook falls to z 17.6 / 17.9.
  - NB-278 (code-default plane z 20–21.6): conflict from 12.5 mm travel. At 10 mm the dip clears the
    plane by 0.9 mm.
  - NB-CAN (the incumbent canonical stack, another architecture, used as evidence only): the dip
    clears the recorded solids by 4.9 mm at 15 mm, but their rear-side part heights are unrecorded.
- **Travel basis:** JST states no unmating stroke. The geometric separation 4.05 − (7.3 − 5.7) = 2.45 mm
  is derived from reference dimensions and does not shorten the 15 mm basis. The generator rejects a
  shorter travel.
- **Proposed reservations (owner decision, not adopted).** Each RV-DIP box is the union of the hook
  envelopes below z 34 in every state, plus the housing sweep and the margin; a test checks that it
  contains every sample.
  - **RV-DIP-J-SIG:** XY [256.0, 162.65, 271.6, 180.35], z 17.0–48.4.
  - **RV-DIP-J-PWR:** XY [276.29, 167.025, 291.6, 180.975], z 17.29–48.4.
  - **RV-ACCESS-J-SIG / -J-PWR:** the housing sweep plus margin, z 0–48.4. These are only needed if
    the middle connectors are serviced with the neighbouring front boards fitted; otherwise those
    boards must come out with the front panel.
- **Requirements on foreign parts:** SW1401/SW1501/SW1601 tops ≤ z 42.39 under the J-PWR run, and
  SW1501 ≤ z 38.40 under the R2 snap leg.
  - The 2MS3T1B1M2QES-5 drawing (rev A 2010-01-26) bounds the terminal tips at z ≤ 15.13: nut and
    washer at −0.25, every body dimension at +0.25.
  - That holds only for a panel-bushing mount with the front nut fully engaged and no foreign wiring
    behind the switch.
  - The exact-MPN gate (-5 suffix vs. the unsuffixed MPN in the lock) stays OPEN.

## Every identified wire states a 10× OD bend radius; the 5 mm route is below it

Primary documents (`sources.json`):
- **JST eGH:** AWG #30–#26, insulation OD 0.76–1.0 mm, 50 V, −40 to +105 °C. JST states no bend radius,
  stroke or force. The GH drawings and handling manuals CHM-1-2149/2111 sit behind a registration
  form; nothing was submitted, and MAKER_DOC values may not cite them.
- **Alpha Wire 1852 / 1853 / 2843/7** all state "Bend Radius 10X Cable Diameter":

| Wire | AWG | OD range (mm) | In JST window | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1852 | 28 | 0.838–0.940 | yes | 600 V, 105 °C |
| 1853 | 26 | 0.940–1.041 | no | 600 V, 105 °C |
| 2843/7 | 26 | 0.737–0.838 | no | 250 V, 200 °C; silver-plated, outside JST's "as a rule" wire |

Required centreline radii (inner conductor of the round bundle):
- 1852: J-SIG 10.3 mm and J-PWR 10.1 mm.
- Any wire at the JST maximum OD: 11.0 / 10.7 mm.

Consequences:
- The B32 waypoints admit only r 5.
- A route with R ≥ 10 that leaves the middle housing along −z must fall at least R before it turns
  back up. The installed hook then reaches z ≤ 27.6, and z ≤ 12.6 during the 15 mm release.
- This stage did not develop an R ≥ 10 layout.
- No wire is selected (Q-H02). S9 records the failure.

## The notched rail meets the planning load only; it is not a strength result

`rail:middle:0` is modelled as a 6 × 12 mm 6061-T6 bar:
- **Supports:** a cantilever from the top crossmember (y 142, joint not evaluated) to a free end at
  y 274.
- **Material:** proof stress 240 MPa minimum (supplier), with a safety factor of 1.5.
- **Section:** the net section includes the flank grooves.
- **Stress concentration:** an Inglis upper bound, Kt = 1 + 2√(depth/ρ), for an end-milled corner
  ρ 0.8 mm. This is conservative, not a chart or FEA value.
- **Allowable tip load:** N1 14.6 N vertical / 11.4 N lateral; N2 19.1 / 14.1 N.
  - The Stage10 planning load of 5 N is met.
  - At 20 N, every case fails except N2 vertical at ρ 1.0.
- **Tip deflection at 5 N:** 0.063 mm without the notches, 0.086 mm with them.
- **Not covered:** the root joint, fatigue, vibration, impact/handling loads and certified machined
  material. Measurement M32-06 stays open.

## Assembly and service keep the core 40 mm motion untouched

The steps are in `summary.md`. In short:
- **Assembly:** close R1/R2 with the rail top exposed. Mate the middle housings on the bench, then
  lower A01-middle along −z before the rear (core) carrier is fitted.
- **Core service:** disconnect K-PWR, then withdraw the core 40 mm. Neither cable nor guide is
  touched, and the clearance is 3.55 mm.
- **Middle connector service:** press the GH latch with a flat tool and pull the bundle within 15° of
  −z (S-JST-HANDLING p.961).
  - Only 2.0 mm of straight bundle sits below each housing before the hook bend, and JST states no
    grip length.
  - Free space from the housing long faces:

    | Face | To bundles | To rigid parts |
    |---|---|---|
    | J-SIG −x | 3.5 mm (its own hook leg) | 12.0 mm |
    | J-SIG +x | 4.6 mm (J-PWR) | 11.9 mm |
    | J-PWR −x | 3.8 mm (its own leg) | 11.9 mm |

  - Which face carries the latch is unconfirmed. CAD gaps are not finger operability: M32-02 and
    M32-03 stay open.
- **Harness replacement:** remove the core, the rear carrier and A01-middle, then open R1/R2 from +z.

## Open conditions and owner decisions

- **Wire and bend (decision):** select a wire whose maker states a minimum bend radius ≤ 4 mm for
  the conductor, which B32 needs. Otherwise authorise an R ≥ 10 re-layout, which needs a deeper
  reservation (≤ z 27.6 installed).
- **Neighbour volume (decision):** approve RV-DIP and RV-ACCESS, or keep the release travel ≤ 10 mm
  once a maker stroke or a coupon supports it. Alternatively, confirm that the neighbouring front
  boards come out with the panel.
- **Local slack loop (condition):** modelled as a two-leg stand-up loop (top z 71, 78.5 with the
  local end withdrawn). Its real shape, and whether it stays clear of the volume between x 227 and
  the core / A01-middle, is unconfirmed (M32-08, M-12).
- **Board bow (condition):** K4 holds for a half-sine bow but not for a uniform 1.282 mm interior bow.
  Measure it (M32-04), or approve a rail rest pad.
- **Foreign requirements:** SW1401–SW1601 ≤ z 42.39 and SW1501 ≤ z 38.40. These depend on a foreign
  design that does not exist yet.
- **Registration:** the sweep-to-SW1701 gap allows 0.35 mm of panel-to-carrier registration, and
  J1610 allows 0.975 mm; the registration chain is UNCONFIRMED.
- **Measurements M32-01…09:** all NOT RUN (`report.json`).
- **Also NOT RUN:** M-01, M-09, M-12, native KiCad, ERC/DRC, simulation, fabrication output and
  energisation.
