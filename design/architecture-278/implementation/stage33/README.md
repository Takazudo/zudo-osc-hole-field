## R33 meets the maker bend rule but has no lateral retention, so it stays CONDITIONAL

R33 (`CONDITIONAL_PENDING_RETENTION`) meets 11 of the 12 criteria. **S3 (lateral retention)
fails**: no part holds the arches. Every clearance below assumes each arch stays in its ideal
vertical plane, so those results are CONDITIONAL until M33-04 measures the arch position or a
retention part is designed. Nothing is adopted.

- **Header moves (broader change, proposed):** J-SIG:middle and J-PWR:middle move to the
  A01-middle **rear face** (z 50; the board plane is unchanged).
  - J-SIG:middle (260, 150) → (250, 152); J-PWR:middle (283, 150) → (290, 150); both 0°.
  - Pins, nets and MPNs are unchanged. The footprint is mirrored onto the rear face (largest pad move
    21.3 / 14.5 mm). This adds an SMT side to A01-middle and needs the copper re-routed; neither is
    evaluated, and routed copper is null.
- **Route:** one vertical-plane arch per cable.
  - It goes straight up from the local exit (z 43.9), along one straight horizontal leg, and straight
    down into the middle exit (z 57.3).
  - Both bends are 90° at **R 12**.
  - Every housing exit keeps a straight **fan-in** in every state: 10.39 mm (J-SIG) / 6.91 mm (J-PWR),
    enough for the outermost pin-row conductor to reach the bundle in an S-bend at 10× OD.
- **Design lengths:** J-SIG **133 mm**, J-PWR **149 mm**. These are not cut lengths.
  - Each is minimum installed (107.7 / 123.5) + 15 mm release extra (15.0) + 10 slack, rounded up.
  - Stage32 B32 needed 136 / 150 mm.
- **Clearances (ideal plane):**
  - The J-PWR arch is 2.77 mm from the core withdrawal volume; its robust spare is 1.89 mm after the
    0.375 mm board-chain stack. K4 holds for every bow case and every 15 mm state, including the
    uniform 1.282 mm bow.
  - Core-to-middle carrier registration is UNCONFIRMED and is not in the stack.
  - Cable to cable is 4.23 mm in plan in every state; this is valid because both fillets lie in the
    vertical plane through the two exits.
  - The installed tops (z 94.1 / 90.4) stay under the rear cover (z 105) with the uniform bow added.
- **Front volume:** R33 never goes below the middle carrier bottom (lowest envelope z 42.15) in any
  state. RV-DIP and the front RV-ACCESS are not needed, and U-FRONT stays UNKNOWN.

## Retention is the smallest open item; a slide guide could sit only on the local legs

| Cable | Lateral allowance (mm) | Limited by | Straight zone on the local leg, every state | Middle leg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| J-SIG | 3.36 | the J-PWR arch (4.23 mm in plan; no rigid part within 5.5 mm) | z 69.3–80.4 | none |
| J-PWR | 1.89 | core withdrawal volume | z 65.8–76.9 | none |

- The allowance is how far an arch may leave its plane before the margin and the stack are used up.
- The cable-to-cable gap is shared: if both arches lean toward each other, the sum of their two
  deviations must stay within it.
- A captive guide that lets the cable slide axially could sit in the local-leg zone, where the
  cable stays straight in every state. The middle legs have no such zone.
- Designing that guide, or measuring the arch position (M33-04), is the next step.
- The Stage33 draft's keeper K1 was withdrawn after the review. It held nothing, and it did not
  cover the J-PWR arch's closest approach to the core.

## Large-radius routing with the middle headers on the front face cannot be shown: F33 is UNKNOWN

A front-face housing exits along −z at z 41.1. A route with every bend ≥ R needs the fan-in plus R
below that exit, so at R 12 the run lies at z ≤ 16.96 (J-SIG) / 20.74 (J-PWR) installed and at
z ≤ 1.96 / 5.74 at 15 mm.

- **Through the slot:** crossing a jack crossmember through the z 30–34 slot leaves −0.35 / −0.06 mm
  per side after margin and tolerance, against K4 0.15. This path is **INFEASIBLE**.
- **Around the +y edge of the jack board:** a descent there clears the foreign XY columns in plan
  (largest clear radius 5.25 mm). But the run then lies below the code-default foreign PCB plane
  (z 20–21.6) even when installed, inside the undesigned front volume, and the middle headers would
  have to move (for example to y > 185) or the run would have to pass under crossmember:jack:1 below
  z 22. This path is **UNKNOWN**.
- **Rescue:** a foreign PCB plane below the hook, foreign depth limits, and a front-volume
  reservation down to z 1.96. R33 needs none of this.

## R 12 meets the 10× rule for every wire in the JST window; a flex rule needs a larger R

The maker documents do not say which surface the "10× cable diameter" radius refers to. Stage33
uses the stricter reading, the inner surface of the bent bundle:
- R ≥ k × OD + envelope radius, where the envelope radius is the round-bundle radius at the
  worst-case OD plus a 0.25 mm dressing allowance.
- The innermost-conductor-centreline reading (0.5 mm less) is reported for comparison.

| Case | J-SIG (mm) | J-PWR (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| 10× OD at the JST maximum OD (1.0 mm) | 11.75 | 11.46 |
| 15× flex sensitivity | 16.75 | 16.46 |
| 20× flex sensitivity | 21.75 | 21.46 |

- No candidate maker document says whether its radius applies to repeated flexing, so the flex rule
  stays UNCONFIRMED. R 12 meets the 10× case only.
- **Radius scan:** clearance with the catch-all stack against K4, slack, the core, and the rear cover
  with the uniform bow (the S11 rule).
  - It fits at R 17 (design lengths 139 / 155 mm), so the 15× sensitivity is CONDITIONAL.
  - R 22 fails: the installed top 104.4 + bow 1.28 + margin 0.5 is above z 105. So the 20× sensitivity
    is INFEASIBLE inside the rear cover.
  - Access, the other bow cases and strength are not re-evaluated in the scan.

## Service keeps the core order and moves all J-SIG/J-PWR work to the rear

The steps are in `summary.md`.
- **Core:** disconnect K-PWR, then withdraw the core 40 mm with every J housing installed. Do not
  move the core while a J housing is withdrawn: the raised arch reaches z 109.1 and is not checked
  against the core in that state.
- **Middle connectors:** rear cover off, press the latch, then pull the vertical bundle within 15° of
  +z.
  - J-SIG has 23.1 mm straight above the housing installed and 15.6 mm at 15 mm. Above its 10.4 mm
    fan-in that leaves 12.7 / 5.2 mm to grip.
  - Every housing's +z column is clear of rigid parts and of the other cable.
  - CAD gaps are not finger operability (M33-03).
- **Harness replacement:** all four ends unmate from the rear. No board, carrier or core comes out.

## Open conditions and owner decisions

- **Decisions:**
  - the rear-face header move;
  - RV-ARCH-J-SIG / -J-PWR (in-plane slabs) and the four RV-ACCESS columns. None of them overlaps a
    designed part. The slab tops above z 105 are reached only with the rear cover off.
- **Retention:** design a sliding guide in the local-leg zone, or measure the arch position against
  the allowance (M33-04).
- **Wire:**
  - For J-PWR, an AWG26, tin-plated wire with OD 0.76–1.0 mm worst case, ≥ 50 V / 105 °C and a
    maker bend rule ≤ 10× OD; or a current basis for AWG28 with the GH contact, which would make
    Alpha Wire 1852 pass for J-PWR.
  - For J-SIG, a current budget.
  - A maker flex rule decides whether R 12 is enough.
- **Unknown:** the latch side; the GH mating force on a rear-face header and the board deflection
  under it; the core-to-middle carrier registration; the rear-cover features.

- **Also NOT RUN:** M-01, M-09, M-12, native KiCad, ERC/DRC, simulation, fabrication output and
