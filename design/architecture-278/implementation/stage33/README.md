## R33 is the only candidate that meets the maker bend rule; it needs two broader changes

Under the stated assumptions R33 meets all twelve criteria. It is CONDITIONAL on owner decisions
and on a wire; nothing is adopted.

- **Header moves (broader change, proposed):** J-SIG:middle and J-PWR:middle move to the
  A01-middle **rear face** (z 50; the board plane is unchanged).
  - J-SIG:middle (260, 150) → (250, 152); J-PWR:middle (283, 150) → (290, 150); both 0°.
  - Pins, nets and MPNs are unchanged. The footprint is mirrored onto the rear face, which adds an
    SMT side to A01-middle (process not evaluated).
- **Route:** one vertical-plane arch per cable. Each goes straight up from the local exit (z 43.9),
  runs one straight horizontal leg, and comes straight down into the middle exit (z 57.3). Both
  bends are 90° at **R 12**.
  - In plan the two arches are separate segments; they never pass over the other cable's housing or
    access column.
- **Keeper K1 (broader change, proposed):** a 2 mm fence rising from crossmember:rear:0.
  - Box x 241–258, y 170.5–172.5, z 62–96, in 6061-T6.
  - It separates the J-PWR arch from the core withdrawal volume (y ≥ 174) where the arch comes within
    10 mm of it. It touches no cable.
- **Design lengths:** J-SIG **116 mm**, J-PWR **139 mm**. These are not cut lengths.
  - J-SIG = minimum installed 90.9 + 15 mm release extra 15.0 + 10 slack, rounded up.
  - J-PWR = 113.7 + 15.0 + 10, rounded up.
  - Stage32 B32 needed 136 / 150 mm.
- **Clearances:**
  - Installed static minimum 1.16 mm (J-PWR to K1); 2.77 mm to the core withdrawal volume.
  - Tightest robust spare after the worst-case stack is 0.56 mm (K4 0.15). This is the same for the
    half-sine bow, the M1–M3 bow and the **uniform 1.282 mm bow**: no cable runs under A01-middle,
    so the bow does not reach it.
  - Cable to cable 4.23 mm in plan in every state (robust 3.36 mm).
- **Front volume:** R33 never goes below the middle carrier bottom (lowest envelope z 42.15), in any
  state. RV-DIP and the front RV-ACCESS are not needed. U-FRONT stays UNKNOWN.

## Large-radius routing is infeasible with the middle headers on the front face

A front-face housing exits along −z at z 41.1. A route with every bend ≥ R must fall at least
lead + R below that exit, so at R 12 the hook envelope bottom is z ≤ 25.35 (J-SIG) / 25.64 (J-PWR) installed and z ≤ 10.35 /
10.64 at 15 mm. To get there:
- The local exits sit on the jack board (x 195–240, y 94–182). The middle board covers x ≥ 232
  above z 48.4, and the 2 mm gap between the jack board edge and rail:middle:0 is narrower than any
  bundle.
- So the route must pass a jack crossmember through the z 30–34 slot, or go below the jack carrier.
- The slot leaves −0.35 mm (J-SIG) and −0.06 mm (J-PWR) per side after margin and tolerance. K4
  needs 0.15. **F33 is INFEASIBLE.**
- The smallest change that would rescue F33:
  - a notch in crossmember:jack:0/1, outside the approval;
  - a reserved channel in the undesigned front volume.
- Even then the 15 mm release crosses the code-default foreign plane. Only a travel ≤ 3.25 mm
  avoids it, and there is no maker basis for that.

## B32 stays infeasible: the Stage32 failures are carried, not recomputed away

- **Bend rule:** B32's r 5 bends are below every identified maker rule (Stage32 S9), and the bounded
  search found no wire with a smaller rule inside the JST window.
- **Uniform bow:** a uniform 1.282 mm bow still fails K4 (−0.382 mm).
- **Rail strength:** its notched rail fails in 11 cases at 20 N.

## R 12 meets the 10× rule for every wire in the JST window; flexing needs more, and the geometry has room

Required radius is measured to the innermost conductor:
- R(innermost) = route R − (bundle envelope radius − OD/2).
- Bundle envelope radius = round-bundle radius at the worst-case OD + 0.25 mm dressing allowance.

| Case | J-SIG (mm) | J-PWR (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| 10× OD at the JST maximum OD (1.0 mm) | 11.25 | 10.96 |
| 15× flex sensitivity | 16.25 | 15.96 |
| 20× flex sensitivity | 21.25 | 20.96 |

- No candidate maker document says whether its bend radius applies to repeated flexing, so the flex
  rule stays UNCONFIRMED.
- R 12 meets the 10× case only.
- The radius scan fits at R 16.5, 21.5 and 22.5 (installed arch top z 96.1 at 22.5). R 23 fails:
  the J-SIG horizontal leg (45.2 mm) is too short for two bends.
- A larger radius needs longer cables and a taller K1 (less strength). From R 16.5 the service-state
  arch top rises past z 105 (105.5 at R 16.5) with the rear cover off.

## Strength is conditional; the 20 N case stays visible

- **rail:middle:0:** R33 crosses no carrier rail, so the rail stays unnotched. Allowable tip load is
  174.5 N vertical / 87.3 N lateral in the Stage32 cantilever model; the root joint is not evaluated.
- **K1:** modelled as a cantilever plate with a 5 N planning load at its top.
  - Allowable 17.8 N at Kt 3.0, 26.7 N at Kt 2.0, 35.6 N at Kt 1.5; the Kt values are assumptions.
  - It fails at 20 N for Kt 3.0. Deflection is 0.083 mm at 5 N.
  - Its root is 12 mm above the A01-middle rear face; no exposed conductor is modelled within 10 mm.
- **Not evaluated:** joints, fatigue, impact loads and certified material (M33-06).

## Service keeps the core order and moves all J-SIG/J-PWR work to the rear

The steps are in `summary.md`.
- **Core:** disconnect K-PWR, then withdraw the core 40 mm. Neither the arches nor K1 is touched.
  Do not move the core while a J housing is withdrawn: the raised arch reaches z 100.6, above K1.
- **Middle connectors:** rear cover off, press the latch, then pull the vertical bundle within 15° of
  +z.
  - The straight bundle above each housing is 14.6 mm installed and 7.1 mm at 15 mm.
  - Every housing's +z column is clear of rigid parts and of the other cable.
  - CAD gaps are not finger operability (M33-03).
- **Harness replacement:** all four ends unmate from the rear. No board, carrier or core comes out.
- **Front jack board removal:** unmate the middle ends, lift the free ends back over the A01-middle
  edge, then remove the front assembly with the cables attached.

## Open conditions and owner decisions

- **Decisions:**
  - the rear-face header move;
  - keeper K1;
  - RV-ARCH-J-SIG / -J-PWR and the four RV-ACCESS columns.
- **Wire:** select an AWG26 wire with OD 0.76–1.0 mm worst case, tin-plated, ≥ 50 V / 105 °C, and a
  maker bend rule ≤ 10× OD. Alternatively, provide a current basis for AWG28 with the GH contact,
  which would make Alpha Wire 1852 pass. A maker flex rule decides whether R 12 is enough.
- **Unknown:**
  - the arches' lateral position (sag) in each enclosure orientation (M33-04);
  - the latch side;
  - the GH mating force on a rear-face header and the board deflection under it;
  - the rear-cover features.
- **Measurements M33-01…08:** all NOT RUN (`report.json`).
- **Also NOT RUN:** M-01, M-09, M-12, native KiCad, ERC/DRC, simulation, fabrication output and
  energisation.
