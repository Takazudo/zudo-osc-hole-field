# Candidate review of length, capacitance and signal requirements

The retained 300 mm/1 nF envelope is an unvalidated project planning rule, not a demonstrated physical cliff. The initial OSC-ES-1 at `f0c8eaa0d16a0ab59427bef8d2e2fb5f9cf9c336` explicitly calls itself a proposal; its `remote_controls` fields say maximum **harness** length and capacitance. The `remote_buffer` cell remains NEEDS BENCH and describes a cable design envelope requiring actual harness qualification. The later architecture278 electrical packet applies 300 mm to the **complete path**, including connectors, traces and test equipment. Those definitions differ. This audit proposes correcting their reporting, not silently changing either canonical value.

`stage4-requirements-report.json` retains the initial source bytes' hash, current source hashes, relevant cell notes and 225 actual crossing identities. Each crossing is mapped to live canonical endpoint ref/pin/net, symbol, unit, source key and attributes, including the actual direction-source nodes. It distinguishes pitch/control, waveform/modulation, analog gate input, conditioned sync command, and DC reference paths. A crossing source may be the output side of an isolation resistor or a switch contact; that does not make the upstream driver disappear.

Pitch needs a DC and dynamic error budget: buffer offset/bias/loading, contact and return voltage, reference accuracy, temperature drift and oscillator calibration share the result. At 1 V/oct, 5 cents corresponds algebraically to 4.166667 mV. That is a whole-chain target, not available interconnect error margin. FM, pitch modulation and tuning transitions also need bandwidth, settling and crosstalk checks; treating every pitch net as static DC is insufficient. Raw wipers need their position-dependent source resistance and receiving bias/load, unlike a low-impedance buffered source.

Gate transport needs actual high/low margin, threshold noise/hysteresis, ringing and false edges, pulse width/loss and timing. The source contains distinct cases: SYNC_BUFFER is an analog buffered jack signal feeding the comparator network; SYNC_GATE is the conditioned HC output; HARD_DRIVE and SOFT_DRIVE pass through a selector to a 100 kohm pulldown, 10 kohm series resistor and 1 nF AC-coupled core-sync network. OSC_GATE_REF is a shared DC threshold reference, not a logic pulse. The planning comparator acceptance windows are 1.35–1.65 V rising and 0.85–1.15 V falling; actual offset, VOL, rails, ground and reference tolerances must be included. A single generic logic-cable rule cannot qualify all these paths.

The source explicitly marks remote-buffer cable stability NOT RUN because a validated OPA4196 model and characterized harness are missing. The shared nine-net direct-capacitance aggregate remains unextracted. Five selected 1 kohm/100 nF octave receivers require separate transient/stability/source-current analysis; neither their dynamics nor common conductor capacitance may be hidden by per-path duplication. Length does not supply capacitance without actual conductor, return, connector and probe geometry.

Proposed candidate reporting correction:

- Preserve historical complete-path 300 mm results as a strict sensitivity screen, with harness length and total developed length reported separately.
- Describe 334 mm as exceeding that allocation, not as proven unsafe. Describe a sub-300 mm result as meeting that scalar allocation, not as demonstrated safe/stable hardware.
- Characterize the actual driver, receiver, grounding, load and parasitics by signal class before deciding whether extra interfaces or a new topology improve the circuit. Do not add topology solely to clear this unvalidated cutoff.
- Compare internal interconnect with protected/buffered external patch paths using their actual circuit conditions. Internal copper has no automatic superiority; the external jack path's protection and buffering also do not validate an arbitrary internal raw-wiper or sensitive node.
- Keep local timing, summing, feedback and storage nodes local. This rationale review grants no remote-routing exception and makes no canonical rule change.

Six focused source-audit tests cover all225 rows, live endpoint net consistency, distinct gate/reference classification the initial proposal status and an unknown-net rejection. No new simulation, native, ERC/DRC, capacitance extraction or bench run was performed. Adoption, canonical requirements correction and hardware qualification remain open for separately reviewed evidence.
