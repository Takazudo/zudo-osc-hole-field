Proposed first route: JLC multilayer FR-4 with ENIG and its documented tighter
circular-hole size option, nominal 1.00 ±0.05 mm. Board layer/finish selection is
not adopted here. Before accepting the route, resolve all of the following together:

1. Agree whether the drawing's Ø1±0.1 board-drilling requirement denotes finished

   manufacturing tool diameter is left to a qualified process, not guessed here.
2. Demonstrate the Ø0.05 position-zone requirement with the drawing's datum and
   registration convention across the complete 96-position grid. Published ±0.075
   hole-position capability alone cannot guarantee that zone. Do not substitute
   ±0.05 axis error for a Ø0.05 diametral zone or assume size service improves position.
3. Qualify the female two Ø2.8 +0.1/−0 fixing holes at x±45 and the drawing offset,
   including plating classification and their relation to the contact pattern.
   Stage24 proposes NPTH; this and the tight one-sided size interval need acceptance.
   The published tighter-hole option's 0.55–2.0 range does not cover them.
4. Preserve solder-tail, seating-height, mating alignment, board thickness,
   insertion/retention and mechanical registration requirements. Male flange holes
   are connector body features, not invented board holes. No arbitrary footprint
   enlargement, lead bending or forced assembly is allowed.
5. Confirm exact-part sourcing and externally completed mixed SMT/THT assembly,
   including a compatible wave/selective/hand process performed by the assembler,
   temperature/time limits, inspection and fixture access. Generic mixed-assembly
   capability is not exact-part acceptance; these solder-tail parts are not newly
   classified as press-fit, reflow or pin-in-paste parts.
6. State inspection evidence and disposition of nonconforming boards/assemblies.
   Sample inspection can establish a sample result but is not a process guarantee.
