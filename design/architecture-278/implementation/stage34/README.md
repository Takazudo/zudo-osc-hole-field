## RG34 bounds the whole arch, but the J-SIG cap does not fit under the rear cover

R33 + RG34 is **CONDITIONAL_PENDING_COVER_REGISTRATION_AND_MEASUREMENT** (RG34
`BOUNDED_EXCEPT_REAR_COVER`). RG34 meets T1–T5 and T7–T11 under the stated assumptions at every
tolerance corner.

T6 fails: the J-SIG cap top is z 104.36. With margin, RG34 tolerances, registration and K4 it needs a
rear-cover inner face at z 106.21, but the cover is at z 105, a 1.21 mm shortfall. The J-PWR cap meets
K4 with 2.65 mm spare. The smallest changes are both outside the approved geometry, so they are owner
decisions (proposals):
- a rear cover at least 1.21 mm deeper (enclosure); or
- a J-SIG design length about 2.42 mm shorter, taken from the inherited 10 mm service slack (its
  consequences for T1–T11 and the Stage32 length rule are not evaluated).

Nothing is adopted.

RG34 is one slot channel per arch on the rear carrier, standing on a post on `crossmember:rear:0`
(a proposal). Each channel has:
- **Walls** on both sides of the arch plane, with 0.3 mm play to the dressed bundle envelope; the lid
  wall is removable.
- **End walls and a cap** above the single-end raised arch at its highest corner.
- **A former** under the top leg, whose corners follow the bend inner surface. It stops the arch from
  sagging and the bends from tightening, and **its height is set at assembly** so the top leg rests on it.
- **Two removable split sleeves**, one on each leg. Each closes only the outer side and the two walls of
  its leg region. The middle sleeve encloses the whole of bend 2 (and, at the low corner, the start of
  the top leg). It has no inner arc: both bend corners belong to the settable former, which runs down
  through the sleeve zone. A fixed arc in the sleeve would let the cable cut between arcs and add about
  0.6 mm of slack, so it is excluded by design.

Sleeve bottoms and block entries include the 1.0 mm registration allowance in z. A withdrawn housing,
its fan-in and the operator's grip therefore stay below RG34 even with the carriers offset.

## The proof is geometric; it does not use cable stiffness
- **Tolerance corners:** every single-end 2.45–15 mm withdrawal is checked at three corners of the arch
  against RG34. Low is a short cable, the uniform bow and RG34 1 mm high; nominal; high is a long cable
  and RG34 1 mm low. Every state is inside RG34 at constant design length at all three. With the lid
  closed, a both-ends withdrawal is blocked by the cap at every corner.
- **Inside RG34:** every point of a cable stays within the play of its plane. The walls bound rotation
  and out-of-plane sway of the whole arch, not of one leg.
- **Free spans:** between each housing exit and its sleeve the cable is free. Its reach is the spheroid
  of every point a span of that length plus all the remaining slack can reach (inextensible cable). It is
  inflated by the play, the registration allowance, the RG34 tolerances and the bundle radius. Every
  free span clears its surroundings with K4 (tightest 0.42 mm, J-PWR local to the A01-middle edge).
- **Slack:** with the former set at assembly the slack is 0.5 mm. With a fixed former, the cut
  tolerance, bow and registration leave 4.28 mm, and the free spans then fail K4 by up to 3.5 mm
  (generated negative control).
- **J-PWR to the core:** the J-PWR channel wall, not the cable, faces the core volume: 0.29 mm robust
  spare. Both sit on the rear carrier, so inter-carrier registration drops out of this condition.
- **Shared gap:** the channel interiors are 3.40 mm apart in plan, enough for both walls, and the
  free-span pairs clear each other.
- **Other negative controls:** a guide on the local leg alone leaves the rest of the arch able to reach
  49.0 / 54.4 mm (bucket handle 28 mm). RG34 without its former lets each local free span grow by
  17.6 / 16.7 mm.

## Conditions that remain
- **Rear cover vs J-SIG cap:** see above; owner decision.
- **Registration:** the jack, middle and rear carriers must register within 1.0 mm. The enclosure cleats
  are not designed, so the actual value is UNKNOWN (M34-06).
- **Service:** during a single-end withdrawal the arch floats above the former. Up to 15 mm of slack is
  then held only by the operator, and is not bounded.
- **Front jack board removal (proposal):** the J-PWR channel is 1.63 mm from the A01-jack M4 screwdriver
  column (−0.34 mm robust with the registration allowance). That column is used only to remove the
  front jack board, so the procedure removes RG34 first. Whether RG34 can be fitted and removed (post
  fastener access, lifting past neighbours) is not evaluated.
- **Reserved volumes:** RG34 walls stand 0.9 mm outside the RV-ARCH half-thickness and occupy the
  RV-ACCESS columns above the grip zones. This is a proposal.
- **R17 (15×):** the cap would need z 111.4 / 107.7, so it is INFEASIBLE with RG34. The Stage33 R17
  geometry without retention stays CONDITIONAL, and the Stage33 20× rear-cover failure is preserved.
- **Strength:** the post takes bending and torsion together: 39.8 N allowable (von Mises); 5, 10 and
  20 N meet, 50 N does not. Root fixity (the post overhangs the crossmember), joints, walls and
  fastening are not covered.

## The A01 current budget replaces the Stage33 J-PWR inputs
- **Stage33 used X1 values.** Stage33 took its J-PWR currents from Stage8, which studies X1 (A01
  implementation was forbidden there). The values were labelled A01-jack, and its AGND bound summed
  rail-to-rail currents. Stage34 supersedes both inputs; the Stage33 bytes stay unchanged.
- **Every value is a sum of recorded terms.** Terms are V/R values from the A01 specification, and
  quiescent and bias currents from the evidence facts, each with its basis and sources. The ADG5412F
  IDD/ISS/IGND values are the ADI rev C table recorded in the Stage8 input: component data at ±16.5 V,
  conditional at ±12 V.
- **J-SIG signal conductors:**
  - REF5 / REFN5: input bias, 50 nA conditional.
  - IN_BUFFER / OFFSET_BUFFER / SUM_NEG: 0.05 mA typical, ≤ 0.135 mA conditional.
- **AGND (each of J-SIG 2, 7 and J-PWR 4):** sharing is UNKNOWN, so each conductor carries the whole
  jack AGND total: 4.2 mA typical, 11.3 mA conditional, 37.3 mA with the output-forced fault.
- **J-PWR:** +12V 10.8 mA and −12V 10.0 mA typical; 19.0 mA and 17.8 mA conditional (44.0 / 42.8 mA fault case); the LM393B supply
  current is UNKNOWN.
- **No conductor has an established maximum.** No fuse, PTC or limiting element exists upstream. A
  short is bounded only by an unselected source requirement (2.1 A), above the JST 1.0 A rating.

## Wire selection stays null
The wire ledger keeps terminal compatibility, contact rating, temperature rise, derating, installed bend
and service flex separate. Rating comparisons against conditional currents are CONDITIONAL, never PASS.
- **Thermax M22759/18-26 (AWG26, ETFE, tin-plated 19/38, OD 0.762–0.864 mm, 600 V, 150 °C):** fails
  nothing. Its current checks are CONDITIONAL, it has no maker bend radius, and its minimum OD is
  0.002 mm above the JST floor.
- **Alpha 1852 and 6710 (AWG28) and 1851 (AWG30):** fail nothing. JST gives no contact rating at those gauges.
  That is UNKNOWN evidence, not a demonstrated failure, and the AWG26 rating is not transferred.
- **Every other identified wire fails the full-tolerance OD window, the plating or the temperature.**
- **No maker states a repeated-flex radius.**

The smallest closing steps are a Thermax bend-radius statement and an OD sample, or a JST rating at
AWG28/30, plus an unpowered flex/abrasion test through RG34.

## Measurements still NOT RUN

2. Measure the arch and free-span positions in six orientations and after a handling push, plus the cut

3. Run repeated single-end withdrawals for drag and abrasion.
4. Test the post and joints by hand load, and measure the carrier registration.
5. Check latch and grip access with one sleeve removed.
