## RG34 bounds the whole arch under stated assumptions; R33 stays CONDITIONAL

R33 + RG34 is **CONDITIONAL_PENDING_REGISTRATION_AND_MEASUREMENT**. RG34 meets T1–T11 under the
stated assumptions. Inter-carrier registration, the mock-up measurements and the wire stay open, and
nothing is adopted.

RG34 is one slot channel per arch on the rear carrier, standing on a post on `crossmember:rear:0`
(a proposal). Each channel has:
- **Walls** on both sides of the arch plane, with 0.3 mm play to the dressed bundle envelope; the lid
  wall is removable.
- **End walls and a cap** above the single-end raised arch.
- **A former** under the top leg, whose corners follow the bend inner surface. It stops the arch from
  sagging and the bends from tightening, and **its height is set at assembly** so the top leg rests on it.
- **Two removable split sleeves**, one on each leg, between the S-bend room above the fan-in and the
  block entry.

The block entry sits the 15 mm travel + fan-in + 8 mm grip above each exit, so a withdrawn housing,
its fan-in and the operator's fingers stay below RG34.

## The proof is geometric; it does not use cable stiffness
- **Inside RG34:** every point of a cable stays within the play of its plane. The walls bound rotation
  and out-of-plane sway of the whole arch, not of one leg.
- **Free spans:** between each housing exit and its sleeve the cable is free. Its reach is the spheroid
  of every point a span of that length plus all the remaining slack can reach (inextensible cable). It is
  inflated by the play, the registration allowance, the RG34 tolerances and the bundle radius. Every
  free span clears its surroundings with K4 (tightest 0.49 mm, J-PWR local to the A01-middle edge).
- **Slack:** with the former set at assembly the slack is 0.5 mm. With a fixed former, the cut tolerance
  and the uniform board bow leave 2.28 mm. That case fails, so the setting step is part of the design.
- **J-PWR to the core:** the J-PWR channel wall, not the cable, faces the core volume: 0.29 mm robust
  spare. Both sit on the rear carrier, so inter-carrier registration drops out of this condition.
- **Shared gap:** the cables sit in separate channels whose interiors are 3.40 mm apart in plan, enough
  for both walls. The free-span pairs also clear each other.
- **Negative controls:**
  - A guide on the local leg alone leaves the rest of the arch able to reach 49.6 / 55.1 mm (bucket
    handle 28 mm).
  - RG34 without its former lets each local free span grow by 16–17 mm.
  - Withdrawing both ends with the lid closed hits the cap.

## Conditions that remain
- **Registration:** the jack, middle and rear carriers must register within 1.0 mm. The enclosure cleats
  are not designed, so the actual value is UNKNOWN (M34-06). The sleeves leave an S-bend length for that
  offset at the maker radius.
- **Service:** during a single-end withdrawal the arch floats above the former. Up to 15 mm of slack is
  then held only by the operator, and is not bounded.
- **A01-jack M3/M4 screws:** the J-PWR channel is 1.63 mm from the A01-jack M4 screwdriver column, which
  fails K4 with the registration allowance. That column is used only to remove the front jack board,
  and RG34 is removed first.
- **Reserved volumes:** RG34 walls stand 0.9 mm outside the RV-ARCH half-thickness and occupy the
  RV-ACCESS columns above the grip zones. This is a proposal.
- **R17 (15×):** the cap over the raised arch would need z 110.0 / 106.3, above the cover, so it is
  INFEASIBLE with RG34. The Stage33 R17 geometry without retention stays CONDITIONAL, and the Stage33
  20× rear-cover failure is preserved.
- **Strength:** calculated for the post only (59 N allowable; 5, 10, 20 and 50 N meet). Joints, walls
  and fastening are not covered.

## The A01 current budget replaces the Stage33 J-PWR inputs
- **Stage33 used X1 values.** Stage33 took its J-PWR currents from Stage8, which studies X1 (A01
  implementation was forbidden there). The values were labelled A01-jack, and its AGND bound summed
  rail-to-rail currents. Stage34 supersedes both inputs; the Stage33 bytes stay unchanged.
- **J-SIG signal conductors:**
  - REF5 / REFN5: input-bias currents, 50 nA conditional.
  - IN_BUFFER / OFFSET_BUFFER / SUM_NEG: 0.05 mA typical, ≤ 0.135 mA conditional (output at the rail
    into ≥ 92.5 kΩ).
- **AGND (each of J-SIG 2, 7 and J-PWR 4):** sharing is UNKNOWN, so each conductor carries the whole
  jack AGND total: 3.7 mA typical, 12.3 mA conditional, 36.3 mA with the output-forced fault.
- **J-PWR:** +12V 18.4 mA and −12V 17.2 mA conditional; the LM393B supply current is not in evidence.
- **No conductor has an established maximum.** No fuse, PTC or limiting element exists upstream. A
  short is bounded only by an unselected source requirement (2.1 A), above the JST 1.0 A rating.

## Wire selection stays null
The wire ledger keeps terminal compatibility, contact rating, temperature rise, derating, installed bend
and service flex separate:
- **Thermax M22759/18-26 (AWG26, ETFE, tin-plated 19/38, OD 0.762–0.864 mm, 600 V, 150 °C):** fails
  nothing. It has no maker bend radius, and its minimum OD is 0.002 mm above the JST floor.
- **Alpha 6710 (AWG28) and 1851 (AWG30):** fail nothing. JST gives no contact rating at those gauges.
  That is UNKNOWN evidence, not a demonstrated failure, and the AWG26 rating is not transferred.
- **Every other identified wire fails the full-tolerance OD window, the plating or the temperature.**
- **No maker states a repeated-flex radius.**

The smallest closing steps are a Thermax bend-radius statement and an OD sample, or a JST rating at
AWG28/30, plus an unpowered flex/abrasion test through RG34.

## Two-sided assembly and re-routing
Final adoption of R33 needs these changes:
- a second SMT side on A01-middle for the GH headers;
- re-routed copper to the moved pads (largest moves 21.3 / 14.4 mm), with nets and pins unchanged;
- rear-face courtyards;
- a rear-carrier fastening for RG34;
- enclosure cleats that hold the registration requirement.

KiCad regeneration, ERC/DRC and fabrication output are NOT RUN and not claimed.

## Measurements still NOT RUN
M34-01…08 are listed in [summary.md](summary.md). The minimal unpowered mock-up plan:
1. Build R33 + RG34 at the design lengths.
2. Measure the arch and free-span positions in six orientations and after a handling push, plus the cut
   lengths and the former gap.
3. Run repeated single-end withdrawals for drag and abrasion.
4. Test the post and joints by hand load, and measure the carrier registration.
5. Check latch and grip access with one sleeve removed.

M-01, M-09 and M-12 also stay NOT RUN.
