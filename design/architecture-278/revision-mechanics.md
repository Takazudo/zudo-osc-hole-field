# Populated rear-core revision

This revision recommends planar rear core tiles with a mechanically supported direct-mating DIN96 connection, short flexible front links, and separately supported bolted power/return straps. It replaces the earlier 15 mm card/20 mm inter-card finger/120 mm enclosure reservations. The owner authorized investigation and proposals; those dimensions were never requirements. This is a design proposal, not architecture adoption or physical acceptance.

Reproduce `python3 scripts/architecture278/populated_mechanics.py --check` and `python3 -m unittest discover -s scripts/architecture278 -p test_populated_mechanics.py`. The source is `revision-mechanical-input.json`; generated report and SVG retain exact footprint and source hashes. The old mechanical packet remains historical evidence, including its failed finger envelope.

## Depth, connector and support

Middle PCB mating datum: **50 mm** behind panel; core mating datum: **69.2 mm**, independent support stack **19.2 ±0.3 mm**. Electrical connector research identifies HARTING **09032966824** female straight THT and **09031966922** male straight THT, with manufacturer PCB-distance interval 18.3–20.0 mm. This interval, rather than a single connector housing height, governs the support stack. The 100 ×15 local bank reservation x=1.5..101.5/y=11..26 includes the 94.9 mm body, mounting span and allowance. The connector's detailed insertion datum, pin lead-ins and manufacturer's mounting tolerance must be transferred to the eventual carrier drawing. Tolerance is an allocation, not a measured assembly result. See the electrical revision's retained manufacturer-source receipts.

Recommend **105 mm inner cover depth**, leaving 16.8 mm after an 18 mm rear handle projection and 1 mm depth tolerance. A 95 mm inner depth leaves 6.8 mm and is a credible compact option; 105 mm gives more room for terminal insulation, labels and cover flex without lengthening the electrical path. Neither option is an already approved enclosure. The cover opens across the entire 318 ×298 rear carrier projection; allow 100 mm clear external work depth. Rear cards withdraw axially 40 mm after release, clearing the signal mate and locating pins before lifting away. Cover fitted extraction is prohibited.

Each tile has four independent M3 retention stations at local (6,6),(97,6),(6,109),(97,109), radius-3 keepouts, on an enclosure-mounted carrier. Two 4 mm diameter locating-guide reservations at (6,30),(97,28) engage **before** electrical pins; specify one round guide and one relieved/slotted guide to avoid overconstraint. The unequal y coordinates provide positive rotational polarization; the board cannot seat in a reversed orientation. Connector flanges receive independent backing brackets secured to the carrier, not PCB solder. Factory assembly reacts insertion force directly at both connector housings; guide pins prevent pin tips from being used to pull misaligned boards into place. Four supports share a 180 N proof-load design target, twice the 90 N maximum insertion force; 60 N maximum withdrawal is supported by the exact female manufacturer drawing recorded in connector research. These connector ratings do not qualify the complete pair mounting or carrier. Load sharing, bracket bending, thread engagement and PCB strain remain physical/structural verification, not a passed strength calculation. Do not assume four screws automatically make the structure adequate.

Two rear pull handles at local (30,6)/(75,6), projecting 18 mm, provide 20 mm finger envelopes *behind* the card. The adjacent board's 5 mm component-height bound ends 13 mm ahead of the handle grip plane; the 20 mm finger diameter leaves 2 mm depth margin after the 1 mm tolerance. The hand holds the handles only after all power terminals and retainers are released. Support screws use axial insulated driver access from the open rear, not lateral latch access. No connector friction or solder joint is the sole support.

Eight bolted interfaces are counted, four fixed to middle and four rear-accessible core fasteners at local x=15/40/65/90,y=82. The middle ends are assembled at the factory. Insulated partitions and captive hardware prevent dropped nuts and rail-to-rail shorts. Electrical contact resistance allocations remain unqualified.

## Six adjustments, measurement and removal

Six retained adjustments occupy local x=15,30,45,60,75,90 at y=96: BASE, RZ, SCALE, HF, SINE_SYMMETRY, SINE_LEVEL. Every trim faces the open rear and remains electrically local to its oscillator. Each has a 6 mm diameter axial tool corridor; its sweep is around its own axis, not a hand swing between boards. Bourns' [TC33 manufacturer sheet](https://www.bourns.com/docs/product-datasheets/tc33.pdf), physical page 0, specifies a 1.7 mm cross adjustment tool, 270 ±20° electrical adjustment, 1.2 ±0.3 mm height and static push below 4.9 N. The PDF was visually inspected. Candidate **TC33X-2-203E** supplies the missing 20 kΩ HF identity from the manufacturer's resistance/order table; this proposal does not mutate the canonical BOM. Its tolerance and circuit suitability still require electrical approval. All other package heights use a deliberately conservative **5 mm procurement bound**, not an invented sourced dimension; exact variant height reconciliation is part of one-zone source work.

Four 4 mm axial probe corridors at x=20/40/60/80,y=107 map AGND,+12V,-12V and existing TRI_SCALED. The waveform node is not a new output and the adapter must obey its loading budget. Waveform/FM/PWM/sync/output measurements also use the existing front jacks. No probe is attached to a raw internal timing or summing node. A later bench procedure must specify the insulated tool, probe insulation, clip load and safe access while energized; no energization occurs now.

Factory sequence: install independently supported front boards and selector carriers; install the middle frame and front links with strain relief; install fixed power strap ends; verify source continuity and polarity; install the keyed connector bodies and backing supports; align guides and seat the core with the backing fixture; torque four captive retainers and four core-side power screws to the eventually specified joint procedure; verify insulation, polarity, all conductors and twelve adjacent calibration approaches; fit the cover.

Power-off replacement: remove external supply; verify each rail is discharged with the separately defined meter procedure; remove rear cover; label O1; undo only its four captive power screws and park insulated straps in their fixed restraints; release four captive carrier screws using the rear axial driver; hold both rear handles and withdraw straight back 40 mm, against the supported connector rather than rocking it; lift clear. O2 and the middle remain attached. Reverse the steps with guides engaged before contacts, inspect every power joint, perform continuity/polarity checks, then use a separately authorized recalibration procedure. No soldering or hot-swap assumption appears in this procedure.
