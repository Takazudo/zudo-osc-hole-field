## Findings

- **M1 — reproducibility defect, fix before design checkpoint:** six independent mutations demonstrate that the SVG ignores declared inputs. Changing `card_y_mm` to [40,250], `probe_diameter_mm` to 30, `support_collar_diameter_mm` to 30, `connector_reservation_width_mm` to 40, `rear_extraction_stroke_mm` to 1000, or `rear_external_work_depth_mm` to 1 leaves the drawing byte-identical and every existing interference status unchanged. Drawing constants must derive from inputs. Add checks for reservation containment/overlap and external depth versus extraction stroke, or expose these as explicitly NOT CHECKED rather than apparently checked access reservations. The deterministic self-copy test does not protect this contract.
- **M2 — retained cable constraint:** the proposed 8 mm bend radius must not be used for the retained Alpha Wire 6821 baseline, whose conservative requirement is 9.398 mm. Preserve the existing bound when comparing the same cable, or explicitly make replacement cable selection and requalification a blocker. This reduces the present opposed-connector margin from 13.7 to 10.904 mm before any still-missing package/cable-solid evidence.
- **M3 — package scope:** mechanics prose calls 250 packages O1 packages; the interface scope includes shared circuitry (220 O1-local packages). Correct the scope. Neither number establishes that a 15 x 266 mm card fits. A source-count correction is not a capacity proof.
- **M4 — architecture blocker retained:** the 15 mm rear card allocation, two connector-bank rectangles and two support stations have no exact component placement, pin-count packing, support/rail solids or strength. Existing rear solder terminals have no final supported detachable conversion. A disconnected-core replacement sequence is conditional and cannot currently establish owner-solder-free replacement. Keep this blocker in the decision and next prompt.
- **M5 — service blocker retained:** six rear-facing axes are proposed, not retained PCB trim locations. HF trim identity, actuator access, probe assignment/loading, latch tool stroke, strain relief and enclosure opening remain unresolved. Adjacent 20 mm finger envelopes conflict at 17 mm pitch. Rearward extraction needs the cover removed and verified external access; diagrams do not establish physical serviceability.
