# Issue 35 partition blocker

Authority: **PROPOSAL (planning, owner-delegated)**. Status: **BLOCKED**, pending source-level I/O/island refinement in issue #60. No board partition has been selected. All circuit/PCB materials remain unvalidated drafts.

## Result

The pre-partition confirmation establishes conditional electrical inputs. Applying the real netlist to the proposed physical boundary exposes a further constraint: broad module islands include their source-facing input protection. Moving a whole module island to the rear core makes its raw jack input cross a connector. Moving the whole island to the 306 × 140 mm jack board exceeds the available placement area. A separate optical board does not resolve that area deficit.

`diagnostic35.json` is generated by `scripts/checks/partition35_diagnostic.py` from the pinned KiCad 10.0.6 native master export. It includes every forced component reference, all 98 raw input nets, scoped island closure, sensitive-net closure, footprint hashes and dimensions, and the envelope package pin maps. The abstract `CN301`/`XB301` are excluded using the existing strict marker filter. There are 5,752 native components and 5,750 physical components, 33 signal modules, 438 panel UIDs and 209 sensitive nets.

## Bounded candidate rejection

The existing placer uses courtyard bounding rectangles and excludes through-hole pads on both board faces. The following bounds count each physical courtyard once, on whichever face would receive it, plus only the jack copper pad shapes on the other face. The latter are verified disjoint and are smaller than the placer's rectangular pad exclusions. Both entire board faces are credited as usable; no edge, routing, support, other solder-tail, connector, bulk-capacitor or unresolved protection allowance is charged.

| Candidate | Forced physical parts | Courtyard sum mm² | Opposite jack pads mm² | Minimum occupied mm² | Both board faces mm² | Deficit mm² |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Preview J, including its indicator islands | 5,049 | 92,981.528400 | 1,918.520254 | 94,900.048654 | 85,680 | 9,220.048654 |
| J with all removable indicators on a separate optical board | 4,544 | 86,563.742400 | 1,918.520254 | 88,482.262654 | 85,680 | 2,802.262654 |

These are lower bounds under the current conservative placement rules, not measured solid-volume proofs. The optical candidate still inherits some indicator parts through sensitive-net closure; silently moving them would break those nets. The complete instrument has 122,395.632200 mm² of courtyard rectangles, of which 75,503.332200 mm² belongs to nonpanel parts. Those whole-instrument numbers alone do not prove a particular board assignment.

This rejects the stated J candidates; it does not prove that every larger or shaped board topology is impossible. A larger J board would need dimensioned control-body cutouts, a new support plan and connector access, while retaining every fixed centre. Increasing layer count cannot create component surface area. Moving nonlocal cores behind J requires a source-level electrical boundary first.

## Envelope optical and package constraint

Each `ENV_LEDS:E1` through `ENV_LEDS:E6` contains both three jack-field magnitude LEDs and two control-field stage LEDs. For E1 the fixed centres are:

| LED | x mm | y mm | Current domain |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| SIG magnitude | 224.65 | 29 | J |
| RISE magnitude | 224.65 | 43 | J |
| FALL magnitude | 224.65 | 57 | J |
| RISE stage | 224.40 | 241 | EP |
| FALL stage | 224.40 | 255 | EP |

The same pattern repeats at 17 mm pitch for E2–E6. An optical board containing all 114 LEDs would span at least x=20.65..309.65 and y=29..255 mm between emitter centres alone. Its outline, 324 other hardware passages, populated component faces and support accesses would still need actual geometry. The 5.9 mm stage-LED offset is inside the 6.75 mm half-width of the pot's planar courtyard; that flags a three-dimensional coexistence question, not a sourced solid collision at an unknown installed height. A full optical PCB has not been accepted. Even granting it for the area comparison above leaves J overfull.

This is not repairable by relabelling only the LEDs. Native E1 U4147 (OPA4196IDR) packs all three magnitude drivers and the RISE stage driver; U4148 packs the FALL stage driver and three unused grounded followers. A prospective refinement can pack both stage channels into U4148 and the three magnitude channels into U4147, preserving the package count, subject to regenerated pin, unused-channel, decoupling and sensitive-net audits. This is a proposal, not an implemented netlist edit. The JSON retains the complete original pin maps.

## Required source-level prerequisite

1. Define local, complete jack input islands including source-facing ADG5412F, current limiting/clamps, input buffer, local references as needed and their decouplers. Cross only their buffered outputs and allowed references/conditioned logic. Audit all 98 raw inputs.
2. Refine whole-module core islands at real electrical boundaries. Preserve every timing, hold, summing, feedback/compensation, integrator and slew storage net locally. Keep each H1/H2 slew pot, resistor, five C0G capacitors and pre/post buffers together. Solve the A/B switch control locality explicitly.
3. Repack multi-unit devices by destination island so no single physical package straddles boards. Separate envelope magnitude and stage drivers as described above. Do not add a second physical copy of a shared package to a board projection.
4. Produce machine-readable islands with instance, panel UIDs, physical component refs, local nets and explicit allowed crossing nets. Account for power and returns separately; no raw input, sensitive net or unclassified crossing can pass.
5. Regenerate the native master netlist, ERC warning inventory, component/package counts, rail ledger, source/capacitance budgets, and pre-partition handoff. Verify all 33 modules and all 438 UID x/y centres survive. Re-run area accounting from that netlist before resuming #35.
6. Retain the exact protection work in #59: 82 outputs, 16 precision-sense and 30 octave-receiver paths remain OPEN. Reserve unknown additional circuit area, current and capacitance. No fictitious part, footprint, conductive bridge, protection PASS or capacity saving may stand in for it.
