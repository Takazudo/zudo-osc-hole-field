# Issue 52 partial implementation audit

Status: **BLOCKED**. This note records deterministic conflicts and the boundary of the selected external-source requirement. It does not select an orderable protection device or authorize energization. The historical inlet schematic is still connected to the instrument rails and is not the selected interface.

The external source/inlet contract owns current limiting, including maximum delivered current of 2.0 / 1.9 / 0.5 A with tolerance and bounded transient let-through. A synth-side switch could rely on a source that demonstrably meets that contract. The synth still needs load-side three-rail sensing, inhibited enables, low-drop reverse-current blocking, coordinated all-rail shutdown/discharge, and bounded injection from powered external patches. There is no selected external source or evidence that it meets its current limit. Source minimum delivery is not a maximum fault-current bound.

## Captured legacy circuit conflict

The captured `DW254P-2X8-L0` inlet and one `mSMD110-33V` PTC per rail belong to the old single-source draft. The retained TECHFUSE PDF at `circuit/sources/power/mSMD110-33V.pdf` (SHA-256 `9f11eea6dd5ca89ad88ff7e9916030dc7ed08ad25a57587ff4ab97922623ee34`, physical page 0, Table II row `mSMD110-33V`) gives 1.10 A hold at 25 °C and 0.250 Ω R1max. At required continuous currents 1.6 / 1.5 / 0.3 A, the first two rails exceed the 25 °C hold value; `I × R1max` gives 0.400 / 0.375 / 0.075 V. All exceed the 0.040 V protection-drop allocation. R1max is a resistance bound, so this calculation identifies a design that cannot *guarantee* the drop budget; it is not a measured drop. The historical connector footprint cannot be relabelled as `OSC-EXT-PWR-8-R1`.

## Candidate assessment

TI's [TPS25947xx Rev C datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf) was downloaded and inspected as PDF bytes (SHA-256 `8f96de389903091650d4f462dcfad3210071c3ae7093623a7978f34baf8a65b4`; physical page 8, electrical characteristics). It specifies 45 mΩ maximum on resistance over −40…125 °C for its positive-rail switch. At 1.6 A this alone yields 72 mV, above the 40 mV protection-path allocation. Its active limit row gives 0.425…0.575 A at the nominal 0.5 A setting, and the fast-trip threshold is approximately twice the active limit (physical page 9). Therefore it cannot by itself establish a 0.5 A including-tolerance +5 V delivered-current cap or a 0.5 A transient let-through cap. This is a family-level exclusion for the stated uses, not a complete analysis of an exact orderable variant or a rejection of every possible source-side limiter.

ADI's [LTC4214-1/-2 datasheet](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/421412f.pdf) was accessible in the official web rendering, but a direct PDF download timed out; no retained byte hash is claimed. Physical page 11 describes 50 mV/RS timed breaker, 70 mV/RS analog limit, 200 mV/RS fast comparator and transient overshoot. If the synth-side analog limit itself had to cap −12 V at 1.9 A, RS would need at least 36.84 mΩ. At 1.5 A continuous that sense resistor alone would drop at least 55.26 mV, above 40 mV. A lower-drop RS is possible if a separately proven external-source limiter performs that function; this controller still needs external MOSFET loss, reverse blocking, off-state injection and coordinated shutdown proof. No LTC4214 identity is selected.

TI's [TPS3704 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/gpn/TPS3704) is a candidate for multi-rail window sensing, but a monitor alone cannot interrupt current or discharge rails. Its exact variant, negative-rail level shifting, thresholds, power source during missing +5 V, startup latch and end-to-end response have not been designed. Upstream power-good is not available in the selected interface.

A rail-voltage-only monitor also cannot detect an open inlet AGND return when a connected patch sleeve becomes the sole return: the load-side rails may still appear valid. This is an explicit unresolved return-continuity and injection case. It requires an architecture-level solution that cannot rely on patch sleeves as power conductors. ADI ADG5401F is under separate pilot review as a possible output and feedback isolation element; no exact output path has been selected or qualified here.

## Required architecture repair before resuming

Select and retain exact primary evidence for the source-side limiter and synth-side switch/supervisor/discharge paths. Demonstrate ≤40 mV protection loss at the declared rail currents and temperature; current/energy bounds including fast trips; operation with every rail arrival/failure order; full output and precision-sense/reference injection paths with EXT off and ±12 V external patch; and a capacitor/auxiliary-current inventory from the actual board topology. Resolve the open-return case without treating a patch sleeve as a valid power return. Promote selected orderable identities through owner bundles and pin maps, then replace the historical inlet sheet. Keep `OSC-EXT-PWR-8-R1` requirement-only with no guessed physical footprint until its header, mate and contacts have retained drawings and assembled ratings. The physical source and coupon tests stay `NOT RUN` in #57.
